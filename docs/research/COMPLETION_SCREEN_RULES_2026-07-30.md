Sequence gate: the screen may only run AFTER (i) fall-camp sweep 1 digests are

## Sleeves

- **F2 macro sleeve (the owner's 12–15 small completions):** expression of the
  dispersion factor (market over-trusts preseason ratings; K3 4/4 settlement record).
- **F1 pure-consensus legs:** ≥1.0-win consensus-vs-market gaps at adequate price,
  held with or without a roster opinion (S7 mechanism; five seasons).

## Eligibility (macro sleeve)

1. Regular-season total **≤ 5.5 for OVERS** (primary; K3 4/4). Totals **≥ 8.0 for
   UNDERS** admitted as secondary (K3 3/4) at the low end of sizing.
2. Consensus gap toward the side **≥ 0.75 wins** (cal-lens board vs current line);
   **≥ 1.0 = priority tier**.
3. Price: cal-lens edge **≥ +2.5%** at the actually-available number (devig from
   real two-ways where posted). This is deliberately below the core book's 4% ✓✓
   bar: factor legs are carried by factor evidence and diversification, and are
   sized accordingly small.

## Exclusions (hard)

- **Mid-expectation disruption OVERS** (S9: middle preseason tercile + high
  turnover / QB-battle flag) — the one zone where the continuity headwind is real.
- Teams on the manual-override list price off the override, and any team whose
  candidate thesis contradicts a fresh (≤7-day) news item is out until re-read.
- Market moved ≥ 0.5 wins AWAY from our side since our capture → out (respect
  market action; Liberty doctrine).
- No new leg may take any team past the 1.1–1.2u season cap including held tickets.

## Boosters (tie-breakers, never qualifiers on their own)

- Loud mechanical arm agreement (top-decile |R_pre| ≈ ≥9 pts, same side).
- Mid-band disruption UNDERS (continuity headwind as tailwind); new-HC unders.
- QB-down-transfer re-read downgrades supporting an under (S10 prior).

## Sizing and caps

- Per leg **0.15–0.25u**: 0.25 = priority tier (≥1.0 gap + macro zone + no flags),
  0.15–0.20 otherwise. **Sleeve gross cap 3.0u** — the macro sleeve is treated as
  ONE correlated bet (mild years exist: 2023–24 stretch 1.19–1.20, |d|≥1 rates
  57–60%). Target 10–15 legs; fewer if the screen produces fewer — no forcing.
- F1 pure-consensus legs sized 0.3–0.5u each (mechanism-validated), outside the
  macro cap but inside team caps; ✓ requires the standard 4% bar.
