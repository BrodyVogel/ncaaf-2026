Prereg committed before analysis. Panel: 534 team-seasons (miss = final − preseason SP+,
2022–25), baseline miss ~ level (strong reversion: −0.194·preseason level).

- **F1 prior-year luck (record vs Σ postgame win expectancy): FAIL.** coef −0.14,

  already largely excluded from the rating, so it cannot contaminate it.

  multiple-testing note applies — treat with suspicion.
- **F3 trench concentration (prior-year OL+DL minus skill, z): FAIL.** coef +0.18/SD,

  scale (~0.1–0.2 pts vs miss SD ~6). Note: crude proxy of the roster arm (unweighted
  grade means, no dossier); failure does not test the full arm, but removes an easy
  would-have-been support.

plus noise; no cheap overlay validated. The bottom-up roster arm remains the only
