**The owner's hypothesis is confirmed and correctly scoped: the formula under-retains
last-year tape across the whole distribution — every above-mean unit is pulled down ~6%
of its deviation too hard, every below-mean unit pulled up the same — and the tails are
NOT special.** The dramatic tail residuals in the 07-24 peek (+1.9 top-decile-on-volume,
−5.3 returning bottom-decile QBs) are the linear miss amplified by large |x|, not extra
tail persistence. A spline is the wrong fix; the shipped per-group k table is not the
mechanism either (Study 2's fit stands).

## Texture (S4-D, within-group volume terciles; the pooled version in the study output
confounds position with volume scale and is superseded)

lo-vol β +0.037 · mid-vol β +0.040 · hi-vol β **−0.036**. The under-retention lives in
partial-season tape; full-season tape is calibrated to slightly over-retained. The correct
2027 remedy is refitting w(n)'s functional form (volume-dependent lift at low n), not a
flat β and not smaller k across the board — a flat correction would overcredit exactly
the full-volume rooms that are already fine.

## Pro-forma 2026 board impact (flat β̂ = +0.059, matched-tape arm; upper bound)

926 units: mean |Δdg| 1.15 percentile pts; **55 units flip sweep trigger status**
(|dg|>8 boundary); 185 units move >2 rank pts (max 13.6, Rice QB). Grade-level effect on
flip units bounded by the blend policy at ±2–5 pts → team-rating effects ≲0.2 pts.
Portfolio-relevant flips: Rutgers OL would have blended UP (+6.9→+10.9 — favorable to the
held Rutgers-over thesis, disclosed); BYU DL / Texas Tech DB would have blended down
(ASU schedule opponents; trivial magnitude). Because the flat β is known-miscalibrated at
high volume (S4-D), the true flip count under a proper volume-dependent fix is smaller.
