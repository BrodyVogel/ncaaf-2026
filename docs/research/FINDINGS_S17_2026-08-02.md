  position offsets; slope t **+12.15**, R² 0.13. The exchange rate is

## Addendum (2026-08-02, owner Q): destination strength — tested, failed, with one caveat

Owner asked whether the integration considers destination competition level
(FCS→B10 vs FCS→CUSA), continuously. ANSWER: S17-L3 tested destination SP+
as a CONTINUOUS term — coefficient −0.024/pt (t −0.73), i.e. a full
B10-vs-CUSA spread (~25 SP+ pts) ≈ −0.6 grade pts, not significant →
the shipped curve is grade+position only, per the registered verdict.
EXTENSIVE margin measured today: FCS intake earning ≥6-game roles = 59.6%
at P4 vs 63.9% at G5 destinations (terciles 58/62/64) — real, monotone,
and far smaller than intuition (~5pp, not 30) because destinations
self-select FCS recruits to their own standard; the roster/dossier layer
owns this margin (role assignment), not the curve. CAVEAT: L3 joined only
505/1,035 pairs (PFF↔SP+ team-name aliasing not applied in that leg) —
power-limited null; full-alias retest folded into the 2027 formula
registration. Contrast stands: continuous strength matters WITHIN FBS
(S16, t −3.61) and dies ACROSS the FCS jump on both ends — the bigger the
leap, the more the receiving market's selection does the pricing.
