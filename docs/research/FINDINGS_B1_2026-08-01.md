  empirically confirmed at weekly-batch scale:** discounted innovations carry
  real forward signal; mechanical outlier-discounting throws it away. v1
  ships with NO mechanical robustness — outlier judgment belongs entirely to
  the qualitative layer (the design's human-owns-the-violence split, now
  data-backed). The registered clipped-residue diagnostic is answered by the

## What the headline means

A home-built, public-data, no-in-week-information anchor lands within ~0.25
pts/game of closing lines on forecast accuracy, and its fair numbers sit
~3 pts RMSE from the closes it never saw. That is infrastructure quality —
exactly what the design doc demanded and exactly NOT an edge claim (S14
stands: proximity to the market ≠ beating the market). The anchor's job — an
unbiased fair-number baseline good enough that qualitative deltas are the
decision variable — is met. B1-iv: preseason correlation decays 0.999 →
~0.8 by mid-November; the anchor genuinely travels.
