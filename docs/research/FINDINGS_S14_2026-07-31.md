# Study 14 findings — preseason consensus does NOT beat early spreads: A and B FAIL (2026-07-31)

Prereg: PREREGISTRATION_S14_2026-07-31.md (registered before any ATS outcome was
computed). Script: pipeline/research/s14_early_spreads.py. Panel: 3,568
FBS-vs-FBS games 2021–25 with median closing spread, both teams SP+-rated
(~700–729/yr); openers present for 3,561.

- **S14-A (weeks 0–3, |D|≥3, vs close): FAIL.** Pooled 48.6% (n=395) — below
  break-even, nowhere near the 55% bar. Per-year: **2021 60.5%, then 46.4 /
  45.3 / 44.4 / 46.0** — four consecutive losing seasons since. |D|≥5 report:
  49.6% (n=234). The public "preseason SP+ beats early spreads" lore appears to
  have been a COVID-dislocation artifact (2021's scrambled priors), fully
  arbitraged since — textbook edge decay.
- **S14-B (same slice, vs openers): FAIL.** 48.4% (n=382), same year pattern.
  Not even opener-picking works via consensus disagreement — Sunday posts
  already price preseason consensus.
- **S14-C decay curve (the design payload):** Week 1 the market IS preseason
  consensus — slope +0.89, R² 0.944, RMSE 4.1 pts. By week 4–5 R² falls to
  ~0.71–0.84 and RMSE vs the preseason anchor reaches 6–7 pts (→ ~9–10 by
  November). Market MAE on actual margins beats consensus-alone EVERY week
  (12.3 vs 12.7 in wk 1, gap ~2 pts by wk 9+). Two implications: (i) the
  owner's prior is right — real information moves team strength ~5+ pts within
  the first month, and an updater that can't move that fast is dead weight;
  (ii) closing spreads are a free, timely, high-quality weekly consensus — the
  natural anchor for the in-season chain (and the reason weekly SP+ scraping
  stays unnecessary).
- **S14-D shape:** no threshold redemption on sides — the loud-disagreement
  pattern from totals does NOT rhyme here (8+ cells: 64.3% on n=14 noise early,
  49.2% on n=585 late; nothing monotone). The F1 mechanism is a soft-market
  phenomenon, not a consensus-quality phenomenon.
