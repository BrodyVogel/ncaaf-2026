## Design consequences (feed P3)

1. The early-season gap is real, structural, and mechanically unclosable
   (N6). It is ALSO exactly the window where our differentiated information
   is richest (fresh preseason dossiers, graded two-deeps, camp intel). The
   program's pre-stated edge-location prediction (wins cluster early) now
   has a sharper form: **early season = biggest mechanical handicap AND
   biggest qualitative opportunity — the paper card must be delta-driven
   there, never raw model-vs-line.**
2. P3 card policy: weeks 2–5, raw anchor-vs-opener gaps are demoted to
   context-only (the anchor concedes ~0.5 to the market there); delta cards
   (qualitative reason codes) are the only first-class cards early. From
   ~week 6 on, anchor disagreements earn context weight (gap ≤ ~0.2).
3. v2 constants are the shipping anchor; blind-arm reporting unchanged.
