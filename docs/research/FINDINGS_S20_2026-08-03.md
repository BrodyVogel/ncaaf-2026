# S20 findings — QB passing-yards props (2026-08-03)

Prereg: PREREGISTRATION_S20_2026-08-03.md (committed before computation).
Data: CFBD per-game passing 2020–2025 (data/cfbd/qb_props/, 24,492
player-game rows), PFF passing grades 2020–2025. Panel: 181 QB-seasons
(t 2021–2025), entry ≥250 att in t−1, year-t at a power program, ≥1 att in t.
Metrics power-conference only per owner instruction.

## H1 — class bias of the desk formula: PASS, far beyond the bar

Synthetic line = 12 × prior-year per-game yards (the formula the FD board
tracks at 0.97–1.06 for same-school returners):

- **UNDER 72.9% (132/181), exact binomial p ≈ 3×10⁻¹⁰; 70–76% in all five
  years** (bar was >55% and 4/5 direction).
- Same-school returners 66.4% (n=113); transfers **83.8%** (n=68).
- **Secure subset** (same-school AND ≥10 games t−1 — closest analog to the
  posted 17): **66.7% under (n=96)**.

Decomposition: (i) availability — even secure starters reach 12+ meaningful
games only **45%** of the time (≥11: 60%; panel-wide mean 8.9 games); (ii)
mean reversion — pace declines ~9–11 yd/g on average (entry selects on big
seasons); secure-subset fit `pace_t = 116.0 + 0.512·pace_{t−1}` (resid sd
44). Given a full 12+ games, the formula-line goes under only 40.3% — the
under edge is mostly an availability bet plus mild pace decay.

## H3 — outlier-game trimming (owner): FAIL

Trimmed pace (drop top-2/bottom-2 games) does NOT beat raw pace as a year-t
predictor: MAE 46.4 vs 46.2 (n=171). Monster games and clunkers carry as much
signal as they cost. Raw pace stays the pricer input.

## H5 — PFF grade predictiveness (owner ask): weak positive, below bar

`pace_t ~ pace_{t−1} + PFF_pass_grade_{t−1}` (n=143 joined): grade coeff
+0.96 yd/g per grade point, **t = 1.7**, ΔR² 0.016 (+10 grade pts ≈ +9.5
yd/g). Right direction, not decisive; supporting color per the p<.01
headline bar. Kept OUT of the v0 pricer; logged as a sensitivity.

## Pricer v0 (mechanical) on the posted 17

Simulate G ~ secure-subset hazard (capped 12), pace ~ N(fit, 44) shared
across games + per-game N(0,75): result is **UNDER-lean on 16 of 17**
(P(over) 0.26–0.48; Chambliss 0.483 = pass). Table in
data/cfbd/qb_props/pricer_v0_2026-08-03.json.

**Read this with the audit's own discipline.** A v0 claiming double-digit
edges on an entire marquee board mostly measures what the model omits:
(a) FD selected these 17 BECAUSE they are the most secure jobs — their true
hazard is better than even our secure subset; (b) the shrink fit includes
declining seniors while the posted group skews ascending underclassmen
(Carr/Stockton/Sellers/Iamaleava priced at 1.03–1.06× is plausibly the desk
pricing development we don't model); (c) no schedule/script/supporting-cast
layers yet (H4, H6–H9 queued). The honest claim: **the class skew is real
and large; per-QB v0 probabilities are upper bounds on the under case.**
