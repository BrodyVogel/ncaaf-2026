## CORRECTION (2026-07-27, owner-prompted, append-only)

The fidelity gate's scope was overstated in this document and in the registration's
interpretation matrix. Gate ρ(D)=0.958 compares the auto-roster arm to the curated
arm THROUGH THE SAME scoring construction (UW unit weights → percentile → z-diff).
It therefore bounds roster-MEMBERSHIP noise only; construction noise (ad hoc unit
weights, percentile tail compression, IND-offset artifact) is common to both sides
and invisible to the gate. "A fail is a REAL fail, not roster noise" should read:
"a fail is real FOR THIS CONSTRUCTION." Under errors-in-variables, measured
β=+0.081 is a floor; if the construction-noise share of Var(D) is large, true β
could be a multiple (unmeasured — S8b's cleaner regressor is the estimator).
L1's registered FAIL stands for S8's construction; the underlying arm's movement
power is reclassified UNCERTAIN, not refuted. Verified separately: the live rig's
adjudication caught the ND/IND artifact case-by-case ("IND-fix ... vs P4 pool" in
the log); the artifact polluted only the mechanical standalone score.
