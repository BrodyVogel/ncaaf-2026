- **S16-L1 PASS:** ΔSP (origin − destination strength) t **−3.61**, coef
  −0.077 grade pts per SP+ pt, ΔR² 0.0144, LOYO 4/4 (t 2.80–3.62);

- **S16-L2 PASS (the Georgia-vs-Purdue leg):** same-class movers — where
  the current machinery applies ZERO — ΔSP −0.097 (t −3.39), LOYO 4/4.
  Split: **G5→G5 −0.204 (t −4.17)** vs P4→P4 −0.055 (t −1.62). The
  within-class effect is real and CONCENTRATED IN G5, where program quality
  spans ~25 pts (the James Madison-vs-Kennesaw gulf outruns
  Georgia-vs-Purdue). Same-class moves are 59% of the 2026 portal.
- **Sign, in plain language:** at equal prior grade, players moving UP in
  program quality outgrade the binary expectation; players moving DOWN
  undergrade it (~1 grade pt per 10 SP+ pts). The naive schedule-ease story
  predicts the OPPOSITE sign — so this is context inflation: tape earned in
  a strong program overstates portable individual quality (selection on who
  leaves + supporting-cast co-movement), exactly the owner's hypothesis.
- **S16-L3 (report):** the effect is a line-of-scrimmage/back-seven
  phenomenon — trench −0.121 (t −3.83), back-7 −0.137 (t −3.22), **skill
  DEAD (+0.02, t +0.56)**, QB thin (n=37; S10's down-transfer QB pathology
  remains its own separately-established prior). Interdependent-unit grades
  carry context; individually-attributable production travels.
- **S16-L4 impact (2026 portal, 1,159 movers with tape):** 45 players where
  the continuous form differs from binary-implied by ≥2 grade pts. Held
  teams: 3 — UConn EDGE Mickens (Ohio State →, adj −2.5), Wisconsin CB
  West (Ohio State →, −2.3), UCF LB Henry (UMass →, +2.1). Each is one
  player inside a deep unit → est. team-rating impact <0.1 pts each; below
  adjudication materiality (owner may still order reviews).

QB handled by the S10 prior), same-class moves included (the largest
