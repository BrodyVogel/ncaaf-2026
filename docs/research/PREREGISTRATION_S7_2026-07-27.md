# Pre-registration — Study 7: multi-year totals kill-test (2026-07-27)

Committed BEFORE pulling or analyzing 2021–2024 boards. Data: SBD historical preseason
win totals (DraftKings OPENING vintage — owner-verified vs his 2025 near-closer capture:
123/131 exact, 6 one-win diffs all movement-plausible). Consensus = preseason SP+ (in
repo) run through the probit engine (sigma 13.5, HFA 2.3, FCS −30) over actual played
schedules; actual = regular-season wins (CFBD). 2020 excluded; reclass teams without
SP+ preseason drop out.

DISCLOSED PEEKS: 2025 already analyzed on the owner's capture (consensus MAE 1.91 vs
1.95; 64% on disagreements; 80% in the 1.0–1.75 bucket). 2025 is therefore EXCLUDED
from all pooled bars below and reported separately. Vintage framing: these tests
measure "beat the DK opener." The 2026 board (basis of the live book) is a
March–April-posted, already-bet-into market — a harder benchmark; owner note logged.

## Bars (pooled 2021–2024)

- **K1 anchor non-inferiority:** consensus MAE(actual wins) ≤ market MAE + 0.05.
- **K2 disagreement direction (the bettable claim):** in the |consensus − market| ≥ 1.0
  bucket, the consensus side of the market line wins ≥ 55% (excl. exact pushes).
- **K3 tilt stability:** the dispersion tilt (low totals overperform / high totals
  underperform, by market-line tier) has a consistent sign in ≥ 3 of 4 seasons.

## Registered decision rules

- K1+K2 pass → the totals-book thesis is upgraded from one season to five; book stands.
- K2 < 50% → nightmare-has-legs: freeze adds on consensus-carried positions pending an
  owner review (no auto-unwind).
- 50–55% or mixed → inconclusive; report and hold the book unchanged.
- Report-only: per-year buckets incl. small-|d| bands; 2025 opener→near-closer drift
  (did summer moves go TOWARD consensus?); 2018–19 optionally later (different era).
