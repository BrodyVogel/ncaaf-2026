Sources: Athlon 2026 MAC (p.3 — header-verified); Phil Steele 2026 MAC (pdf p.10
spread, header-verified visually). NO Pick Six (P4-only source). Captured
2026-07-16.
2025 (fact): 5-7, 4-4 MAC — most wins since 2017. W: Duquesne 51-7, CMU 28-22,
Buffalo 24-16, UMass 44-10, BGSU 19-16. L: Wyoming 0-10, Nebraska 0-68, UAB
28-31, Toledo 3-45, Miami-OH 7-20, Ball St 28-42, Kent St 35-42 (OT — the game
that cost win #6). THE STRUCTURAL FACTS (PS): an APR penalty made them
bowl-INELIGIBLE even at 6 wins AND cost one practice day per week (Thursdays)
ALL season; "the lowest football budget & lowest coaching payroll in the FBS";
"the acknowledged lowest NIL in the FBS." The APR restrictions ARE LIFTED for
2026 — bowl-eligible again (Athlon: "Akron's bowl ban for poor APR scores has
been lifted"). No winning season since 2015; 25-59 at home vs FBS since the
stadium opened.
