# Purdue 2026 — magazine notes (blinding v2: team-level projections/rankings EXCLUDED)

Sources: Athlon 2026 (p.15 — header-verified); Pick Six 2026 (book pp. 96-97 = pdf
37-38, header-located); Phil Steele 2026 (pdf p.20 spread, header-verified).
Captured 2026-07-16.
2025 (fact): 2-10, 0-9 B10 — beat Ball St 31-0 and S. Illinois 34-17, then lost ten
straight. 18-game B10 losing streak (last B10 win 2023); 3-21 over two years, no
P4 wins; first back-to-back winless B10 seasons since 1920-21; the two-year Bucket
aggregate is Indiana 122, Purdue 3. Competitive losses: Michigan 16-21, Rutgers
24-27 (blew 24-21 with 4:00 left), Minnesota 20-27 (outgained them by 200+, lost on
a late pick-six). Uncompetitive: Indiana 3-56, Washington 13-49.

## Coaching — NO HC change (Odom yr 2)
- Odom 2-10 in yr 1 (career 46-43; UNLV 2013-22-drought turnaround: 9-5 then 11-3
  with B2B MW title games, first conf COY since '00; Mizzou '16-19 25-25).
- "Odom has set his jaw and swears this will be a bowl team" (Athlon).
- OC Josh Henson yr 2 (Missouri OC '12-15; USC '22-24 but "HC Riley called the
  plays" — PS); Air Raid roots frame (P6).
- NEW DC: Odom FIRED Mike Scherer and brought back KEVIN KANE — Purdue's DC
  '23-24 under Walters ("his D was last in the Big Ten each year" — PS; "Kane's
  2024 unit was statistically worse" than 2025 — P6). Minnesota LB coach in '25.
  "Not exactly a 'splash hire'" (P6). New OL coach Zach Crabtree.
- Roster churn AGAIN: ~55 new players for the 2nd straight year (29 transfers + 4
  JUCO + recruits); '25 was 29 pre-spring transfers, then 28 out / 46 in post-spring.
- Athlon scout: "Some continuity definitely helps Odom in Year 2. Truthfully, I
  wasn't incredibly impressed with what they brought in through the transfer
  portal... I don't think they took great advantage of [the playing-time pitch],
  but I definitely think they'll be better from a continuity standpoint... they
  have a good coaching staff."

## QB
- BROWNE RETURNS as the settled starter — all 12 starts LY (first Purdue QB since
  Blough '16): 2,153 @ 59% with 9 TD / 10 INT (PS); "ranked dead last on the Big
  Ten QB Rating leaderboard"; "his first six games were noticeably better than his
  final six, perhaps due to a shoulder injury he took in the seventh game" (P6).
  "Browne can make all the throws and can tuck and run — but he needs to be
  better" (Athlon). Odom: "from as high of a mountain top as I can be on, I
  believe in Ryan Browne."
- '24 context: led the OT comeback vs Illinois off the bench; spent the '25 spring
  with Bill Belichick and North Carolina before returning (PS).
- GONE: Singleton (202 rush + 439 pass in '25) -> App State. Backups: Chuba
  (Wash St '25 add), Meredith, G. Odom, true FR Berry "battling for the backup
  spot" (PS).

## RB
- GONE: Mockobee — first Purdue RB to lead the team in rushing four straight years;
  #4 all-time (2,987); OFY (injury) vs Rutgers; 521 (4.2) LY. Also M. Thomas (243,
  4.5) expired; J. Thomas -> Minnesota; Merriweather -> Troy.
- IN: a "thunder/lightning duo" (P6) — Gibson (Texas; 529 @ 4.6 L2Y) + Terrell
  (Jackson St; 1,067 @ 6.0 L2Y; "quick and elusive" return ace) + Ijeboi (Minnesota;
  454 @ 4.6 L2Y; "got a big freshman workload"). RETURN: Harris (305 @ 4.4 with
  QBs #4-5 LY).
- PS: "I do like the talent in this room so despite losing a top notch RB this
  unit could top '25's production (top 4 RB 1271 yd)."

## WRTE
- The '25 top targets are ALL gone AGAIN (second straight full restart): Jackson
  (570 @ 9.0) departs, Tuggle (500 @ 14.7) -> South Carolina, Horton (289) departs;
  Branch -> USF. "Drops were a big issue in 2025" (Athlon); "has lacked a true
  game-changing receiver since Charlie Jones in 2022" (P6).
- RETURN: Morrissette — "missed the whole season" injured (P6); the '25 top
  returnee. Burhenn (TE) — "injuries have defined his career, but if he can stay
  healthy, the lithe tight end could be special" (Athlon); 4 games LY.
- IN: Waseem (FAU — 66 catches, 699 yds LY), Owens (Penn — All-Ivy; 25 st, 1,664
  @ 12.1 L3Y), Townsend (UCF/Iowa State rotational — 243 @ 13.5 LY at ISU),
  Hornsby (Syracuse), Fox (UCF TE2), JUCO adds. PS lists Magwood (UCF '23-24;
  2 gm LY) at WR2 — sole-source print (not in Athlon/P6 charts).
- Athlon: "Is there an alpha wideout in the house? Hopes are high for four
  transfers and a juco."
