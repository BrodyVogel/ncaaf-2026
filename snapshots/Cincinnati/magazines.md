  loss to Arizona, Rusnak's 3 missed FGs in the 12-pt BYU loss, TCU rout, Navy bowl domination.

  cycle; "Cincinnati sued him, and was embroiled in another off-field controversy and lost his

  "Moneyball" - non-AQ/FCS transfers into All-B12 stars ("excellent track record recently").
- QB: French (Georgia Southern) emerged as leader - 5,760/37 + 554 rush over two seasons; 6th in
  SBC QB Rating 2025, 9th in 2024 ("will need to improve on his passing efficiency"); drawn by
  heavy RPO/play-action; Satterfield RETAINS play-calling w/ internal OC promotion. + O'Brien (Penn).
- RB: Walker (All-B12) + Pryor (-> Florida) = 1,231 combined gone. Zion Johnson NAMED starter
  (bowl start; "most explosive"); battle: All-SBC Perry (Louisiana), Payne (ND), Tabb (Stanford).
- WR: top three gone - Allen (2nd-tm All-B12, 13 TD = 3rd natl), Goodie (-> Missouri), Caldwell

  2024), Wolford (Kent St - 27 y/c All-MAC, may get carries), Fenner (S.Dakota - 15 FCS TD),
  Henry (C.Ark - 10 TD), Schiele (D2 - 4* transfer grade). I.Johnson moves up. "Transfers from
  the FCS or DII may seem to be roulette chips, but Cincinnati has an excellent track record."
- TE: Royer (All-B12) drafted 5th round; Grover next (250 true-FR snaps; a yard shy of a TD vs KU).
- OL: December JOINT ANNOUNCEMENT - trio stays: LT Cotton, LG Tengesdahl, G/C Tyo. Lose Gerhardt.
  2025: #2 natl Sack Rate, #3 P6 OL Run Push, #5 yds/carry, Joe Moore semifinalist - "one of the
  strongest Big 12 lines yet again." Latulippe + Lozowicki projected for the two open spots.
- DEF: 2025 = #54 of 68 P4 opp-adjusted (offense was #11); pass D bottom five; #123 Negative Play
  rate. NEW DC Nate Woody (Satterfield's App State DC; 2024 Army D = #1 natl total defense).
  Shift 3-3-5 -> 3-4 "but they'll always bring a fourth in pass rush" (P6; Athlon frames it as a
  pressure-based 3-3-5 - scheme label attributed both ways).
- DL: All-B12 pair Corleone + Hunt graduated; Coleman -> Miami OH. Parker = spring top rusher;
  T.Johnson (NC Central): 12 sacks/18 TFL FCS.
- LB: Golday drafted 2nd round; Thompson back (3rd-yr starter, all-league push); S.Coleman +
  Simms move up; Maciorowski (NIU, 97 tkl).
- DB: lone returning starter = Peek (All-B12, Thorpe semifinalist, nickel). "Another round of
  defensive backs from the portal and as many as four could start": Cannon (BG, All-MAC, spring
  standout), Goodwill (S.Ala).
