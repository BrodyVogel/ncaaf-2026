# James Madison — magazine findings (Athlon + Phil Steele, 2026 editions)

RULES: extract unit FACTS only. G5 = TWO-SOURCE (Athlon + Phil Steele); no Pick Six for the Sun Belt.

## THE SHAPE (2-source consensus)
NEW HC Billy Napier (from Florida; band x1.13). The best Sun Belt team in '25 (12-2, 8-0 SBC) but a
MASSIVE roster exodus (a huge outflow to UCLA + others) and a 4-way QB reset. The reload leans heavily
on lower levels (D2/D3/FCS). A high program floor (Napier's culture) + the market's #1-SBC rating vs a
thin, heavily-rebuilt roster. Wide band (coach change).

- Header: NEW HC Billy Napier. NF 82, Sun Belt East 1. 12-2 (8-0 SBC).
- OFF: a 4-way QB competition after a string of SBC Players of the Year - transfers Arrington Maiden

  starting experience; JC Evans ('24 Boca Raton Bowl MVP, 21 career passes) enters year 3. WR Braeden

- DEF: LB Gannon Weathersby back in a bigger role (57 tkl); S KJ Flowe (pick-6 vs Coastal) rising. DE
  Amar Thomas "back after a great season" + WKU transfer Okeke; "JMU looked largely to the LOWER levels
  to find defensive linemen" (D2 transfers Kevin Roberts, Jakolbi Wilson, Mason Purham). Toledo CB DJ
  Kelly "might step right into the starting role."
- ST: K Morgan Suarez + P Patrick Rea both back (transferred in last year, immediately started; Rea 41.0
  avg, 16 inside the 20, long 61).
- Depth chart is heavily transfer-laden (nearly every OL/DB starter marked *). Returning bold: Greenberg
  (C), Wisloski, Weathersby, Flowe.
- Scout: "a nucleus who only know a championship culture... the floor is higher than other programs in
  transition. Napier is a really good coach... he'll keep that thing rolling."
