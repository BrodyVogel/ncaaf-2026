## Program frame
- 2025: 0-12 (0-8 MAC) — first winless full season in program history (excl. the
  0-4 2020). Outscored 38-11 on average; −165 ypg; LAST in FBS in scoring (11.1
  ppg) AND total offense (247.5 ypg); "the only FBS school to finish under 1,000
  rush and under 2,000 pass yards" (PS). 30 different players missed time with
  injuries. 16-game losing streak entering 2026 (nation's longest, −26.8 avg
  margin). Since the FBS move: 26-122; no winning season in 15 years; last road
  FBS win 2023 (Army), last home FBS win 2021 (UConn).
- 2026 = Joe Harasymiak yr 2 (hired Dec 4, 2024 — Maine HC '16-18, 20-15, FCS
  COY '18; Minnesota '19-21; Rutgers DC '22-24, Broyles nominee ×2). Just 11
  scholarship players remain from before his arrival; signed 24 true FR in
  December. NEW OC Max Warner (co-OC at Bowling Green '22-24 — coached Harold
  Fannin Jr. to All-America; predecessor left for the NFL); DC Jared Keyte yr 2;
  STC Castellitto yr 2. HC continuity → no coach-change multiplier.
- Athlon final analysis: "Several key injuries decimated a thin roster... If the
  Minutemen can stay healthier and Watson lives up to expectations at QB, UMass
  will be capable of competing most weeks in the MAC. The streak should end in
  September" (Sacred Heart, Stonehill, Sacramento St on the slate).
- Opposing MAC assistant (Athlon): "Now that they're in the MAC, I actually
  think it should be a really good job... they're the only game in town in the
  whole Northeast at that level... but their roster was just so bad... I don't
  know if they can win enough games right now."
