RULES (blinding v2): unit/player-level only, attributed. 2026 team-level projections EXCLUDED.

## Pick Six 2026 (B12 pp. 120-121)
- 2025 arc (historical): 4-8 with six double-digit losses in RichRod's return; the September
  injury cascade took preseason All-B12 RB White, backup Edwards, QB Marchiol, top WR Bray,
  then the OL. Highlights: the OT Backyard Brawl comeback over Pitt (Marchiol off the bench,
  Edwards the winning score); Fox's wins over #22 Houston (45-35) and Colorado (29-22).
  Closed 49-0 to Texas Tech; the 45-13 UCF loss "even worse." RichRod after Kansas:
  "nothing was good, coaching? not good, playing? not good."
- 2026 = A SECOND TRANSITION: senior-heavy '25 roster graduated; 83-85 new players (49
  recruits + 34 transfers); "as many as 19 new starters." NOT the usual yr-2 bonus (P6).
- QB: five '25 QBs cycled; true FR Scotty Fox emerged - 301 pass vs TCU (Dykes compliments),
  led both B12 wins, 27 carries/123/2 TD in them. All other QBs transferred out. Fox
  battles Michael Hawkins (Oklahoma) - "dual-threat skillset needed for this scheme";
  started as a true FR at OU in '24 but "struggled in a tough spot."
- RB: top four gone. Cam Cook (Jacksonville State) = the NATION'S RUSHING CHAMP: 1,659 +
  16 TD at 5.6 - "will be the workhorse." 4* FR Amari Latimer = RB2.
- WR: top three gone (Vaughn, Weimer, Gallagher) BUT original top target JADEN BRAY
  RETURNS from the early season-ending injury - "their clear #1 receiver to build around."
  Adds: Francis (LSU; Top200 '25 recruit), Strachan (USC; Boise success, buried behind
  stars), Epps (Troy; slot starter).
- TE: top two graduated; Ward assumes the job; 6'7 Ball (MissSt) + Sapp (Clemson) = P4
  backups. RichRod: more 12 personnel this year.
- OL: '25 bottom-15 nationally in P6 Run Push (#121) AND Pass Pro (#120); 9% sack rate.
  Return: C Livingston + HM All-B12 Krahe. HIRED BACK RICK TRICKETT (53rd season as an OL
  coach; led the 2001-06 WVU + recent JSU top-5 rushing lines). Adds: Vass (KSU; started
  the final 7 there), Grayson (JSU; 1st-team CUSA), Top100 FR Kevin Brown ("instant
  starter at right tackle"); retained top backups Agbo + Aisosa.
- DEF: ELEVEN new starters. '25: 31 ppg (#114); pass D bottom-ten most categories, #66 of
  68 P4 opponent-adjusted.
- DL: All-B12 Vesterinen graduated; "lost their top six in the trenches." Gabriel (300
  snaps) = projected NT; Henson (JUCO All-American), Durham-Campbell (Coastal Carolina,
  all-league) + Holloman (WKU, all-league) at end, Haastrup (Oregon; Top250 '25 recruit).
  P6: "an upgraded room from 2025 despite the loss of Vesterinen."
- LB: both starters + two risers gone. Cutter rises; Stolsky (FAU starter) battles Hood
  (Illinois backup).
- DB: EVERY safety departed + top three corners + NB Perry (the team tackle leader).
  New: S Wilson (Memphis) + Powdrell (UNLV); backup corners Taylor + Robinson move up;
  NB = Wisconsin starter Latimer; Top100 FR Matt Sieg the wildcard.
- Outlook facts: "basically a second transition season"; roster upgraded w/ the rushing
  champ, improved lines, top-25 HS class.

## Phil Steele 2026 (B12 PDF p12; VISUAL page-read)
- QB: '25: Marchiol injured, portal after 4 starts; Henderson OFY wk 5; Wilkins banged up;
  Fox = first true-FR starter since 1952 - lost his first two, then the Houston/Colorado
  wins: 1,276 (59%, 7-6) + 201 rush, #4 rusher. Adds Hawkins (PS#19; 4 OU starts) - "the
  mobile QB Rich Rod covets." RichRod (attributed): "could be the deepest QB room he has
  ever had." "Much more stable and productive with 2 capable players."
- RB: '25: White OFY week 2 (led '23/'24); Edwards 141 vs Pitt then OFY; Knighton ruled
  ineligible in August; Hubbard led with just 335 (3.5). Adds: 2nd-team AA Cam Cook
  (1,659 @ 5.6 JSU), JC Boswell (1,109 @ 9.5), true FR Latimer (PS#22) + Cheeks (PS#41),
  FB Kayden Luke (Arizona). "Will blow away last year's paltry production."
- WR: '25 returned just one of the top five (Gallagher: 7 st, 288); top offensive weapon
  Vaughn led a unit with just 1,116 total yards. Four full-time-starter transfers added
  (2 from JSU-adjacent moves); "vastly improved from a year ago" (RichRod framing).
- OL: '25: only Livingston back (1 st at C); completely rebuilt; dropped to 161 (3.7) w/
  31 sacks "despite QBs being the #3, 4 and 5 rushers." 2026: lose 5 OL (3 starters);
  return two starters; add 4 full-time starters (two Jax St-linked, where the OL coach is
  from); career starts up to 120. + Trickett.
- DL: lose 7 DL (48 starts, 139 tkl) - all four starters. Add 3rd-team SBC DE
  [Durham-Campbell, 5 sk], 3rd-team CUSA DE [Holloman], BGreen starter [Afogho], and
  PS#3/#31/#33/#69 JUCOs. "Should at least equal last year's production… need the
  interior guys to take the next step. Size up front could be a concern."
- LB: '25: Carrico led team in sacks (4.5), #3 tackler; C.Wilson (13 st, 98 tkl CSU) +
  1st-tm CUSA S Fred Perry (110 tkl) added then. 2026: all 3 starters depart (#1/#3/#4
  tacklers). Add 7 transfers, "6 with 39+ tkl in a season… on par with 2025. May not have
  a star yet, but it'll be a lot deeper."
- DB: PDR #123 ('25: 64.9%, 28-11) after #125 ('24) - two straight all-new secondaries.
  2026: lose all 4 starters AGAIN; add 7 transfers incl. Kent St/Memphis/SFA starters +
  PS#100 LSU [Bradford]. "Same shape as last year but could move up some… Rich Rod is
  excited that his secondary has more length."
- ST: '25 #116 STR; lose K (10-12, L50), P (42.4, 38.9 net), KR (21.9), PR (9.6). Add
  Colo St P Hansen (44.1 net!) - "could make a decent rise with better team depth."
- Coaches: RichRod 2001-07 here: 60-26, six straight bowls; calls his own plays. DC Zac
  Alley yr 2 (his DC at Jax St '22-'23). 2025 context: ~50 transfers, many post-spring;
  -125 ypg in B12 play; injuries "really hurt."
