## THE SHAPE (2-source consensus: Athlon + CFN)
Sonny Cumbie Year 5 (19-31 at school; NO HC change). An 8-5 CUSA team in its Sun Belt DEBUT (CUSA
agreed in April to let La Tech leave for the SBC in 2026). MASSIVE roster turnover: a 24-out portal
exodus PLUS the graduation/NFL loss of the two best players. Keeps BOTH top QBs (Baker + Kukuk), TE
Finley, LT Christman + LG Cole, and LB Kolbe Fields (DPOY billing) + Jadon Mayfield. Loses its top 2
graders (S Jakari Foster to the NFL, LB Sifa Leota to graduation), all 3 top RBs, the top 4 WRs, the
entire secondary starting five, and both '25 placekickers. A heavy transfer/HS rebuild.

## Athlon (SBC p12) — depth chart + prose
- Header: Sonny Cumbie, 19-31 (4 yrs). NF 104, Sun Belt West 3. 8-5, 5-3 CUSA (incidentally visible - NOT used).
- OFF: "Three different QBs saw significant reps in 2025, the majority going to soph Blake Baker before
  a torn ACL ended his season in November. JUCO transfer Trey Kukuk presided over a 3-game closing win
  streak. Baker went through non-contact drills in the spring... there will be a role for Kukuk, who adds
  a dynamic element as a runner." Both top QBs back (Baker the better passer; Kukuk the runner).
- RB: "Last year's top three running backs all moved on, leaving the position without a player who has
  played a snap at the NCAA level." Depth chart RB1 = Jaden Miller* (transfer), RB2 = Dylone Brooks (Fr).
- WR/TE: "The top four wideouts are gone, too, although senior TE Eli Finley is a proven target
  underneath. The revamped receiving corps includes 2025 injury casualty Jay Wilkerson; FCS Eastern
  Kentucky transfer Marcus Calwise Jr.; and Oklahoma State transfer Talyn Shettron, a once-touted recruit."
- OL: "Left tackle Hayden Christman has logged 24 consecutive starts for an offensive line that allowed
  the FEWEST SACKS in Conference USA last season." (Depth chart returning-bold: Christman LT + Cole LG.)
- DEF: "The Bulldogs feasted on takeaways in 2025, tying for 2nd nationally with 22 INTs. The bad news:
  the majority of those picks left with safeties JAKARI FOSTER (NFL), Jacob Fields (TCU) and Michael
  Richard (Michigan State). The entire starting five in the secondary will be [replaced], with last
  year's backups vying for reps with an influx of transfers."
- LB: "Few concerns at linebacker, where sixth-year vet Kolbe Fields headlines a deep unit. Fields... was
  on his way to living up to his billing as the preseason CUSA Defensive Player of the Year last fall
  before a leg injury cut his season short. His absence left plenty of reps for freshmen Jadon Mayfield,
  Alonzo Jackson Jr. and Zheric Hill." (NOTE: Sifa Leota, the 84.3/638-snap '25 LB, is NOT on the depth
  chart and NOT named - a yr4 graduation.)
- ST: "Placekickers Drew Henderson and John Hoyet Chance finished just 10-of-18 on FG in 2025. Their
  struggles opened the door late for true freshman Kaegan Kent... The new punter, freshman Reuben
  Edwards, arrived in the spring from Australia. There is no experience in the return game."
- Scout (opposing CUSA assistant): top two QBs back, Baker the better passer, Kukuk a Taysom-Hill-type;
  "their leading receiver [TE Eli Finley], he's a really good player - we were hoping he'd get in the
  portal, but he didn't." Likes S Kam Franklin + the Arkansas transfer CB Ahkhari Johnson. "Only bringing
  in 12 portal guys" (a develop-from-HS program under Cumbie).

## College Football News (2026 preview) — independent 2nd source

- Confirms departures: S Jakari Foster GONE (his "eight-INT season" departs; declared for the 2026 NFL
  Draft per the Ruston Daily Leader); LB Mekhi Mason -> Kansas State (14.5 sacks/68 stops); CB Michael
  Richard -> Michigan State; DL Christian Davis -> SMU.

- RB "relatively thin" with portal additions Kenny Thomas + Jaden Miller (confirms the backfield reset).
