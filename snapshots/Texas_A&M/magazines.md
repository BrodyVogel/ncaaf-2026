## THE SHAPE (3-source consensus)
Elko Year 3 (no HC change; band x1.00). An 11-2 team (best start since 1992, first Playoff bid,
0-2 finish) facing a "reload not rebuild": a DOUBLE coordinator switch, seven new starters on the
lines of scrimmage, and a SCHOOL-RECORD 10 NFL draft picks out - offset by a Top-10 transfer haul.
QB Marcel Reed returns as the centerpiece; the secondary is the strength; the OL and LB are near
rebuilds; the DL reloads after losing the SEC DPOY.

  do they win the big games... Elko is a real-deal ball coach, they play complete team football."

## Pick Six (p143-144, Brett Ciancia) — facts only (rankings/Game-Grader NOT recorded)
- "A double coordinator switch, seven new starters on the lines of scrimmage, and must replace a
  school-record 10 NFL draft picks." Top-10 transfer haul makes it a "reload."
- Coaching: OC Collin Klein left for the Kansas State HC job -> promoted WR coach Holmon Wiggins
  (ex-Alabama, coached DeVonta Smith + 5 first-rounders; first-time playcaller).
- QB: Reed top-5 SEC in yds/att, TDs, QB Rating + 493 rush/6 TD; "a legit Heisman contender into
  November"; the memory is the redzone INTs in the two losses. "One of the most proven leaders returning."
- WR: Concepcion + Craver "the only P4 pair of 900+ yard receivers"; Concepcion "picked in the first
  round this April," but Craver returns as an All-SEC headliner. Bethel-Roman (20+ ypc final 7) +
  Alabama's Isaiah Horton (#7 transfer WR, 8 TD, redzone solution) + Bussey + frosh Aaron Gregory.
- TE: "top three tight ends are gone" (Boerkircher + Niblack graduated, Ohrstrom -> SMU); captain
  Micah Riley + All-AAC Houston Thomas (UTSA).
- RB: Le'Veon Moss (original starter) injured midseason; Rueben Owens took over (639 yds, 5.4). "Owens
  is back"; four-star Jamarion Morrow is #2.
- OL: "complete rebuild... four starters gone. All four were drafted and three - Zuhn, Bisontis,
  Reed-Adams - were All-SEC." Mark Nabou the lone returning starter (C); 4 SEC-transfer starters
  (54 combined starts): Formby (Alabama), Tyree Adams (LSU), Echols (LSU, 8 starts as RS-fr), Baugh (SC).
- DL: "Howell, and tackles Albert Regis and Tyler Onyedim were all drafted." Anto Saka (Northwestern,
  All-B10, 1st-round buzz) + returning end TJ Searcy + 5-star DJ Hicks (starting) + CJ Mims (UNC) +
  Davis-Swain (Colorado).
- LB: "Taurean York and Scooby Williams both went pro"; Daymion Sanford returns (run stopper); All-AAC
  Ray Coney (Tulsa, league-best 128 tackles, 3-yr starter) added; Top100 Noah Mikhail next up.
- DB: "packed with returning talent... All three safeties are back - Dalton Brooks, Marcus Ratcliffe,
  Bryce Anderson - + Byard (Colorado). Will Lee went pro, but starting corner Dezz Ricks returns.
  Rickey Gibson (Tennessee) was an All-SEC candidate before a season-ending injury."
- OUTLOOK: "a reload instead of the expected rebuild... a Playoff contender, projected a spot or two
  outside the bracket" (schedule toughens: 5 of the top 8).
