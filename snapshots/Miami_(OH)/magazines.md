# Miami (OH) — magazine notes (blinding v2: team-level ratings/projections/market numbers excluded)

Sources: Athlon MAC preview p.10 (header-verified "MIAMI (OHIO) REDHAWKS");
Phil Steele MAC pdf p.3 (300dpi spread, banner visually verified). No Pick Six.

## Program frame
- 2025: 7-7 (6-2 MAC) — THIRD STRAIGHT MAC title game (first program to do it
  since NIU 2010-15), lost it 13-23 to WMU (in all 3 years the regular-season
  loser won the title game); lost the Arizona Bowl 3-18 to Fresno. Won 7 MAC
  games in a streak after a 1-4 start. 2nd-toughest MAC schedule (PS).
- 2026 = Chuck Martin yr 13 (72-74 at Miami; career 146-81; Grand Valley 2×
  national titles; ex-ND OC). 55-21 run since his 4-14 start; 17-2 after their
  last 19 MAC losses; 53-14 in MAC play over 7 yrs when his starting QB plays
  the whole game (PS). OC Gus Ragland; DC Bill Brechin (long-tenured internal
  staff). Deep continuity → no coach-change multiplier.
- Program bona fides: MAC all-time leader in wins (740), MAC titles (17), bowl
  wins (9). PS: "HC Martin is running a machine."
- Athlon final analysis: "a good chance for a fourth [title game] if the
  higher-end transfers turn out to be as good as advertised."
- Opposing MAC assistant (Athlon): "Tough, gritty. They don't turn the football
  over... one of the best special teams coordinator jobs in the country...
  always one of the top-25 most efficient ST units... pretty simple
  schematically on defense, but it doesn't matter because they have all the
  answers. They just don't do anything stupid."
