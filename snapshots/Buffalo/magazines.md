# Buffalo — magazine notes (blinding v2: team-level ratings/projections/market numbers excluded)

Sources: Athlon MAC preview p.6 (header-verified "BUFFALO BULLS"); Phil Steele MAC
pdf p.8 (300dpi spread, banner visually verified). No Pick Six (P4-only publication).

## Program frame
- 2025: 5-7 (4-4 MAC). Opened 5-4 (led Troy 17-0 in 4Q but lost it after QB
  Roberson's injury), then lost the last 3 and missed a bowl. +25 ypg in MAC play
  but −7 turnover margin. 2024 was 9-4 with a bowl win (Lembo yr 1 magic: first
  November wins since 2000, first top-25 win since 2008).
- 2026 = Pete Lembo yr 3 (14-11 at UB; career 126-76 over 17 yrs: Lehigh, Elon,
  Ball St 33-29, ex-Memphis STC). PS: just SIX returning starters (2 off + 4 def)
  — one of the thinnest returning cores in FBS — and "one of the lowest NIL pools
  in the country."
- Staff overhaul below the HC: DC Joe Bowen LEFT FOR PITT (opposing coach: "he was
  a fantastic defensive coordinator"); new DC Brian Dougherty (ex-UB NB/S coach,
  D2 Indianapolis DC). New OC Tony Tokarz (9 yrs on Mike Norvell staffs, FSU TE/QB
  coach; with Lembo at Memphis '19). New STC Garrett McLaughlin (UNH — FCS-best 5
  blocked punts in '23). HC continuity → no coach-change multiplier (HC-only rule).
- Athlon final analysis: "Lembo is starting over in a lot of ways, and a lot of
  the rebuild will depend on the quarterback... The lines need to be better than a
  year ago, and it is not clear that the Bulls upgraded there. Reaching a second
  bowl in Lembo's first three years will be a challenge."
- Opposing MAC assistant (Athlon): "They lost their DC to Pitt... and their Mike
  linebacker, Red Murdock. He made the show go... They're a tough out. Lembo
  coaches them well. They're a pain in the a– on special teams... I wouldn't count
  them out of competing for a MAC title."

## Offense (Athlon: TWO returning starters — Gathings, Heininger)
- QB: both 2025 QBs depart (Roberson 2,520 @ 58%, 17-12 after the Troy injury;
  Gray). Athlon starts rFr JASON WRIGHT (used as a running QB in '25: 43 att @
  2.9, ZERO pass attempts; "promising... high ceiling as a two-way threat"); PS's
  lineup starts D2 transfer ELIJAH HOLMES (Wingate: 3,040 @ 59%, 24-6, 290 rush
  in '25) — split-print battle, second straight build. True FR Qur'an McNeil is
  PS QB #55. New OC Tokarz's system is the learning curve. PS: "the dual threat
  QB's that HC Pete Lembo covets."
- RB: top guy Al-Jay Henderson (755 @ 4.1 LY; 1,078 in '24) and Sperling (234 @
  6.0, → UTEP) gone. Terrance Shelton Jr. (244 @ 4.3) is the guy — Lembo calls
  him "a younger version of Henderson." Burch = gadget back (explosive plays,
  98 rec yds L2Y). True FR Chase Enlow was in for spring. PS: "the unit could
  top last year's output."
- WR/TE: lost BOTH All-MAC wideouts to the P4 portal — McMillan (975 @ 15.8, UB
  record six straight 100-yd games → Kansas) and Snow (815 @ 13.1, top PR → NC
  State). Gathings is the top returner (410 @ 11.4, 10 st; Akron '23 2nd-Tm MAC
  → UConn '24 → UB '25) and "will be Wright's top target." Morrow (ex-Louisville
  RS) is a 6-4 red-zone threat; Early/Simmons "turned heads during the spring."
  Adds all-conf D2 WR Jaylin Andrews (E. Stroudsburg 623 @ 15.6) + FCS TE Trevor
  Wilson (Campbell, 11 st, 193) + D2 TE Clint Walker (Davenport). Both volume
  TEs (Velez, King) expired.
- OL: FOUR starters gone (PS); Athlon: "only one starter returns in guard Alex
  Heininger" (9 st then out-for-year injury LY) — "the area with the most
  questions on offense." Davis (1 start at C) to RT; Arena (ex-Virginia Tech,
  9 gm '24) at LT; imports Joe Crocker (Baylor, 18 gm L2Y, PS OL #67),
  Francavilla (Miami FL), Natoli (Bucknell, 20 FCS starts). PS forecast:
  "questions all over the offense but since last year's group underachieved,
  I could see this year's unit still topping last year's numbers."

## Defense (4 returning starters — Evans, Blackwell, Bradley, SaVeon Brown)
- DL: total rebuild — NT Cornell Evans is the lone returning starter (7 st).
  Departed: Stewart (12 st; 9.5 sk in '24), White (12 st, #6 tklr), Wolo (10 st),
  Poyser (7 st, 5.5 sk → Wisconsin), Morgan (3.5 sk), Ballard (→ BC). Will Clark
  "had a nice spring" at DE. Transfer edges Logan Goodwin (D2 IUP: 64 tkl, 12.5
  sk, 8.5 tfl L2Y) and Sedrick Vessah (D2 PennWest Cal: 52 tkl, 11.5 sk, 4.5 tfl)
  "were all-league at their previous stops" + D2 Nickel, FAMU Smith, Wofford
  Oliver.
- LB: the program's signature unit is GONE — Red Murdock (#1 tklr, 142, 5 sk;
  first UB consensus All-American since the room also produced FBS tackle king
  Dolac in '24; broke Khalil Mack's UB career FF record with 17) departs to the
  NFL (7th-rd DEN), Crawford (12 st, #2 tklr → Michigan State) and Gonser (5 st,
  #4 tklr, ex-Harvard) also out. Rebuild is ALL transfers: Cornell's Joey
  Cheshire (10 st, 73 tkl — "impressed coaches early"), Virginia's Stevie Bracey
  (PS LB #125, med-RS '24), D2 Missouri Southern's Zach Bergmann (100 tkl, 2
  int) + rFr George Hill.
- DB: the strength. CBs Kobi Blackwell (7 st, 8 pd as rFr) and Keontez Bradley
  (8 st, ex-Arizona St) are "both all-MAC caliber" (PS); NB SaVeon Brown (8 st,
  #7 tklr, 6.5 tfl) is "expected to be the biggest playmaker on the backline";
  S Miles Greer (1st-Tm PSAC at D2 Shepherd '24; 74 tkl, #3 tackler LY despite
  not starting). UB was #23 nationally in pass D (186.4 ypg) and PS PDR #51.
  Loses S Solomon Brown (12 st, #5 tklr), McNair (→ Virginia), McCartherens
  (→ Marshall). Adds Citadel CB Smothers. Athlon starts So. Ronnell Davis at
  the other S.
- 2025 as-played footnote: this defense's LB room (Murdock) is literally the
  league p50 LB reference tape — and none of it returns.

  (→ Purdue) and PR Snow ALL depart.
- 2026: K = rFr Oliver Hautanen (PS K #67, zero college action) vs D3 SUNY
