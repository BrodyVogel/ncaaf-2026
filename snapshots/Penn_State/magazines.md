# Penn State 2026 — magazine notes (blinding v2: team-level projections/rankings EXCLUDED)

Sources: Athlon 2026 (p.14 — header-verified); Pick Six 2026 (book pp. 74-75 = pdf
15-16, header-located); Phil Steele 2026 (p.8, text image). Captured 2026-07-16.
2025 (fact): 7-6, 3-6 B10 — a 3-0 start, then the 2OT White Out loss to Oregon
(24-30), losses to winless UCLA (37-42) and Northwestern (21-22) — "the first team
ever to lose consecutive games as 20+ point favorites" (Fallica via P6) — Iowa 24-25,
OSU 14-38, Indiana 24-27. Franklin FIRED in October (104-45 at PSU). Interim Terry
Smith went 4-0 incl. the Pinstripe (40-36 over Clemson).

## Coaching — FULL CHANGE
- MATT CAMPBELL hired after a 50+ day search (Iowa State's winningest coach; first
  ISU 11-win season in '24; 3x B12 COY; Toledo before). Franklin -> Virginia Tech
  (nine-plus PSU players followed him there).
- Staff: OC Taylor Mouser (with Campbell since 2015; ISU OC last 2 yrs) — the OFFENSE
  TRANSPLANTS WHOLE. DC D'Anton Lynn (PSU alum CB; UCLA '23 / USC '24-25; 4-3 base)
  replaces the failed Knowles year (#1 -> #31 adjusted, "declined in all 17 of my
  stat categories" — P6). Interim Terry Smith RETAINED on staff. STC Lustig yr 3
  RETAINED.
- The transfusion: 48 out / 38 in; "24 of the team's 40 transfers coming from Ames"
  (Athlon); Campbell brought "13 starters off his 2025 Iowa State team" (P6).
- Athlon scout: "I don't necessarily know that they're gonna be immediately good in
  Year 1... the three big guys who followed Knowles to Tennessee went for way more
  money than I would've paid... they kept a couple of really good players that
  could've left, like Tony Rojas... They took pretty much that whole offensive staff
  and brought a lot of defensive players from Iowa State. That brings continuity."

## QB
- GONE: Allar (OFY vs NU; declared — rd-3 PIT); Grunkemeyer (finished the year 4-0 +
  the bowl; 1,341 @ 69.1%, 8-4) followed Franklin to VT.
- IN: ROCCO BECHT (Iowa State) — "the most experienced FBS quarterback in college
  football, with 39 career starts, 26 wins and more than 2,500 snaps" (Athlon); 2nd
  in P4 career yardage, 3rd in passing TDs (P6); 2,584 @ 60.5%, 16-9 in a down '25;
  "his deep ball is excellent, but he'll need to hit the shorter lay-ups with more
  consistency" (P6); McSorley comps; rehabbed offseason surgery on the NON-throwing
  shoulder (limited spring; threw with a VR headset — Athlon).
- The continuity is total: his OC, his top two WRs, his top two TEs, his RB all came
  with him. Depth: Manske (ISU rFr), FR Falzone (IFS '25), Barry (D3 spring story).

## RB
- GONE: the record duo — Allen (school rushing record 4,180; All-American; rd-6 WAS)
  + Singleton (school TD record 45; drafted '25 class... both graduated per P6).
- IN: Hansen (ISU 2-yr starter; 950-952 @ 5.1; 2nd-team All-B12; "between-the-tackles
  workhorse") + Peoples (Ohio State; Top100; "home-run explosiveness" — P6).
- RETURN: Martin (bowl star — 20-103; PS#3 recruit '24). PS: "this unit remains
  strong despite the loss of two career-leading RBs."

## WRTE
- "This position has held back the Penn State offense for several years and it's
  another restart after last year's top four all departed" (P6) — Pena (552), Ross
  (501), Hudson (288) all expire; the '25 import class never hit.
- IN (Becht's own targets): Sowell (ISU — three straight 500-yd seasons; B12
  Newcomer) + Eskildsen (ISU — league honors; 526/5) + ISU FRs Brookins/Robinson.
  RETURN: Howard (ex-4*, WR3).
- TE: Brahmer (ISU — #2 TE transfer, Mackey semifinalist, 6'6+) + Burkle (ISU 2-yr
  starter) + RETURN Rappleyea (Top100). Athlon: Brahmer "will continue the program's
  recent string of draft picks at the position."

## OL
- GONE: 6 OL incl. 4 starters (53 gm/122 career starts): Ioane (FIRST-ROUND pick),
  Shelton (rd-4 DAL), Dawkins, Rucci; Shanahan -> Florida; J.Williams -> Charlotte.
- RETURN: Donkoh — the only returning starter; moving G -> T; Campbell calls him an
  "AAOT" (PS). Cousins (bowl-game G starter); Top100 trio Goodman/Sexton/Aliciene
  battling at tackle.
- IN: Riker (Texas State C — Campbell: "could be the best C in the FBS" — PS) + FOUR
  ISU linemen (Buhr — All-B12, "a lock at guard" — P6; Ikakoula, Kuol, Tompkins) +
  Huff (D2). PS: "figure to be down a notch" vs Campbell's "absolute strength."

## DL
- GONE: all four starters — Dennis-Sutton (6.5 sk; rd-4 GB), Durant (rd-5 BUF),
  Gilliam -> Tennessee, Fisher -> USC — plus the depth (Harvey -> UNC, Wafle -> Duke,
  White -> Oklahoma State, Artis -> Temple). "The top five defensive tackles are
  gone" (P6).
- IN: the 300-lb wave — "four transfer tackles who weigh at least 319 pounds"
  (Athlon): Taupaki (UCLA; played for Lynn there; EIGHTH season) + K.Williams (UCLA)
  + Nnodim (Oklahoma State) + Ezeogu (ISU); Vakalahi (Utah), McPherson (Colorado),
  Carnell (ISU).
- RETURN: 4* Granville; Arrington II moves to edge; Kemajou, Blanding.

## LB
- Rojas RETURNS — "Penn State's top returning defensive player" (Athlon), the
  captain; tore his ACL before the UCLA game ("a turning point"); PS#19 LB.
- IN: the ISU trio — Ebel (2-yr MLB starter), Bacon (4* transfer OLB; 17.5 combined
  TFL w/ Ebel — Athlon), Brezina (two-deep). GONE: A.Campbell (#1 tkl; 76 tkl, 6.5
  sk) -> Tennessee, DeLuca (#3 tkl).
- Campbell (attributed): "the best LB room he has ever had"; PS: "my #12 rated LB
  corps"; Lynn's interior-size plan "seeks to funnel plays to the linebackers"
  (Athlon).

## DB
- RETAINED (the interim-continuity wins): CB Collins, NB Tracy ("a Swiss Army knife"
  — Campbell impressed, Athlon), Dixson (true-FR surge that "caused two veterans to
  transfer out" — P6).
- GONE: the top three safeties — Wheatley (the best tape), Mack -> NC State, Lane ->
  Tennessee; plus Washington -> Clemson, Woseley -> VT, Belgrave-Shorter -> ASU.
- IN (three ISU safeties): Neal — 4* transfer, B12 HM, 11 TFL, "will captain the
  secondary" (Athlon); "one of the best defensive players he has coached" (Campbell
  via PS) — Cooper ("would be in the NFL right now if he did not get injured" — PS)
  + Patton. Also Davis (BC), Fileppo (WVU), McDaniels (Syracuse).
- PS: "should get back towards the top in the PDR. Campbell's Iowa St secondaries
  were #10, #5 and #34 the last 3 years."

## Specialists
- K Barker RETURNS: 18-of-19 — led the FBS at 94.7%, perfect inside 50 (Athlon/PS).

  P Tiyce (Mississippi State), K Rosa (JMU). Returns: Martin KR, Howard PR.
- PS: STR #14 last year; "they remain solid but do need a P to emerge."
