# Maryland 2026 — magazine notes (blinding v2: team-level projections/rankings EXCLUDED)

Sources: Athlon 2026 (p.6 of B10 extract — header-verified); Pick Six 2026 (book pp.
90-91 = pdf 31-32, header-located); Phil Steele 2026 (p.17, text image). Captured
2026-07-16. 2025 (fact): 4-8, 1-8 B10 — a 4-0 start (incl. 27-10 over Wisconsin), then
eight straight losses (three by one score: UW 20-24, NEB 31-34, UCLA 17-20).

## Coaching
- Locksley yr 8 (37-49 here). NO HC change but the seat is discussed openly: Athlon
  scout — "they would like Locksley to work out. They're not trying to get rid of him,
  but it may just get to a point..."; Athlon: "the clock is ticking more loudly."
- NEW OC: Clint Trickett (from Jacksonville State — nine wins and the nation's rushing
  champ in '25); Pep Hamilton re-assigned to analyst. Trickett wants Washington to run
  more, a faster pace, more motion (Athlon). DC Ted Monachino RETAINED.
- The roster strategy (P6): budget shifted from portal to RETENTION — kept all ten of
  Inside Maryland Sports' most-important players; "second highest amount of returning
  production in Power 4" (P6); Athlon: "returns 71% of its 2025 statistical
  production, second only to Notre Dame."

## QB
- Malik Washington RETURNS after starting all 12 as a true FR (high 4*): 2,963 yds,
  17-9, freshman school records for completions and yards; 459 vs MSU = #2 all-time
  school game; 773 yds in first 3 games = most by a P4 true FR debut since 2018 (P6).
  #2 rusher on the team (303 @ 5.4 — PS).
- Efficiency gap named by P6: 57% comp, #16 in B10 QB rating — "room to grow";
  "should get the usual second-year starter bonus." PS: back in a spread system where
  "he should thrive and be one of the top QBs in the Big Ten as a sophomore."
- Athlon scout: "[Washington] is elite. He's a really good athlete, and we hit him a
  couple times, and it just didn't bother him... he was not really the reason they
  were losing."
- Depth: Kargman (Kent State; 7 career starts, 1,151, 43%, 8-5 — PS#56) + true FR
  Bernhard (PS#39).

## RB
- DeJuan Williams RETURNS as lead rusher (501 @ 3.9) and the receiving outlet — 426
  rec yds, most by a Maryland RB since 2001 (P6); 45 catches (Athlon).
- GONE: Ray (289-374 @ ~4-5; splits differ by book) -> Boston College.
- IN: Dalton (USC rFr; 4* in the '25 class — P6/PS), Worthy (Temple), Coombs (PS#47
  FR). Howard (125 @ 3.2) returns. PS: "RB by committee... talent with everyone back."
- Context: team rush production slid to 1,252 yds (4.1); 104 ypg = #124 (P6) /
  next-to-last in B10 (Athlon).

## WRTE

- IN: Durr (Wyoming's top receiver) + Gladding (Old Dominion; All-Sun Belt) — combined
  96 rec, 1,136 yds, 10 TD in '25 (P6); TE Preston Howard back from Auburn (former
  Terp). [ERRATUM 2026-07-16: a 'FR McDuffie' clause removed — that player is
  Northwestern's (P6 NU article); cross-page contamination during extraction.]

## OL
- '25: elite pass protection — sack on just 2% of attempts, #1 in P6's pass-pro
  metric, 9 sacks allowed ("aided by Washington's quick release" — P6; PS: "an elite
  1.8%") — but #128 OL Run Push (P6) and 104 rush ypg.
- GONE: All-B10 Herron (the line's best), Bah -> LSU. CONFLICT on Wright: P6 says
  three starters gone incl. Wright; Athlon prints Wright as a RETURNING RT starter
  (caps) AND names him in prose ("tackles Rahtrel Perry and Isaiah Wright, key
  figures"); PS's "91 career starts" unit math requires him. Adjudicated RETURNS
  (yr4 override — news.md).
- RETURN: C Hershey (All-B10-Academic), T Perry, Wright; Gilchrist (Top100) back from
  season-ending injury — "should become an all-league star" (P6).
- IN: Lockette (Texas State; 3-yr starter; three-star — P6). PS: unit has 91 career
  starts; "should be improved."

## DL
- The young edge pair is the headline: Stewart (school-record 10.5 TFL by a FR;
  FR All-American) + Mathis (Top100; Shaun Alexander FR-of-Year semifinalist; 6 sk) —
  "already one of the best young pass rush duos in America" (P6). PS: "top two sack
  guys are back" (Stewart 11 st/7 sk; Mathis 10 st/6 sk).
- PLUS: five-star Zion Elee signed — #1 DE and #5 overall in the 2026 class, "their
  biggest recruiting prize in school history" (P6); Athlon starts him at OLB day one.
- Interior is the question: run D collapsed — six straight 190+ yd rushing games
  allowed, 176 ypg (4.4) after 247 ypg over the last 5 (PS); Fontus -> Syracuse.
  RETURN: 300-lbrs Thomas + 4* Jenkins ("potential star" — P6), Taylor, Holmes.
- IN: Johnson (Texas), LeBlanc (UCF), Parker (Washington) — "three more Power 4
  linemen" (P6); PS: "10 players 300+ lbs... size to compete in the Big Ten."

## LB
- Everyone back: Wingate — first 100-tackle Terp season since 2018 (P6); "the star...
  a future pro on the inside" (PS); Reddick (#5 tkl) + C.Smith (64 tkl as a true FR —
  #4 tkl) + Flowers. Locksley: "the strength of the defense" (PS).
- PS: "an underrated unit"; lose only two NB (8 st, 37 tkl — counted at DB).

## DB
- PS PDR history: #95 in '24 -> #69 in '25 (63%, 25-19); nation-leading 19 INTs (P6).
  P6's opponent-adjusted view is bearish: "53rd of 68 P4 defenses... 52nd in pass
  defense."
- GONE: S Jalen Huskey (the do-it-all star; 1st-tm MAC import who hit; drafted — PS
  "#3 DC JAX"); plus CB depth exodus (Roland + Lee -> Arkansas, Humes -> Rutgers).
- RETURN: "7 of the top 8 back... The CBs are a strength" (PS): Joyner + Glasker —
  both B10 honorable mention, both "draft prospects" (Athlon); S Scruggs; Delhomme
  (ex-blue-chip, PS#32) "ready for a starting role" (P6).
- IN: Jackson (Boston College). FR Carey "could be the next big thing" (Athlon).

## Specialists
- BOTH legs return and both are decorated: K O'Haire — 1st-team All-B10, led the
  conference at 1.75 FG/gm, 21-24 (87.5% = 2nd-most accurate season at MD ever);
  P McFerson — 2nd-team All-B10, 44.2 avg (2nd in league), "regularly providing a
  field position edge" (Athlon).
- PS counterpoint: STR #105 — third straight 100+ finish (coverage/return units are
  the drag); "both are back and they will move up in the STR this season."
