# Washington 2026 — magazine notes (blinding v2: team-level projections/rankings EXCLUDED)

Sources: Athlon 2026 (p.19 — header-verified); Pick Six 2026 (book pp. 72-73 = pdf
13-14, header-located); Phil Steele 2026 (pdf p.9 spread, header-verified).
Captured 2026-07-16.
2025 (fact): 9-4, 5-4 B10 (LA Bowl W 38-10 over Boise St). OSU snapped the 22-game
home streak (6-24); the Maryland game was a 20-0 hole flipped by a 300-yard/24-point
final-four-drives comeback (24-20); 38+ in the other four B10 wins (RUT 38-19,
ILL 42-25, PUR 49-13, UCLA 48-14). Losses: OSU, Michigan 7-24 (the 3-INT game),
Wisconsin 10-13, Oregon 14-26. P6: Top-20 in BOTH his adjusted O and D ("only five
other programs pulled that off") with "an extremely young two-deep"; the gap —
"got within a score in the fourth quarter [vs playoff-tier teams] but failed to
finish."

## Coaching — NO HC change (Fisch yr 3, 15-11)
- Fisch calls the offense. DC Ryan Walters yr 2 (hired when Steve Belichick left
  for UNC): rush D "88th to 15th in yards/carry"; "16th of 68 P4" in P6's
  adjusted D. STC Petrilli continuity.
- The build shift: the '24 transition was transfer-heavy (his Arizona players);
  '25-'26 pivot to high school — back-to-back Top-25 classes, this one "#13
  nationally — a UW record" (P6). Athlon: "highest-rated recruiting class in
  school history."
- Athlon scout: "I almost see them in the same light as Minnesota. They're not a
  dark horse, but they'll be a top-half team... It was assumed that [Williams]
  was going to LSU when he entered the portal... once it came out that his
  agency cut ties with him because of it, there was no way that was gonna
  happen."

## QB — THE SAGA (adjudication-critical)
- WILLIAMS RETURNS: after the bowl win he "signed a contract to remain at UW.
  Two days later, Williams attempted to enter the transfer portal and it
  appeared headed to a precedent-setting court case, but then ultimately he
  returned to UW" (P6). The portal-out feed carries the entry (no-dest,
  Immediate) — override STAYS, triple-print.
- 2025: 4th in B10 QB Rating; led B10 QBs with 611 rush yards; the 16th FBS QB
  ever with a 400-pass/100-rush game (vs Rutgers); 25 TD/8 INT overall — but
  the split: 22-2 in the nine wins, 3-6 in the four losses (#2 "wins-only" QB
  rating, #12 "losses-only"); the 3-INT Michigan game. P6 3rd-team All-B10;
  "he could enter the Heisman discussion" with big-game growth. Fisch: "face
  of the program."
- Depth: E. Brown (Stanford transfer) + true FRs (McMillan).

## RB
- GONE: Coleman (1,100 total, 17 TD; NFL draft; injured in November) +
  Mohammed (consecutive 100-yd games in relief) -> Cal.
- RETURN: J. Washington — "top-notch explosiveness but suffered a severe injury
  in spring and is DOUBTFUL" (P6) — Athlon still prints him RB1 (conflict
  captured; availability flag).
- IN: Limar (Oregon 4*) + Cooley (Troy — "P4 vet" framing... Troy is G5; P6's
  words) + 4* FR Bonner ("expected to be part of the rotation" — Athlon).
  "An inexperienced position" (Athlon).

## WRTE
- GONE: BOSTON — "a pure #1 receiver... selected in the SECOND ROUND" (P6) —
  yr3 NFL declare. Vines-Bright -> Arizona State; O. Evans expired.
- RETURN: ROEBUCK (42-560-7 as a FR — #2) + R. Williams ("originally a starter
  lost to injury in September") + Lawson/J. Williams ('25 4* signees).
- IN: Moss (Kennesaw State's #2 target) + B. Miller (Ohio State; Top200 '25
  signee) + the Top200 FR trio James/Clay/Davis — "six four-stars competing
  for spots" (P6). Athlon projects "big roles" for the FR class.
- TE: Q. Moore gone ("the best blocking tight end [Fisch has] ever coached");
  DEGRAAF returns (32-360, #3 on team); Eldridge back (missed half).

## OL
- FOUR starters return: MILLS (Freshman All-America at LG) + the HATCHETT
  brothers (Landen C; Geirean G — "the Hatchett brothers are back at center
  and guard," P6) + AZZOPARDI RT ("improved in his second season").
- GONE: LT Willis (the lone loss; NFL). The battle: 5* FR GREENE ("expected to
  start right away" — Athlon) vs 4* transfer Dieterich (Sam Houston).
- '25: "improved by 50 spots in my OL Run Push" (P6).

## DL
- "The uncertainty lies in the trenches": two starters graduated (Durfee — the
  co-sack leader — and Uiagalelei) AND "another five rotational linemen
  transferred out to Power 4 programs" (Macon/OkSt, A. Parker + J. Parker/MD,
  Butler/TT, C. Smith/Bama). Quiet yr4 expiries on top: Thompson, Lynch, Pepa.
- RETURN: E. DAVIS — "the most consistent and he returns as the anchor at nose
  tackle" (P6); LANE (co-sack leader) at edge; Ward + R. Davis "back from
  injuries"; Hyde.
- IN: McClendon (Miss State — 12 games as a true FR, missed '25 with a knee) +
  Conley (Ball State starter) + Watts (Sacramento St) + George (Ohio State
  edge) + Top200 FRs Colman-Brusa and Hill. Athlon: "should be stout."

## LB
- "Overflowing with star power and is the strength of the defense" (P6); "one
  of the nation's best linebacking corps" (Athlon).
- RETURN: MANU ("should push for All-Big Ten" — injury-shortened '25) +
  ALEXANDER (surged late; 70 tkl, #2) + AL-UQDAH ("an original starter back
  from injury") + RAINEY-SALE (Top100 — "a wildcard the staff can use at
  backer and off the edge on passing downs").
- GONE: Bryant -> USC (their build documents the arrival).

## DB
- "The back seven remains elite" (P6). GONE: the nation's tallest CB duo —
  T. Davis + Prysock (both out of eligibility) + Esteen (yr4) + Holmes (->
  OkSt).
- The new tall duo: ROBINSON (Top200; 5 starts as a true FR; "first-round
  traits" per the staff) + KARNLEY (Virginia — ACC starter; 6'3).
- RETURN: McLAUGHLIN — "Defensive MVP... led the team in tackles and scored on
  a Pick Six and a fumble return" (P6); All-B10 HM (Athlon) + CLARK (nickel) +
  DILLARD-ALLEN ("Freshman All-American" — Athlon) at FS.

## Specialists
