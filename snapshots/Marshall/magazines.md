## Athlon (SBC p8) — depth chart + prose
- Header: Tony Gibson, 5-7 (1 yr). NF 95, Sun Belt East 3. 5-7, 3-5 SBC (incidentally visible - NOT used).
- OFF: "First-year HC Tony Gibson assembled an offense that produced on par with the one that won the
  conference the previous season. The bones of that group are back, including QB Carlos Del Rio-Wilson, who
  led the Herd in rushing and was an efficient passer in a breakout campaign." Run-first, QB-centric; "look
  for Jo'Shon Barbie to assume a larger share of the carries, supplemented by transfer TJ Lester (FCS West
  Georgia)." WRs Tamarez + Norton back; TE Toby Payne "will start for the 3rd consecutive season"; + transfers
  Owen Sweeney (FCS VMI) + Christian Hamilton + G Robby Martin (both from West Virginia).
- DEF: "The Herd had one of the WORST DEFENSES in the Sun Belt last season, and P4 teams still picked off
  several of their top players in the portal. The front was DECIMATED - three of the top four sack leaders
  transferred out, and the team's top three tacklers graduated. Javae Gilmore is the lone starting LB
  returning. Newcomers Kelvon McBride (NC State) + Mickel Williams (UTSA) can replace the lost pass-rush.
  The more pressing concern... is the secondary. Only a handful of teams allowed more passing yards. Safety
  Daytione Smith + CB Josh Pierre-Louis are the main holdovers... With Boogie Trotter (5 INTs) GONE, more
  playmakers will have to step up."
- ST: "Transfer Jorge Diaz Nicolas (Virginia) has big shoes to fill after All-MAC placekicker Lorcan Quinn
  transferred to Alabama. Nicolas has yet to attempt a kick in college, and punter Cooper Heimbach is a true
  freshman... Xayvion Turner-Bradshaw is the only established starter of the group."
- Scout (opposing SBC assistant): "Gibson can turn it around... you think you're inheriting a championship
  roster and literally you're starting over from scratch... they're tough, gritty... they got a RB from FCS
  West Georgia, TJ Lester, I really like him."
