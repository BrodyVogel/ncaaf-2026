## Program frame
- 2025: 4-8 (3-5 MAC). Defense allowed 475 ypg over the first 6 games, 352 over
  the last 6; opened 2-7, won 2 of the last 3. 2024 was 5-7 after a 5-2 start.
- 2026 = Chris Creighton yr 13 (61-83 at EMU; career 200-129 over 29 yrs —
  NAIA Ottawa 32-9, D3 Wabash 63-15, Drake 44-22, 8 conf titles). Six bowls in
  the last ten years at a program with no winning season 1996-2021; 25 upsets
  in the last 8 years. OC Mike Piatkowski (7th yr at EMU). CO-DCs Tate Omli +
  Kasey Teegardin; STC situation reshuffled (Kurzner returns after 2 yrs away).
  HC continuity → no coach-change multiplier.
- PS Exp Chart: #7 nationally — "one of the more veteran teams they have had
  recently" (2025 had just 5 offensive/3 defensive starters back; 2026 flips it).
- Athlon final analysis: "Having Kim and his top receivers back is a big plus...
  needs more from its front seven if it wants to contend for a bowl. EMU must
  find three wins beyond MAC newcomer Sacramento State, UMass and FCS
  Lindenwood."
- Opposing MAC assistant (Athlon): "Really, really well-coached... schematically
  sound... [OT] Tyler Brumfield is going to be a really good player for them...
  the expectation to make a bowl game is unrealistic at a place like that moving
  forward [portal economics]. Creighton does a great job."
- PS 2026 prose: "In their last 109 games, 79 have been a win or loss by one
  possession... QB Kim should be one of the MAC's best. Coach Creighton feels
  they will surprise people this year and likes the schedule."

## Offense (Athlon: 5 returning starters — Kim, Devereaux, Long, Anderson, Gallegos)
- QB: Noah Kim RETURNS — granted a 7TH SEASON (PS; feed still lists yr4 —
  override documented). Led the MAC in passing: 2,817 @ 61.4%, 18-11, 12 st;
  EM's #3 rusher (186). Orig Michigan St (5 starts '23). First time EMU returns
  its starting QB since 2017. HC Creighton: "Kim will be the best QB in the MAC
  this year." Depth: Stuckey (PS#115), rFr Button (Scout Team POY '25), Yale
  transfer McCaughey (390 yds in 2 gm '24; RS'd at Yale LY).
- RB: BOTH top backs gone (McMillan 1,014 @ 5.7 — first Eagle with 100 rush +
  100 rec in the same game; Dunlap 428 @ 4.0). Top returnee Mattord had 48 yds.
  Adds grad Braydon Bennett — Virginia Tech '25 (little action) after ~2,000
  career yds at Coastal Carolina ('21-24, played with QB Kim there) — and
  Syracuse transfer Malachi James (RS'd LY), plus rFr Blue (PS#100). PS:
  "despite the additions this unit won't match '25's solid production."
- WR/TE: top 3 pass-catchers RETURN — a veteran group "for once": TE Joshua
  Long (2nd-Tm All-MAC; 37-357-3), Nick Devereaux (3rd-Tm All-MAC; 510 @ 15.0,
  7 TD; 5-121 vs Ohio), Harold Mack (333 @ 16.7; 6-179 vs Miami OH in week 8 of
  his true-FR year). Plus Prosper (2 st, JC pedigree), Lemaster (ex-Kentucky),
  Fox (ex-Colorado St/JC). Loses #6 Lockett (263) and #5 (301). Adds 3
  transfers + 6 true FRs who were all in for spring. PS: "could have their best
  output since '21."
- OL: TWO starters back — C Gallegos (9 st) and RT Anderson (17 st L2Y) — plus
  yr4-override depth Strey. Imports with pedigree, not FBS tape: LT Tyler
  Brumfield (JC All-American; RS'd at Oklahoma St LY — the opposing-coach
  praise piece), Dellinger (Michigan St, injured/DNP L2Y), Payne (Arizona rFr
  DNP '24 → Delaware St 5 gm), Kayed (St Francis PA, 2 st/18 gm), McMillan
  (Northwestern St/SEMO starts; DNP at Illinois LY). PS: "solid OLine and
  should be at their best in years!" (offense-wide claim).

## Defense (4 returning starters — Evans, Manning, Llewellyn, Salas)
- DL: rush defense allowed a league-worst 232.1 ypg. Returns veteran DE Carter
  Evans (11 st, 37 tkl, 5.5 tfl — yr4 override, both prints) + EDGE Quincy Byas
  (EM Def Newcomer OY as true FR: 3 st, 23 tkl, 3 sk) + rotation (Gonzalez,
  Tayou, Gilbert, Thomas, Patterson, Hardin). Loses its two best interior
  players (Green, Martin — both expired) + Miles (→ UTEP). Adds 2nd-Tm
  Southland NT Peyton Christian (Lamar, 13 st, 38 tkl, 3 sk), D2 Harding's
  Oluwayemi, and JC PS#60 Jefferson Adam — who missed ALL of 2025 with a
  preseason injury and "projects to start" (yr4 override, Athlon-explicit).
- LB: Barry Manning returns (10 st, 73 tkl, 5.5 tfl — 2nd on team, tied for TFL
  lead; "capable of playing safety," forced to LB by injuries; yr4 override) +
  Mowchan (3 st '24 then OFY; injury-battled two seasons; yr4 override). Adds
  two JUCO tacklers: McDuffey (86 tkl) + Flowers (103 tkl). Loses A.Marshall
  (→ Minnesota) + Patierno. The unit allowed foes ~70 ypg above their average
  in '25 (PS) — the weak level of the defense.
- DB: "deep and experienced backline" — S Bryce Llewellyn (2nd-Tm All-MAC, team
  MVP, #1 tackler: 12 st, 102 tkl, 5 tfl, 2 int; yr4 override) + BAN Jason
  Marshall (4 st, 64 tkl; yr4 override) + FS Salas (6 st, 34 tkl) + CBs Coley
  (ex-Michigan St, now full-time), Dobbs (3 st), Rogers, Norman, Toney (OFY
  inj). Loses Scott (→ Nevada), Deener (expired), both Mondses. PS PDR was
  #107 in '25 — the tape is worse than the experience narrative.
- 2025 ST/DEF context (retrospective): PS STR #111 in '25.
