# Illinois 2026 — magazine notes (blinding v2: team-level projections/rankings EXCLUDED)

Sources: Athlon 2026 preview (Illinois section); Pick Six Previews 2026 (pp. 82-83);
Phil Steele 2026 (p. 13, text + lineup grids). Captured 2026-07-16.
2025 result (historical fact): 9-4, 5-4 B10; won Music City Bowl 30-28 over Tennessee.

## Coaching
- Bielema yr 6 (37-26 at Illinois; 134-84 career). NO HC change. OC Barry Lunney Jr.
  CONTINUES. DC change: Aaron Henry -> Notre Dame; hired BOBBY HAUCK (longtime Montana
  FCS HC, Rocky Long tree) to install an aggressive, attacking 3-3-5 (Athlon/P6). P6:
  Bielema's 2022-design scheme had been "essentially figured out"; he wanted the change
  and "offered only one man the job."
- Athlon: Bielema "counting on the element of surprise to give his team a boost."
- Roster context (fact, not projection): P6 — "graduation of a large, program-changing
  senior class leaves behind a younger and inexperienced two-deep"; Athlon — "one of the
  least-experienced teams in the country a year after being one of the most experienced."
- Recruiting: 2026 class #21 nationally — highest-ranked in program's modern era (P6);
  Bielema 5-for-5 on Top 50 classes at Illinois.

## QB
- Altmyer GONE (yr4): 23 wins (2nd-most by an Illinois QB), highest comp% (64) in school
  history, 7 game-winning drives over three years — nation's most in that span (P6).
- Katin Houser IN from East Carolina (began at Michigan State; former 4*). P6 2-yr ECU
  line: 5,306 yds, 37 TD, 17 INT; 4th in AAC in QB rating in '25. PS: 12 starts '25 —
  3,300 yds, 65.9%, 19-6; earlier MSU stint 7 starts, 1,132, 58.5%. PS ranks him among
  his top-15 QBs (unit-level). Athlon: "made a strong impression in the spring, winning
  over his new teammates with his talent and leadership."
- P6 scouting: "bigger frame, strong arm, but lacks the elusiveness of Altmyer. That
  could be an issue given the complete rebuild of the offensive line."
- Backup: Carson Boyd (Fr.) after Hampton portaled out (Southern Miss).

## RB
- Trio that shared '25 carries "returns intact" (P6): Valentine led with 614 yds (speedy,
  shifty); Laughery "may be the best of the bunch if he can avoid injuries"; big-back
  Feagin moved to TE this spring. Athlon: Laughery + Valentine combined 996 rush yds, 7
  TD in '25; coaches felt good enough about the room to move Feagin out.

## WRTE
- GONE: top receiver Hank Beatty (3rd-tm All-B10, #2 in B10 in YAC behind the Biletnikoff
  winner — P6); red-zone threat Bowick (Oklahoma State); BOTH TEs — blocker Arkin and
  receiver Rusk (Arizona; 4* transfer grade, most TE yards here since 2020).
- RETURN: #2/#3 receivers Dixon (35 rec) and Clement (36 rec) — combined 1,002 yds, 8 TD
  (P6); "figure to be Houser's top targets" (Athlon).
- IN: 1st-team All-CUSA 6'5 Alex Perry (FIU) + speedy Jayshon Platt (FAU) — combined
  1,560 yds, 14 TD "at the non-AQ level" (P6); Ty Robinson (Ball St).
- TE: Feagin conversion "could have a major impact" (Athlon); Abney moves up to start.
- Freshmen Trimble + 4* Rankin "could push into the rotation" (P6).

## OL
- "Complete rebuild" (P6) / "mostly new offensive line" (Athlon). Four starters gone incl.
  first-team All-B10 (P6 wording; PS: 4th-team AA) JC Davis. Guard BRANDON HENDERSON is
  the lone returning starter.
- '25 line was veteran yet: #50 OL Run Push, #98 yds/carry, #110 pass pro — sack on 9% of
  attempts, "and that was with a top-notch scrambler in Altmyer" (P6 — their own metrics,
  raw ranks).
- IN: C Jake Renfro (Wisconsin) — "Big Ten starting experience," son of an Illinois OL
  alum; LT Christian Martin (Colorado State, 12 starts — PS). Staff signed 3 of the top
  5 JUCO linemen: Matelau, Taylor, Washington (P6).
- Knapik "played well in the bowl game" with Davis opted out (P6). Line coach Bart Miller
  retained — "spent the spring trying to fit the pieces together" (Athlon).

## DL (3-3-5 front)
- PS: Illinois "loses their entire starting front seven." Scheme change "caused an exodus"
  (P6): four top linemen transferred to P4 rosters — 4* Durojaiye (South Carolina),
  McCullom (Texas A&M), Coenen (Michigan State), Neal (Purdue). "Quite the talent drain."
- Gabe Jacas off to the pros (1st-tm All-B10; led league w/ 11 sacks — one of the
  program's best pass-rush seasons). OLBs Bryant + Lowery also gone.
- RETURN: Farrell ("was in the rotation, moves into a starting role" — P6; "learned from
  Jacas" — Athlon); Barna "played well in the bowl game" (P6).
- IN (rotational, non-AQ per P6): Sullivan (Washington State), Prater (Jacksonville St),
  All-Ivy Janki (Penn); Holmes (Utah State) in Athlon two-deep.
- Opposing B10 assistant (Athlon scouting box): their DL "was outrageously good the last
  two years... every bit as good as Penn State's, Michigan's and Ohio State's. But I know
  most of that group has left, so it'll be interesting to see who they replace them with."

## LB
- Starting MLB Rosiek GONE. Kreutz + Brown are "the top returnees" (Athlon); Brown a
  former JUCO All-American (P6). 4* Beerman "impressed as a freshman and could become
  their next star" (P6). Kante ranked the #1 JUCO linebacker (P6).

## DB
- "The clear strength of the defense, and the overall team, is in the secondary" (P6).
- Safeties Bailey (team-high 76 tkl '25 — Athlon) and Xavier Scott BOTH "turned down the
  pros for a final season" (P6). Clarke ("Juice") back at corner — "should be an honors
  candidate" (Athlon); P6 calls it "three all-league stars in the defensive backfield."
- IN: #1 JUCO cornerback Jakwon Morris (P6). GONE: three-year starter, two-time captain
  Miles Scott. P6: "this will still be a top secondary in the Big Ten" (unit-level).

## Specialists
- K Olano won the bowl with a late FG, then transferred (Texas A&M). Moczulski returned
  to Illinois after a year at Washington to take placekicking (Athlon). P Lars Rau added
  from Texas State (Athlon two-deep starter; PS: 24 punts, 36.3 avg). PS: '25 STR #51.
