  3-3-5 = "nation's #1 pass defense in opposing QB Rating"; 4-3 start w/ two final-play losses -> closed hot.

  8 TD on 25 touches, 6-4), Hunter (moves up), Richardson (299 as FR), Mizell (fastest). Adds:
  Gallagher (WVU starter), DJ Jordan (USC Top100). P6 opinion: "the 2026 room looks like an upgrade."

- OL: stability paid off 2025 (P6 OL Run Push #90 -> #40); 3 starters gone incl. all-league LT Buchanan;

  injury-forced "mixed results"; Henning (Washington) added at C.
- DEF: "biggest defensive turnaround in FBS" - +50 spots in all 17 P6 categories; #11 opp-adjusted;
  22 INT (2nd natl); 2.5 TO/gm (1st); 8 straight opponents under 200 pass yds (best in B12 since 2004 Mizzou).

  3rd-tm - all gone + three more corners. Cole = lone returning starter (All-B12, 4 INT). EIGHT transfer
  DBs: Hartzog (Nebraska starter, position-flexible), Chapa (1st-tm All-Big Sky), Boss (All-MW),
  Austin (Oregon Top100), Tagoa'i (USC Top100, LB->S). P6 cautionary comp: Illinois 2022 #1 pass D ->
  #70 the next year after sending 3 to the draft... "in this example though, the mastermind coordinator returns."
- Front six: 6 of top 10 back; captain Tre Smith returns from injury (60 tkl/8.5 TFL/4.5 sk career);
  Palu middle; Pese B12 All-Freshman; LBs Brown (3rd-yr starter), Kennedy (hybrid), Mann ("Red Rover"
  pick-6), Robinson.
- Context (facts only): #8 B12 five-year recruiting; "second-worst from the portal with just one
