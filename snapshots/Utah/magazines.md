## Pick Six 2026 (B12 pp. 104-105)
- 2025 arc (historical): 11-2, 7-2 - five league wins by 27+ (42-10 over defending champ ASU,
  43-0 halftime lead on Colorado, held Sorsby to 33% completions); 4Q fades in the two losses
  (TT, BYU). "One of Whittingham's strongest Utah teams" - 7 of 9 linemen All-B12.
- COACH CHANGE: Whittingham stepped down after 21 seasons… then "a few days after the
  announcement, took the head coaching job at MICHIGAN," bringing OC Beck + key players
  (Snowden, Daley, Lea'ea, Buchanan, Moa all -> Michigan per feed). MORGAN SCALLEY promoted -
  "head coach in waiting" since 2023, All-American S on the '04 team, DC for a decade.
  Whittingham: "Scalley is an exceptional football coach, a proven commodity."
- Scalley delegates DC/play-calling to Colton Swan (LB coach here since 2019). New OC: Kevin
  McGiven (Utah State) - "scheme similar to what worked here last year… a bit more passing,
  more vertical downfield shots."
- QB: Dampier returns off B12 Newcomer of the Year - 24 TD/5 INT, improved accuracy and
  turnovers in the P4 step-up, "the most elusive pocket scrambler in America," battled
  injuries. Ficklin = lightning: 10 rush TD at 8.4/carry; "look for packages… these two
  don't mind sharing."
- RB: Parker 981 @ 6.6 (2nd in P4 min-100 carries, behind only Jeremiyah Love) returns as
  the workhorse; add short-yardage "bulldozer" Chavez-Soto (SJSU).
- WR/TE: top three pass-catchers gone (Davis, TEs Bentley + Buchanan). Scalley: "the best
  wide receiver crew we've ever had at Utah." Adds: Pegan (Utah St) + Shoels (SJSU) - both
  All-MW, ~1,700 combined, both 4* transfer grades. Returners poised: Simmons, R.Johnson,
  Whittemore; TE Andrews back from '25 injury. No 1,000-yd receiver in 13 years (context).
- OL: "best offensive line ever" (Whittingham) - #2 Run Push/#13 Pass Pro, school rushing
  records - ALL FIVE STARTERS GONE (Fano + Lomu drafted as FIRST-ROUNDERS; all five
  All-B12; ace line coach Harding gone too). Return: 6th/7th jumbo-package linemen Moea'i +
  Olson as starting guards; Harrison (bonus eligibility season) at C; add FCS national
  champ Jefferson (Montana State) RT; 5* LT Obot "could start right away."
- DEF: 9 starters gone incl. ALL FOUR DL, the sack leader, two veteran LBs, the top corner.
  Daley (led P4 in sacks/game) + Lea'ea + Snowden all -> Michigan.
- DL: inside - Sepulona + Kaufusi (130+ snaps each); ends - Dillon + Holtzclaw move up;
  add NT starter Ethan Day. '25 run D "unusually weak" (KSU's 472 the low point).
- LB: tackle leader Hall returns; Barton + Damuni gone; Reynolds up; NO transfers signed.
- DB: "the safety room is a strength" - Bennee "is a play-maker" (4 INT, All-B12 hype);
  Evans (original starter) + 6th-yr Ritchie back from '25 injuries. Five DBs out to P4
  rosters; Scooby Davis = lone returning corner; JUCO Caldwell at nickel; Reed (Akron) +
  Chenault (USF) battle for the other corner.
- Culture note: "RSNB" (Relentless Smart Nasty Ballhawks); nation-best 24 straight seasons
  with a pick-six.
