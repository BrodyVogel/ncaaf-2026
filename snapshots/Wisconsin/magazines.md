# Wisconsin 2026 — magazine notes (blinding v2: team-level projections/rankings EXCLUDED)

Sources: Athlon 2026 (p.20 — header-verified); Pick Six 2026 (book pp. 78-79 = pdf
19-20, header-located); Phil Steele 2026 (pdf p.14 spread, header-verified).
Captured 2026-07-16.
2025 (fact): 4-8, 2-7 B10 — 81 points in nine conference games; 12 ppg (worst in
P4); PASS p0 as-played; back-to-back losing seasons for the first time since
1991-92 (the '24 season had already snapped the FBS-best 22-year bowl streak).
The arc: 2-6 with "Fire Fickell" chants -> AD McIntosh's vote of confidence ->
two days later the 13-10 upset of #23 Washington (field storming; snapped
Fickell's 0-10 vs ranked) -> the 27-10 upset of #21 Illinois (second storming).
Also: W Miami-OH 17-0, W MTSU 42-10; L Bama 14-38, Maryland 10-27, Michigan
10-24, Iowa 0-37, OSU 0-34, Oregon 7-21, Indiana 7-31, Minnesota 7-17.

## Coaching — NO HC change (Fickell yr 4, 17-21; 10-17 B10)
- P6's frame: "considered by most as the top hire of the post-2022 carousel,
  Fickell now ranks dead last of the 10 Power 4 hires" on three-year output; a
  win-column drop each year; two straight empty rivalry-trophy years. Retained
  via the November vote of confidence.
- OC Grimes yr 2 (the "RVO" Reliably Violent Offense); DC Tressel continuity.
  NEW OL coach ERIC MATEOS (Arkansas — "his line helped them land the #1
  rushing offense in my opponent-adjusted ranking" — P6; Grimes's Baylor
  colleague). FIRST full-time STC of the Fickell era: Bob Ligashesky (poached
  from Minnesota).
- Portal class: 33 transfers, ZERO four-stars — 49th nationally, 13th in the
  B10 (P6); "unable to retain several veteran multi-year starters."
- Athlon scout: "They scored 81 points in nine conference games. I don't care
  who your quarterback is. That speaks to a lot of bigger issues... I was
  surprised that both Wisconsin and Maryland decided to run it back with their
  head coaches."

## QB
- The '25 collapse: Edwards injured on the THIRD PLAY of the season (->
  UNC now); O'Neil threw as many picks as TDs and was hurt in game four; true
  FR C. Smith stepped up in November. "Three straight years of transfer
  starting quarterbacks getting injured early" (P6).
- IN: COLTON JOSEPH (Old Dominion) — Sun Belt OPOY (Athlon) / 2nd-team All-SBC
  (P6); 1,000+ rush yards LY incl. two 75-yd TD runs vs Indiana; two-year
  totals 4,200 pass / 1,600 rush / 56 TD; #21 transfer QB; "a true dual-threat
  ... a mobile element that the program has been missing" (P6); ODU downfield
  shots + play-action fit (Athlon).
- RETURN: O'Neil ("should be back in time for fall camp") + C. Smith. Also
  D. Adams (Louisville).

## RB
- 2025: the top two backs missed seven games combined (663 total yards); 3.3
  ypc (#128); no 100-yard rusher until game 11; Joseph (at ODU) had more
  10+yd runs (37) than Wisconsin's whole team (33).
- GONE: D. Jones -> LSU. RETURN: DUPREE (lead rusher). IN: SAMA (Iowa State —
  1,933 career; the 270-yard "Snowmageddon" game) + B. Jackson (USC) +
  Palmer (TCU). "They want a running back rotation" (P6).

## DL
- P6: "their top four defensive linemen [and] top four outside backers... are
  all gone" — Reiger (the quoted OLB; "you start to feel like a touchdown is
  the end-all, be-all"), Peterson, Barten, Suggs, P. Petersen, B. Lane,
  Walker, Witt — all yr4 expiries.
- RETURN: CHEEKS ("second on the team in QB pressures") + FEARBRY at the ends

  STAYS; Athlon DE1).
