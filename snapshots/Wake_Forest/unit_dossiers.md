## THE SHAPE
A GOOD team (9-4) hit by heavy attrition. The defense was the strength and a
deep, proven DL is the anchor that survives. But Wake lost its QB duo, its
lead RB, all but one WR, four of five OL starters, its two top LBs, and its
three secondary STARS (Andersen 87.3, Prunty 84.1, Webb 77.7 - all yr4). The
offense returns only 2 starters and is a transfer/FCS-heavy reload behind a
new QB (UNC transfer Gio Lopez). The kicker is good; the punter is an
unproven freshman. Net: strong front seven, questions almost everywhere else.
