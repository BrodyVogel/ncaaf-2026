# Bowling Green — magazine notes (blinding v2: team-level ratings/projections/market numbers excluded)

Sources: Athlon MAC preview p.5 (header-verified "BOWLING GREEN FALCONS"); Phil Steele
MAC pdf p.9 (300dpi spread, banner visually verified). No Pick Six (P4-only publication).

## Program frame
- 2025: 4-8 (2-6 MAC). Eddie George yr 1 after Scot Loeffler left for the NFL in
  February 2025 — a brutally late HC change that wrecked the portal cycle; PS: only
  6 returning starters, near-bottom nationally in experience in 2025.
- 2026 = George yr 2. Career 28-30 (5 yrs: Tennessee State 24-22, BGSU 4-8).
- Staff: 4-yr co-OC Greg Nosal elevated to sole OC. NEW DC Joe Bowden — George's LB
  coach for 5 years (TnSt + BGSU '25), internal promotion. New STC Greg Froelich
  (Charlotte '23-24). HC continuity → no coach-change multiplier (HC-only rule).
- PS 2025 season shape: opened 3-3 (road losses to bowl teams Cincy/Louisville/Ohio;
  first 20+pt comeback in BG history to beat Toledo), then blew leads vs Kent St
  (led 21-3 in 3Q), EMU and Akron in a 5-game MAC skid; closed 45-14 over UMass.
  −48 ypg in MAC play. Down to the #4 QB for the last 4 games.
- George signed the #1 recruiting class in the MAC (per PS); Athlon top-10 recruits
  list is all 3-star (Durham-Smith EDGE, 3 TEs, Mock QB, 2 OTs).
- Athlon final analysis: "several unknowns but a potentially elite quarterback in
  Novosad... questions along the offensive line... secondary isn't deep, but there
  are difference-makers in the first two levels. A bowl berth is the ceiling."
- PS 2026 prose: more stable at QB, year two, "stronger on both offense and defense."

## Offense
- QB: lost 2-yr starter pre-2025; 2025 was a 4-QB catastrophe (Pyne 882 then OFY;
  Anderson OFY; May injured vs Kent St; #4 QB Najm threw 473 at 50% the rest of the
  way). 2026: only Najm (3 st) returns. Adds Austin Novosad (Oregon, ex-4-star, QB2
  behind Nix/Gabriel/Moore — 4 passes in 3 years; Athlon: "expect Novosad to be the
  guy in the fall", "potentially elite"; PS QB rank #14) and Jay Kastantin (D-II
  Assumption: 3,658 pass / 710 rush / 37 TD over 3 yrs; 9 st, 1,974 at 62%, 19-5,
  530 rush in 2025). PS's own lineup lists KASTANTIN first — open battle. True FR
  Jarin Mock (PS#113). PS: "can only be more stable."
- RB: 2025 top-3 committee mostly gone; Austyn Dendy (ex-Missouri) DNP first 7 games
  then first BG back with 3 straight 100-yd games since 2017 — led team 493 (4.6).
  Athlon: leading returning rusher, 1,000-yd shot. Adds Ke'Marion Baldwin
  (Charleston Southern FCS, 778 @ 4.9). Nakai Amachree returns (PS RB #141).
  PS: "I look for a big year from Dendy" but RB was "my biggest question" in '25.
- WR/TE: loses top 6 receivers — but they combined for only 1,394 @ 11.1 (post-
  Fannin collapse; 2025 was down to TE Jyrin Johnson leading with 466). Shifted to
  12/13 personnel. Top pass-catcher has been a TE 3 straight years; So. Jeremiah
  Scoby (PS TE #126) projected as the featured piece; A-TE Blane Cleaver (11 st) is
  the lone returning starter on offense. Ridley and Sharp move up from reserve
  roles (Ridley ex-Hampton 596 in '24). Adds 2nd-Tm CAA WR Isaiah Dawson (Richmond,
  575 @ 13.7), Nick Sowell (SC State 247), D3 Reddik Pillarelli (Ohio Wesleyan 876).
  PS: "can only be more productive."
- OL: total reboot — ALL FIVE 2025 starters gone. James Thomas Jr. (2 starts, ~400
  snaps) to RT; Alexis Sanchez (ex-NAU) was rotational; C Brody Bolyn 1 career
  start; LT = App State transfer Gabe Funk (1 game LY). PS: "the OLine is the major
  question mark." Athlon: "questions along the offensive line."

## Defense (PS: 4 returning starters — Pringle, E.George, Pascal, Estoesta)
- DL: loses 4 starters (Afogho, Kelly, Branch-Haynes + Booker) but returns both sack
  leaders: Rush Myles Bradley (5 sk; 41 tkl, 6 sk, 9 qbh L2Y) and Eriq George (4 sk;
  son of HC; 2nd-Tm Big South-OVC at TnSt '24) who moves inside to NT. Adds
  Louisville DT Selah Brown (PS DT #56, 8 gm LY), Kade Kostus (ex-CMU, 14 st '23,
  injured '24) and JaxSt rFr Timothy Taylor. Athlon: "front six has the potential
  to be strong." Went from 2-gap to knock-back front in '25; sacks dropped 33→22.
- LB: lost #1 tackler / 1st-Tm MAC LB Gideon Lampron (119 tkl, 17.5 tfl) + #5.
  MLB Dorian Pringle: dominant early '25 (27 tkl, 6 tfl, INT, 2 FF in 3 games —
  #11 MAC tackler at OFY) before ACL vs Liberty; Athlon: All-MAC ceiling if healthy.
  AJ Odom (ex-Memphis, 2 st) at SLB; Andrew Hines III (3 st, 22 tkl) behind Pringle.
  Adds Purdue transfer Sanders Ellis — Big South-OVC Def FrOY at Tennessee St '24
  (14 st, 86 tkl, 13 tfl) under George/Bowden, then 11 quiet games at Purdue.
  PS: "much more experienced."
- DB: lone returning starter in the secondary = FS Kal-El Pascal (12 st, 80 tkl, #2
  tackler, INT; "leader of the secondary"). CB JoJo Johnson (ex-Indiana) returns
  after leading the MAC with 13 PBU (4 st); boundary S Preston Estoesta (4 st, 29
  tkl) also back. Loses the #3/#4 tacklers. Adds 1st-Tm MEAC CB Drey Braxton (NC
  Central, 12 st, 40 tkl), NMSU S Armahn Hale (7 st, 44 tkl), FAU S Mike Wright III,
  CharSo CB Nick Sawyer. Athlon: "the secondary isn't deep."

## Special teams
- 2025 was strong (PS ST rank #34) — and ALL THREE anchors depart: K Jackson
  Kleather (19-22 FG, 56-yarder), P John Henderson (40.5), KR Cam Pettaway (FBS-best
  33.0 KR avg, 1st-Tm MAC, MAC FrOY; opened the season with a 98-yd KR TD).
- 2026: K = walk-on Travis Kenner — career HOLDER, zero college FG attempts
  (Athlon: "which isn't always ideal"). P = SEMO transfer Ryan Kingston (41.0 avg,
  38.2 net, 22 inside-20, 2nd-Tm OVC; orig E. Michigan). KR Amachree, PR Ridley —
  both unproven. PS: "they figure to drop some."
