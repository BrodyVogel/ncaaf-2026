## Pick Six 2026 (B12 pp. 15-16 = book 112-113; verbatim)
- 2025 arc: 8-5. Opened B12 play with consecutive 27-24 wins (Baylor, TCU) on last-minute Leavitt
  drives; beat #6 Texas Tech on a final-minute TD. Leavitt then KNOCKED OUT of the lineup: Utah dealt
  a 42-10 beatdown, lost to Houston, five turnovers in a 23-7 Territorial Cup loss. Two-year window
  framing: 19 wins (2024 B12 title + CFP appearance) = best two-year span since 2013-14.
- TRANSITION: "18 starters are gone" - "from the most veteran roster to a new-look transition
  lineup." Transfer haul ranked 3rd-best in B12, #15 nationally (247, both books).
- QB: Leavitt OUT to LSU ("top-rated transfer this cycle"). IN: Boley (Kentucky, 6-5, former 4*):
  started 10 games as RS-Fr, #12 SEC in QB Rating, #6 among all P4 freshmen, 65% comp, "big arm with
  impressive touch"; 12 INTs - OC Arroyo claims many were end-of-game desperation heaves. Keene
  (Michigan; 34 starts at UCF/Fresno). Top100 true FR Fette "stole the show in spring."
- WR: Tyson drafted #8 OVERALL; McClain graduated. Eusebio (All-B12 HM) = "lone returning starter"
  on offense. Portal: "one of the nation's best receiver hauls" - three P4 starters: Omarion Miller
  (Colorado MVP, All-B12, #3 transfer WR), Reed Harris (BC, 4* hype, vertical threat), Vines-Bright
  (Washington, cracked rotation as true FR). Moss returns in slot. "The clear strength of the team."
- RB: Raleek Brown (1,141 @ 6.1, workhorse) OUT to Texas w/ 4* grades. Jason Brown 134 in Sun Bowl;
  Kyson Brown = returning veteran off 2025 injury; FCS pair: Gillis (Del State, Top-10 FCS: 1,200 @
  6.5) + Avit (Villanova short-yardage hammer).
- TE: Metayer graduated with B12 honors -> former 4*s Khamari Anderson + AJ Ia.
- OL: "total rebuild with all five starters gone"; LT Iheanachor = 1st-round pick. C set: Alo-Tupuola
  "stolen" off Georgia Tech's top-rated line. Baklenko (2-yr Stanford starter, OU backup) should win
  RT. Jarmaine Mitchell = nation's #1 overall JUCO prospect, Georgia flip, likely LT. Stewart earned
  late-season starts at G. P6 (opinion): "a third-straight Top 40 finish in OL Run Push is in question."
- DEF: DC Ward has stacked consecutive Top-25 defenses (opp-adjusted; only ASU + BYU in league).
  "To make it three straight, they must pull off a total reset of the two-deep. Ten opening day
  starters are gone including six who earned All-Big 12 nominations."
- DL: Fite = the lone returning starter, "star defensive tackle" who turned down an early NFL bid.
  Dorbah/Wodtly/O'Neal gone. BREAK: 2024 starting DE Clayton Smith WON AN ELIGIBILITY APPEAL for a
  bonus 2026 year. Adds: P4 starters Thompson (MSU) + Winston (Baylor); 4* Hugo = "spring hero per
  practice reports"; JUCO Vakalahi (6-5, 260) wildcard.
- LB: Crook + Elliott both 2nd-team All-B12, combined 199 tackles (1-2 on team), both graduated.
  Hughes (400 snaps partial-starter) rises. Owen Long (Colorado State) = "the nation's tackle leader,"
  1st-team All-MW, 4* transfer grades, "flying all over the field in spring" - makes LB "a 'reload'
  instead of a rebuild" (P6 opinion).
- DB: "All five original starting defensive backs are gone." Wilson stepped up after Alford's injury;
  Warren surged at nickel (both return). Abney -> NFL; Robinson -> Wisconsin. Adds: Stamps (LSU,
  "once an SEC starter before falling out of the lineup last year"), Bimage moves up opposite,
  Rawls (Kansas) had 73 tackles.
- Context (facts only): P6 outlook cites "too many uncertainties - the offensive line and 10 new
  starters on defense"; schedule/finish framing EXCLUDED per blinding.

## Athlon 2026 (B12 PDF p4; clean text)
- 2025: 8-5 (6-3 B12), Sun Bowl L 39-42 vs Duke. Coach: Dillingham 22-17 (3 yrs). OC Marcus Arroyo,
  DC Brian Ward - both return (continuity).
- Scouting quote (opposing B12 assistant): "impressed with who they picked up in the portal...
  better across the board; better at wideout... Omarion Miller is a special player, Reed Harris is a
  special player... they'll lose a bit of production in the backfield because Raleek Brown was
  really, really good. But I'm expecting them to be good."
- OFFENSE: leans on the No. 15 transfer portal class (247). Boley "will likely be the triggerman";
  added "the nation's top-ranked wide receiver transfer class" (Miller, Harris, Vines-Bright) -
  "All three look like future NFL Draft picks." Alo-Tupuola (helped GT rank among top FBS rushing
  attacks) stabilizes; Baklenko at RT. Backfield "deep and talented": Kyson Brown back at full
  strength from season-ending injury; Gillis 1,100+ at FCS Delaware State.
- DEFENSE: "should again be very stout": Fite = all-conference candidate at NT; transfer ends
  Thompson + Winston; Long (FBS leading tackler) replaces the two 2nd-team All-B12 LBs w/
  "sixth-year returner Zyrus Fiaseu and Martell Hughes also starter-level players." Secondary =
  "ASU's biggest question," esp. nickel; Wilson could move from FS to NB. Stamps ("two-year starter
  at LSU") helps replace Abney/Robinson; Bimage "ready to shine"; Rawls led Kansas with 57 solo tackles.
- SPECIALISTS: McLarty (OSU transfer) = "big-legged Australian punter" to fix "second-worst in the
  FBS in net punting in '25"; K Carson Smith (Austin Peay) = FCS UAC Specialist of the Year.
- FINAL ANALYSIS (unit-relevant): "deeper and more talented than Dillingham's prior three teams...
  will need to prove that they didn't regress too much at quarterback with their move from Leavitt
  to Boley." Team-level forecast numbers EXCLUDED.
