## Athlon (p.13)
- 2025: 8-5 (6-2 MAC); Boca Raton Bowl vs Louisville Dec 23 (results-column
  OCR partially jumbled; header record verified).
- HC Jason Candle -> UConn. Mike Jacobs (Mercer) first season; career 94-23
  over 10 years. OC Cris Reisert, DC Jahmal Brown. 14 Mercer transfers
  followed Jacobs to Toledo.
- OFFENSE (1) returning starter: LT STEPHEN GALES (bold; "All-MAC
  candidate"). QB John Alan Richter the "known commodity": backup past two
  seasons, 3 starts as rFr in '24, career 66% comp, 7 TD / 1 INT, "shown
  flashes in limited opportunities."
- RB: CJ Miller (Mercer, one of the 14) "should be the top running back" —
  1,267 scrimmage yds, 17 TD in '25. Connor Walendzak (led Toledo rushing
  two years ago) "should also be a factor."
- OL "welcomes back four players with starting experience," Gales the lead.
- WR room "full of transfers": Kalvin Gilbert Jr. (SEMO), Anthony Simpson
  (Michigan), Adjatay Dabbs (Mercer) early impressions; Bryson Hammer a
  factor after being primarily a punt returner so far.
- DEFENSE (0) returning starters. DE Andrew Zock = 2025 FCS Defensive
  Player of the Year (20 TFL, 11.5 sk) — stayed with Jacobs despite Power 4
  offers, "will be the leader on the defense." LB Kari Jackson (2 seasons
  at Penn State) early spring impression. LBs Julian Fox + Isaac Prince
  All-SoCon at Mercer. S Armorion Smith: final season, 7 career starts at
  Michigan State. CB Cam Jones All-Patriot League (Holy Cross); former
  D-III nickel Mo Conteh "turning heads" in spring.
- SPECIALISTS: K Reice Griffith 14-of-17 at Mercer, half his makes from
  40+. P job "up for grabs" between returner Emilio Duran and two
  newcomers. Hammer will be primary PR after leading the MAC in PR yardage
  the past two seasons.
- Scouting (opposing MAC assistant): "I thought they were the best team in
  the MAC last year... it's more about the amount of players that they
  lost vs. what they brought in. They're losing a potential first-round
  safety [Emmanuel McNeil-Warren], which always hurts... their tight end
  that they lost, CC Ezirim [WKU], is gonna be a really good player...
  Mike Jacobs has won at every level, and he's done a great job."
- FINAL ANALYSIS: Jacobs 15-1 in SoCon play the past two seasons; Toledo
  hasn't had a losing record in 16 years; staff confident it won't be a
  slow build "despite essentially starting with a brand-new roster."
- Depth chart (bold = returning starter, * = 2026 transfer): OFF — WR
  Hammer/Dabbs*, WR Gilbert*/Bond*, WR Simpson*/Braceful, LT GALES/Hofer,
  LG Harder/Medlock, C Senda*/Rector*, RG Carwise*/D.Johnson*, RT
  Traore/N.Thomas*, TE Newell*/Strickland*, QB Richter/Robinson*, RB
  Miller*/Ison*. DEF — DE Zock*/Z.Johnson*, DT Camese*/Gwinn, DT
  Lambert-Pipkin/Chandler, DE Ollison II/Odinjor*, LB Long/Zay, LB
  Fox*/Jackson*, CB H.Miller/Russell, CB C.Jones*/Petite*, NB
  Conteh*/Hutchinson*, S Thompson/Hohler, S Watkins*/A.Smith*. ST — K
  Griffith*, P Duran, KR Bond*, PR Hammer. Only bold name anywhere: GALES.
- Top recruits: Sharard Vaughn III LB, Jake Palucki IOL, Seth Elchert WR,
  Tyrell O'Neal DL, Ziiyahn Blair S (all 3-star per Rivals).
