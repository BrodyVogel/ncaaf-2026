## THE SHAPE
An 11-4 Playoff team gutted by the NFL draft + portal (17 in / 24 out) — a
"downgrade in every position room aside from the elite secondary" (P6). The offense
is a near-total reset: QB Simpson (1st round) gone with a Mack/5-star Russell battle
to replace him, both top RBs gone, FOUR OL starters gone (Proctor + Brailsford to
the NFL) leaving only Carroll, and the leading receiver (Bernard) + starting TE
(Cuevas, drafted) gone — though the WR duo Coleman-Williams + Brooks returns. The
defense lost all three DL starters and both ILBs, but the ONE strength is elite and
nearly intact: a secondary of All-American S Hubbard + Sabb + CB Zabien Brown +
Dijon Lee (COV p95 in '25). Edge Pierre (former 5-star, returns for his 4th yr)
anchors a rebuilt front seven. A rebuild loaded with 5-star talent but almost no
returning production outside the back four — the swings (QB, OL, DL, ILB) are wide.
