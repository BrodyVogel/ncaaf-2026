## Pick Six 2026 (B12 pp. 5-6 = book 102-103; verbatim)
- 2025 arc: 12-2 (8-1). Returning QB suspended weeks before kickoff -> true FR Bear Bachmeier
  (spring at Stanford) took over; "didn't look or play like a freshman" ("centaur" - opposing HC
  after 69-0). Nation-best 27 straight scoring quarters to open; Holy War walk-in TD carrying
  three Utes. Losses: at Texas Tech + B12 title game (TT); Pop-Tarts Bowl win = 12 wins (5th time
  ever, first vs P4 schedule). Sitake two-year 23-4 = 2nd-best in program history; targeted by
  Penn State, stayed, "significant boost to roster funding."
- RETENTION (the program story): "BYU put their chips on high school recruiting and player
  retention - and they went two for two." Record 8 four-star signees, program-high #21 class
  (#2 B12). "Successfully retained all available returning starters... 17 starters earned some form
  of Big 12 honors - 10 returned, 7 graduated, and zero transferred out." Kept all 10 eligible
  All-B12 players.
- QB: Bachmeier = B12 Freshman of the Year (3,033 + 527 rush, 26 total TD; only true FR with
  3,500+ total yds; 8th B12 QB Rating but top-5 comp% 65 and 7.8 Y/A). "Look for a second-year
  starter bonus... he'll actually get spring ball in Provo this time." Near-5* Ryder Lyons (#5 QB
  2026) signed - ON MISSION this year.
- RB: LJ Martin returns after B12 OFFENSIVE PLAYER OF THE YEAR - league-high 1,305 yds THROUGH a
  shoulder injury (offseason surgery; 100 ypg after it). All-America push; Jamaal Williams career
  record in reach.
- WR/TE: all three All-B12 pass-catchers gone - Roberts + TE Ryan graduated, Kingston EXPELLED
  (felony charge). Phillips + Hagen = "experienced veterans." IN: three former 4*s - Kasper
  (Oregon, 6-6), TE Walker Lyons (USC Top100, co-starter last year), TE Saleapaga (Oregon);
  + Top200 4*s Goettsche and Harris (mission) "to complete the reload at tight end."
- OL: TJ Woods again Top 40 in both P6 OL metrics (#38 Run Push / #18 Pass Pro; 4% sack rate).
  3 starters gone; RETURN all-league pair: Mitchell (1st-team C) + Gentry (RT). Adds w/ starting
  experience: Finau (Washington 4* - named LT after spring), Yamauchi (Stanford), Sia (Utah State).
  "The line is veteran with five redshirt seniors in the two-deep."
- DEF: DC Jay Hill -> Michigan (w/ Whittingham). INTERNAL PROMOTION: Kelly Poppinga ("influences
  from Bronco Mendenhall and Hill... I'd be an idiot to change much"). Consecutive Top-25
  opp-adjusted finishes. "Usually a departing coordinator is able to pull some starters with him,
  but not here" - Poppinga's 12-hr/day phone campaign kept the lineup; six All-B12 defenders back,
  "no significant transfer hits."
- LB: signed the #1 LB transfer - All-ACC Uluave (100 tkl, 12 TFL, "legit All-America candidate");
  Kelly graduated/pro; "do-it-all" Glasker returns -> "top linebacker duo."
- DL: Tanuvasa retained (All-B12, "he elevated everything" - Poppinga); loses starters
  J.Taumoepeau + Lutui, "the rest of the two-deep is back"; Kirkland (former OkSt NT) back off
  redshirt; Schoonover + Akana round out rotation.
- DB: Wall (1st-tm All-B12) graduated. Satuala returns (All-America praise, led team in tackles,
  ISU pick-six); top cover CB Johnson back (tied league lead, 5 INT); Alexander + Damuni back.
  2025 pass D #13 opp-adjusted (strength).
