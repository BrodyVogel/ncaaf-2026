# Michigan 2026 — magazine notes (blinding v2: team-level projections/rankings EXCLUDED)

Sources: Athlon 2026 (p.7 of B10 extract — header-verified); Pick Six 2026 (book pp.
70-71 = pdf 11-12, header-located); Phil Steele 2026 (p.7, text image). Captured
2026-07-16. 2025 (fact): 9-4, 7-2 B10 — losses @OU 13-24, @USC 13-31, OSU 9-27,
Texas 27-41 (Citrus, blown 4Q lead); wins incl. Nebraska 30-27, NW 24-22 (walk-off FG),
Purdue 21-16.

## Coaching — FULL CHANGE
- Sherrone Moore FIRED a week after the OSU loss ("for an affair with a staffer, and
  then arrested and charged for other crimes" — P6). Program also on NCAA probation for
  recruiting violations, fines up to ~$30M (P6).
- NEW HC: KYLE WHITTINGHAM (Utah's HC for 21 years; 177-88; "I still have a lot left
  in the tank"; hired pre-bowl — PS notes the staff transition preceded the Citrus
  loss). Style: "physicality, toughness, and grit" (P6).
- NEW OC: Jason Beck (Utah '25 — led the most-improved offense in FBS: #1 yds/carry
  6.0, #2 rush ypg 266, #31 QB rating; New Mexico OC '24). NEW DC: Jay Hill (BYU
  '23-'25 — #15/#24 in P6's opponent-adjusted rank last two years; Weber State HC
  68-39; Whittingham tree, aggressive 4-2-5, "nickel 90% of snaps"). NEW STC: Kerry
  Coombs. OL coach Jim Harding from Utah ("one of the best" — PS; two 2026
  first-rounders).
- Athlon scout: "I love that roster... four or five players in that freshman class
  who'll contribute immediately... 2027 was the target year... I think they can win
  the conference. Not a group with a lot of household names, but a lot of depth with
  really good players."

## QB
- Underwood RETURNS (the 2025 #1 overall recruit, true-FR starter): 2,428-2,963 range
  by book (PS: 2,428 @ 60.3%, 11-9) + #3 rusher (392 @ 4.5). P6: "limited by a
  conservative gameplan, plenty of drops... but also rookie mistakes"; 9 INT, <100 yds
  passing 3x, #12 B10 QB rating.
- Fit: Beck's scheme is "geared towards dual-threat quarterbacks, and Underwood's best
  games were the ones when he used his legs" (P6). PS: "going from a weakness to a
  potential strength... should be one of the Big Ten's top QB's."
- P6 frames the season: "Michigan's 2026 fate depends on Underwood's development."
- Depth: Fowler-Nicolosi (Colorado State; 3,460 @ 62%, 22-16 in '23 — PS#22), true FR
  Smigiel (PS#11), Herbstreit; Keene -> ASU, Warren -> Stanford, Garcia gone.

## RB
- Marshall RETURNS: All-B10; 932-946 yds (5.1), 10 TD; four straight 100-yd games
  (wks 7-10); "surged late" (P6). Haynes (All-B10; 857 @ 7.1, OFY after USC) ->
  Georgia Tech.
- IN: five-star Savion Hiter (PS#1 RB recruit) — "should get carries right away and
  form a top duo with Marshall" (P6); "looked the part in the spring" (PS). Tatum
  (Oklahoma) added; Kuzdzal (walk-on who had 20-100 vs MD) withdrew from the portal
  and stays. PS: "my #13 rated RB corps."

## WRTE
- Marsh RETURNS: true-FR revelation — 45 rec / 651 yds ALL after September, B10
  honors, "an instant star" (P6); also the KR/PR front-runner (Athlon).
- GONE: McCulley (#2 at 588 @ 15.1 — yr4), Morgan -> UCLA, F.Moore -> Michigan State;
  TEs Bredeson ("do-it-all") + Klein both out (P6).
- IN: JJ Buchanan (Utah — "#5 receiver transfer," started as a FR there) + Top50
  Jaime Ffrench (Texas) + Top200 FR Travis Johnson. "A young room but has sky-high
  potential" (P6). Athlon scout groups Underwood/Marsh/Ffrench/Buchanan as the
  offensive excitement.
- TE: Z.Marshall moves up (16 catches); Hansen behind. Drops were a named 2025
  problem in both books.

## OL
- '25: 210 rush ypg (5.4), 21 sacks, Joe Moore semifinalist despite injuries costing
  8 starts (PS); "four guys earned All-Big Ten honors" (P6).
- RETURN: Sprague (T) + Guarnera (C) — both All-B10 per P6 — plus FR-year LT Frazier
  ("stepped up and thrived" — P6), Link (may shift to G), Efobi, Norton; 5* Babalola
  back from the fall-camp injury.
- GONE: El-Hadi + Crippen (the other two All-B10s). Portal outs: Haywood + Strayhorn
  (both Alabama), C.Jones, Roebuck.
- IN: Ka'aha'aina-Torres (Nebraska); OL coach Harding (Utah). Athlon: "primed to make
  a jump" with four starters back.

## DL
- GONE: both All-B10 linemen — D.Moore (#2 DC DET) + Benny (#7 DC BAL) — plus Guy,
  Payne, T.Williams (5 DL, 34 st, 13 sk — PS); Barham (PFF-classified DL; PS calls
  him LB) also gone.
- IN from Utah: 1st-team ALL-AMERICAN DE John Henry Daley — led the nation in sacks
  (11.5) until his Week 11 injury; "should be back for fall camp" (P6) — and starting
  DT Jonah Lea'ea.
- RETURN: Pierce (Athlon "bullish"), Etta (withdrew from portal, stays — Athlon DT
  starter), Nichols, Brandt; four Top100 FR (T.Davis, Vallejo, N.Marshall '25) +
  five-star Carter Meadows.
- PS: "Whittingham always has a solid D-Line... remain top notch."

## LB
- "A complete restart" (P6): All-B10 trio Rolder (#4 DC DET, #1 tkl), Hausmann (#2
  tkl), Barham ALL gone + "backer of the future" Sullivan -> Oklahoma.
- Pieces: Bowles (PS#7 LB), rFR Owusu-Boateng (Top200), FCS standout Staehling
  (North Dakota State), Aisea Moa (Michigan State), Alford (BYU), Chase Taylor.
- PS: "a solid unit but less experienced"; Athlon: "linebacker is thin"; unit
  "returns 0 starts" (PS).

## DB
- The strength: "fully loaded with an all-league corner pair of Zeke Berry and Jyaire
  Hill joined by Utah's Smith Snowden at the nickel" (P6). Snowden: 2nd-team All-B12,
  4* transfer grade, even took 21 offensive touches at Utah. Athlon: "seasoned and
  full of playmakers."
- Rod Moore (3x captain) back at S — "has not had a full healthy season since 2023"
  (P6); PS: "a year removed from his ACL." Other S: Bracy (Memphis; 10 st, 81 tkl —
  "a disruptive playmaker" Athlon) or near-5* ATH Salesi Moa (10.7 sprinter, WR reps
  in spring — P6).
- GONE: 3+ DBs per PS (the spring exodus ran past the prints: TJ Metcalf, Hillman,
  Sanders, Mangham, Dotson also out per feed). PDR: #42 ('24) -> #39 ('25); PS:
  "should get back to their normal Top 20 finish."
- Berry note: portal-entered, all three books print him returning (adjudicated —
  news.md).

## Specialists
- '25: Zvada fell to 15-22 FG; P netted 38.7; PR -2.25 / KR -4.76; STR fell to #80
  and the ST coach was FIRED (PS). Athlon: "struggled with fundamentals."
- 2026: Zvada GONE. IN: K Butkowski (Pitt — 20-of-23 as a FR), P Brown (UNLV — 41.1
  net), new STC Coombs; Marsh (+ S.Moa/Ffrench options) on returns. PS: "should have
  a Stock Market rise in the STR."
