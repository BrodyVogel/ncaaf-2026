## THE SHAPE
A 2-10 team with a NEW staff and a near-total rebuild (41 in / 43 out). The paradox of
'25: an explosive offense (OFF p68, RUN p99 - P6's #1 P4 rushing behind dual-threat
Taylen Green) that blew four 4th-Q leads, paired with a defense that "couldn't stop
anybody" (DEF p17, COV p7). The rebuild INVERTS that: the good half is gutted - Green
went pro, 1,000-yd RB Washington + WRs Blake/Sharpe + All-SEC TE Rohan Jones (90.3) +
All-SEC LT Carmona all graduated - and reloads with LOWER-tier transfers (Memphis,
Boise, Louisiana). The bad half (defense) gets a higher-upside but unproven P4/AAC
portal transfusion, with All-SEC edge Quincy Rhodes the one real anchor and a genuinely
upgraded secondary (All-AAC CB Johnson). A wide-band bottom-of-the-SEC projection.
