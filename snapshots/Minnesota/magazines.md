# Minnesota 2026 — magazine notes (blinding v2: team-level projections/rankings EXCLUDED)

Sources: Athlon 2026 (p.9 — header-verified); Pick Six 2026 (book pp. 84-85 = pdf
25-26, header-located); Phil Steele 2026 (p.12, text image). Captured 2026-07-16.
2025 (fact): 8-5, 5-4 B10; 7-0 at home (first since 1967); all five league wins vs
teams with losing B10 records (9-46 combined, outgained in four); losses by avg 24
(OSU 3-42, Oregon 13-42, Iowa 3-41); won the Rate Bowl in OT.

## Coaching
- Fleck yr 10 (66-44 here; extension ~$6M/yr; 7-0 in bowls). NO changes: OC Harbaugh
  yr 4, DC Collins yr 2 (Fleck assistant since '17). Full continuity.
- Athlon scout: "They're so consistent... won't be in the bottom half... They
  evaluate really well. They're just like Illinois. Their GM [Gerrit Chernoff] is
  awesome... they know exactly what their wheelhouse is."
- The stunner: Koi Perich — two-time All-B10, homegrown face of the program —
  transferred to OREGON. "There are thousands of FBS transfers each cycle, but this
  one was unbelievable" (P6). Transfer haul in response ranked 10th in B10 (P6).
- Returning production: 71% offense / 65% defense — 7th-highest in FBS (Athlon/ESPN).

## QB
- Lindsey RETURNS (rFR season): 2,382 yds, 63%, 18-6; #11 in B10 QB rating (tied
  Underwood); "exceeded expectations and took care of the ball" (P6); GW drives to
  beat Rutgers + MSU; Fleck: "took really good command of the offense." 6'5" 230;
  "won more games as a frosh than any QB in school history" (PS).
- Second-year emphasis (P6): pre-snap reads, audibles, pocket elusiveness.
- Depth: Merdinger (UNC '24 bowl starter; 2 starts at Liberty '25), Shikenjanski.

  PS — LG Greg Johnson is "my 1st team AA," LT Roy (PS#40) "already on NFL scouts
  radar... this unit will more resemble your typical Fleck offensive line and is one

## DL
- The edge trio: A.Smith — 1st-team All-B10, B10-best 12.5 sacks (first double-digit
  Gopher season since 2009), "dominant bowl game and still opted to return" (PS;
  would break the career record with another 10+) — plus Menz (6.5 sk) and IN TJ
  Bush (Cal's best pass rusher; 13 st, 5.5 sk; 91 pressures/13 sk L3Y — Athlon).
  P6: "one of the best [DE trios] in the Big Ten."
- DT: "a total rebuild in the middle — the top four defensive tackles departing"
  (P6): Eastern + Logan-Redding expired. IN: Crowder (Marshall; 310-320-lb
  "space-eater"; "needs to be an anchor" — Athlon), Chapman (FIU), Kaba (Marshall).
- Context: 35 sacks incl. 9 vs Nebraska (school record) + 7 each vs Rutgers/MSU;
  #4 in P6's Negative Play Rate; but "collapsed in the 38-35 Northwestern loss."
