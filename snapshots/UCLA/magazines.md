# UCLA 2026 — magazine notes (blinding v2: team-level projections/rankings EXCLUDED)

Sources: Athlon 2026 (p.17 — header-verified); Pick Six 2026 (book pp. 86-87 = pdf
27-28, header-located); Phil Steele 2026 (pdf p.16 spread, header-verified).
Captured 2026-07-16.
2025 (fact): 3-9, 3-6 B10. An 0-4 start (43-10 Utah; UNLV; the 35-10 New Mexico
meltdown — "Foster's 50-0 moment" per P6) got Foster FIRED two days after game
three... [fired after the NM loss]. The interim surge: TE coach Jerry Neuheisel
took the play-calling and the Bruins became "the first 0-4 team to beat a Top 10
opponent in 40 years" — 42-37 over then-#7 Penn State ("You'd think it was Mardi
Gras" — interim HC Tim Skipper), then beat MSU 38-13 and Maryland 20-17. Lost the
final five (incl. 56-6 Indiana, 48-10 OSU). Seven losing seasons in ten; Rose Bowl
attendance at a 37,000 average.

## Coaching — FULL CHANGE (x1.13 APPLIES)
- BOB CHESNEY hired after a three-month, season-long search — "one of the top
  prizes in the biggest coaching carousel in college football history" (P6).
  Career 132-52: JMU 21-6 over two years (took over after Cignetti left for
  Indiana with his staff and 20 players), won the Sun Belt, made the CFP as a
  school five years removed from FCS; before that 5 Patriot League titles in 6
  years at Holy Cross; D2 Assumption, D3 Salve Regina.
- He brought BOTH JMU coordinators — OC Dean Kennedy, DC Colin Hitschler (co-DC
  Cincy '22, Wisconsin '23; JMU D was national top-10 in total D, rush D, opp QB
  rating, 3rd downs in '25) — and TEN JMU players. New STC Canan (Holy Cross).
- The context frame: JMU was top-10 in BOTH scoring O and scoring D while UCLA
  was bottom-10 in both (P6).
- Athlon scout: "If I was going to defend Iamaleava, I would... stop him from
  running and make him throw — particularly downfield... without his feet, I'm
  not sure he can sit in the pocket and carve you up... I was blown away with
  what their NIL budget was a few years ago. It was unfathomably low... I've
  heard they've been kind of arrogant on the road with following the Cignetti
  model."
