# Georgia Southern — magazine findings (Athlon + Phil Steele, 2026 editions)

RULES: extract unit FACTS only. G5 = TWO-SOURCE (Athlon + Phil Steele); no Pick Six for the Sun Belt.

## THE SHAPE (2-source consensus)
Clay Helton (no HC change; new DC Mike Mutz from SFA). A 7-6 team that outscored people (OFF p54,
PBLK p91) behind a historically bad defense (DEF p2, dead last in FBS). 2026 loses its two biggest
offensive pieces (RB Arnold, 1,100 yds -> draft; + 4 OL starters to the portal) and resets QB to
veteran journeyman Max Johnson (UNC). Edge MJ Stroud is the lone defensive bright spot on a unit
being rebuilt largely with FCS transfers. Middle-to-lower Sun Belt East.

## Athlon (SBC p5) — depth chart + prose
- Header: Clay Helton. NF 102, Sun Belt East 4. 7-6. NEW DC Mike Mutz (from Stephen F. Austin).
- OFF: QB Max Johnson (24yo journeyman - LSU/A&M/UNC - burning a final year at home) the projected
  starter; frosh Weston Bryan (6 TD/49 snaps) the heir. "Outgoing RB OJ Arnold left a 1,100-yard hole"
  -> a homegrown committee of 3rd-year backs. WR: a fleet of P4 transfers (never lived up to their
  rank) + breakout candidate Josh Dallas (missed nearly all of '25 to a hip injury; 6 TD in '24). OL
  "must replace" its portal losses.
- DEF (last in the SBC in total defense + YPP): headliner All-SBC edge MJ Stroud; established DTs Caleb
  Moore + Latrell Bullard, LB Brandon Tyson, CBs Tracy Hill + Chance Gamble. Most new arrivals are FCS
  (Mutz's SFA/Abilene Christian pipeline): S Aaron Sears (SFA all-conf), LB Rashon Myles (Abilene Christian all-conf).
- ST: K Tripp Bryant (16-16 inside 40, but 2-5 beyond) + a transfer punter (Alex Smith).
- Depth chart returning starters in bold (Tyson, Tracy Hill). Transfers marked *.
