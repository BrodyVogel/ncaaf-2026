# Nebraska 2026 — magazine notes (blinding v2: team-level projections/rankings EXCLUDED)

Sources: Athlon 2026 (p.10 — header-verified); Pick Six 2026 (book pp. 80-81 = pdf
21-22, header-located); Phil Steele 2026 (p.11, text image). Captured 2026-07-16.
2025 (fact): 7-6, 4-5 B10; lost to Michigan + USC in Lincoln by a combined 7; PSU
10-37, Iowa 16-40 (10th loss in 11 yrs to Iowa), bowl loss to Utah 22-44 (outgained
by 200). 30 straight losses to ranked teams (streak predates Rhule).

## Coaching
- Rhule yr 4 (19-19 here). NO HC change. OC Holgorsen CONTINUES (took over late '24).
  NEW DC: Rob Aurich (San Diego State — #97 -> #5 scoring defense in his one DC
  season; Broyles nominee); replaces Butler; scheme shifts 3-3-5 -> attacking 4-2-5.
  NEW OL coach: Geep Wade (Georgia Tech line-builder). NEW co-STCs incl. Brett Maher
  (ex-NFL/Nebraska K); Ekeler left for USC.
- Rhule: "I want player development, I want physicality, I want effort" (P6).
- Athlon scout: "They're in a ton of denial with the quarterback situation... losing
  [Raiola], I thought he was their last hope... I think five wins, maybe six at most
  ... Rhule can make you run through a wall, but there's just always so much turnover
  with his staff."
- Development context (P6): 3 NFL draft picks 2024-26 (2nd-fewest in B10) despite
  Top-25 classes; Rhule 2-10 in November here; empty spring game noted by the beat.

## QB
- GONE: Raiola -> OREGON (2,000 yds @ 72%, 19-5, OFY game 9 — "shocked Husker
  Nation"). Lateef (true FR) led the UCLA win, lost his last 3 starts (904 @ 60.2%,
  5-1) — RETURNS as the #2.
- IN: Anthony Colandrea (UNLV) — MOUNTAIN WEST PLAYER OF THE YEAR: 3,459 @ 65.9%,
  23-5 (PS) + 649 rush / 10 rush TD (P6). Prior: 11 starts at Virginia with a 26-20
  ratio. P6: "gunslinger... highlight-reel scrambles and off-balance throws but will
  also turn it over (29 picks and 17 fumbles over three seasons)." Holgorsen studied
  Diego Pavia off-script film for the fit; the mobility "adds an element missing from
  Raiola's skillset" and should ease the protection woes.
- Kaelin also back (was contending at Virginia; returned). "Depth is great" (PS).

## RB
- GONE: Emmett Johnson — 2nd-team ALL-AMERICAN (PS): 1,451 @ 5.8 on a 251-carry
  workload + 370 receiving; went pro (#5 DC KC). "Leaves behind a wide-open room
  which Rhule decided not to supplement from the portal" (P6).
- The room: Nelson (147 @ 5.4; 88 in the bowl), Mozee (converted WR, 115 @ 4.4),
  Ives (power back, hamstring-limited), true FR Rule (PS#46; "home run ability";
  75-yd spring TD). PS: "they won't match Johnson's production."

## WRTE
- RETURN: Hunter (617 @ 14.3 — team lead; "could push for All-Big Ten" — P6) +
  Barney (55 rec as a FR in '24; 484 @ 10.8 in '25) + TE Lindenmeyer (29/312 — HM
  All-B10). Clark: "breakout potential" (Athlon).
- GONE: Key (452-587 @ ~13 by book; yr4). IN: Gilmer (UCLA starter; 12 st, 535).
  P6: "the starting trio are experienced former four-stars."

## OL
- '25: collapsed in pass-pro OUTCOMES — #112 sack rate; 7 sacks to Michigan, 9 to
  Minnesota (school record vs them), 5 to MSU — but just 12 sacks in the other 10
  games (PS). Injury chaos again: Prochazka OFY after 5 starts, Gottula OFY after 4.
- GONE: 4 OL (30 gm/117 career starts — Lutovsky, Spindler, Corcoran, Prochazka);
  Ka'aha'aina-Torres -> Michigan.
- RETURN: C Justin Evans + 5* LT Pritchett ("developing into a pro prospect" — P6);
  Gottula back from OFY.
- IN (+123 career starts — PS): Black (Iowa State; 30 starts; 4* transfer grade),
  Babalade (South Carolina; 15 starts; 4*), Mubenga (LSU; 11 starts) — all three
  familiar to new line coach Wade. PS: "they can only stay healthier... Rhule says
  they are significantly more talented."

## DL
- 2025: allowed 175 (4.8) rush with just 22 sacks; #114 vs the run (P6). "From a
  strength to a question mark" (PS).
- GONE: Jeudy + Davis (BOTH to Miami — P6; feed shows Davis portal/Jeudy yr4),
  McCullough (JLB; 7 st).
- RETURN: former five-star Nwaneri — "should emerge" (PS, ranks him among his top
  DEs); Lenhardt; Van Poppel. IN: Whittington (Pitt — "breakout freshman"),
  Stoudmire (BC starter), A.Jones (UCLA), V.Evans (FIU — listed CB in feed, DB).
- Athlon: "Improvement along the defensive line... is paramount"; adds "expected to
  add instant pass rush" for a sub-2-sacks/gm defense.

## LB
- GONE: 3 of the top 4 — Wright (#1 tkl), Watson-Trent (SBC Def POY import, #7 tkl),
  McCullough. RETURN: Shavers (61 tkl, 7.5 TFL through injuries; Blackshirt).
- IN: Chambliss (San Diego State — 110 tackles UNDER AURICH; 1st-team All-MW in this
  exact scheme), Foster (Oregon State; 11 st, 95 tkl L2Y), Hawthorne (Iowa State).
  Aurich wanted bigger LBs — both adds are 6'3 (P6). PS: "the top unit of the
  defense... bigger and more experienced."

## DB
- 2025: #3 nationally in pass ypg; #28 in P6's adjusted pass number; PDR #30 (PS).
- GONE: two All-B10 DBs (C.Wright, Singleton — 17 combined starts at S per PS);
  Hartzog -> Arizona; Buford expired.
- RETURN: CB Marshall (the returning All-B10 — P6), D.Jones (moves to the new
  nickel), Guthrie (started the last 8 at S; #3 tackler), Conn (starter at the other
  S — P6).
- IN: McDougle — Aurich's OWN starting safety from SDSU; five-star FR Odem (the
  only blue-chipper on the two-deep) battles Charles at CB2. PS: "my #15 rated unit."
- Scheme-change caveat named by P6.

## Specialists
- '25 STR #18 — highest since 2014 (PS). K Cunanan RETURNS (16-19, long of 52);
  ambipedal Aussie P Wilson RETURNS (39.4 / 38.3 net). LS Wills in (MSU).
- GONE: KR K.Williams (95-yd TD; 32.9 avg) -> MSU; STC Ekeler -> USC (Maher takes
  over). Barney handles KR/PR per Athlon. PS: "could drop some after the large rise."
