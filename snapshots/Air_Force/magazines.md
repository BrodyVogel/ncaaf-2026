## Athlon (p.3)
- 2025: 4-8 (3-5). Tight losses: at Navy 31-34, at UNLV 48-51, Army
  17-20; W Wyoming 24-21, at SJSU 26-16, at CSU 42-21.
- Calhoun yr 20 (139-97). OC Mike Thiessen; DC Steve Russ.
- QB Szarka (Jr, BOLD): 10 games before BREAKING HIS WRIST; led the MWC
  with 13 rush TD; 2nd in league at 92.2 rush ypg; 9-5 TD-INT; first
  three career starts = 200+ pass AND 110+ rush each (best 3-game QB
  start in program history). "Two more years."
- FB Owen Allen returns — five 100-yard games.
- OL: lost two All-MWC players; two full-time starters back + Vonderhaar
  ("extensive experience"). "Expect the Falcons to reload."
- WR = biggest question: Dawson leading returner (12 rec); 80% run rate.
- DEFENSE: 10th in league total D, 30.3 ppg — "young players took their
  lumps"; now "almost the entire back eight intact." MLB Fletcher:
  2nd-Tm All-MWC, team-high 108 tackles (BOLD). Daley + Hubert (BOLD)
  flank him — "very solid linebacking corps." S Roger Jones Jr. HM
  All-MWC (BOLD); CBs Seiken (BOLD) + K.Johnson "tested"; S Mustell 3
  INTs as a true FR (BOLD-listed). "Graduation wiped out the defensive
  line, and Air Force does not take transfers — inexperienced up front."
- ST: K Medina lost the job after missing half of six FG attempts; solid
  spring, No. 1 again. P Will Duncan new. KR/PR Matt Long.
- Scouting (opposing MWC assistant): "Really cool that they don't do the
  transfer portal... if you turn it over against them it'll kill ya —
  possession-based team... They recruit a specific type of kid."
- Depth chart (bold=ret): OFF (3) — WR Dawson/Hrynciw, WR Long/Hayes,
  LT ELWOOD/Nazworth, LG Tarascio/FALK, C Varela/Thomas, RG Ashford/
  Smith, RT Vonderhaar/Roser, TE Kibbee/Stalfort, QB SZARKA/Johnson,
  FB Allen/LaDue, TB Conti/Turner. DEF (5) — DE Forsythe/Reese, NG
  B.Williams/Oquendo, DT Barnett/Tillett, OLB HUBERT/Vasek, MLB
  FLETCHER/Fisher, OLB Daley/Canosa, CB K.Johnson/Drain, CB SEIKEN/
  McIntosh, NB Perez/Malbranche, SS R.JONES/Hendrix, FS Paterson/
  MUSTELL. ST — K Medina, P Duncan, KR/PR Long.
- 2026 schedule: Duquesne, North Dakota St, at Nevada, Navy, at NIU,
