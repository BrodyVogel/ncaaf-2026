## Athlon (p.8)
- 2025: 3-9 (2-6 MAC), farewell MAC season; 132nd nationally in total
  offense. Results: W Holy Cross 19-17, L at Maryland 9-20, L at Miss St
  10-38, L San Diego St 3-6, L Miami (OH) 14-25, L at EMU 10-16, L at
  Ohio 21-48, W Ball St 21-7, L at Toledo 3-42, W at UMass 45-3, L WMU
  19-35, L Kent St 31-35.
- HC Thomas Hammock left in FEBRUARY to join the Seattle Seahawks staff;
  INTERIM HC Rob Harley for 2026 (first season; Athlon lists him as DC
  too). OCs Tony Petersen + Quinn Sanders.
- QB three-way "that will influence their offensive philosophy": Davidson
  (So — 2 true-FR starts, pocket passer, 27 att/start), Macon (Sr —
  run-first, closed 2025 as starter, "explosive with the ball," few
  passes), Hamric (D2 Charleston — dual threat, 155 pass + 61 rush ypg,
  led his team to a league title).
- RB: Telly Johnson Jr. top back — 5.7 ypc (2nd in MAC), 80-yd run vs
  EMU. Porter backup.
- WR: DeAree Rogers (46-506) = No. 1 target (BOLD returning starter);
  Rickey Taylor Jr. missed half of 2025 hurt but team-best 14.9 ypr; adds
  Kevin Holmes Jr.*, Cam Thompson*, Tavaris Coles*.
- OL "will be young": C Landon Hron + LT Ross Liegel (both BOLD) "the
  only experienced starters"; RG Ty Mead* transfer.
- DEFENSE (0) returning starters. Secondary = "best position group": JC
  transfers Justin Harris* + Tyrese Stanley* the safety tandem; Dev'ion
  Reynolds one corner; Yassine Falke* (NAIA Marian, all-conference)
  expected to make an impact. 4-2-5: Broderick Windham MOVES S->LB;
  Cooper Mau* vs Kimar Nelson (came on strong late as a true FR) at the
  other spot. DL counting on Amari Washington (DT, "significant
  progress... coaches excited") and Jacob McGhee* (D2 Lincoln MO).
- SPECIALISTS: P Jake Ference back (shared duties, 43.7 avg); Rogers PR,
  Reynolds KR. K = JJ Schlenhardt (Fr) on Athlon's chart.
- FINAL ANALYSIS: "limped through 3-9... moving to the Mountain West...
  interim head coach... questions at quarterback and youth along both
  lines, it projects to be a difficult season."
- Scouting (opposing MWC assistant): "more coaches [like Hammock] leaving
  to take NFL opportunities... Schematically they'll have a little
  adjustment to the Mountain West because there are more spread-out
  offenses than in the MAC... wild elevation places... but they've got
  good coaches over there, so they'll figure it out and adapt."
- Depth chart (bold = returning starter, * = 2026 transfer): OFF (4) — WR
  ROGERS/Thompson*, WR Holmes*/Bryant, WR Taylor/Coles*, LT
  LIEGEL/Gustafson, LG Skartvedt/Rumohr, C HRON/Gross, RG Mead*/Senenat,
  RT Mahnesmith/Welch, TE Fowler/Stein, QB Davidson/Macon, RB
  JOHNSON/Porter. DEF (0) — DE J.Thomas/Bradley*, DT McGhee*/Scarlett,
  DT A.Washington/Canion, DE Nnamuchi*/Smith-Davis*, MLB Mau*/Nelson,
  WLB Windham/Kerperin*, CB Reynolds/M.Washington*, CB Falke*/Lindo*,
  NB Warren*/Jones*, S Harris*/Armstrong, S Stanley*/Conyers*. ST — K
  Schlenhardt, P Ference, KR Reynolds, PR Rogers.
- 2026 schedule: at Iowa, Illinois St, at Arizona, at Georgia St, Air
  Force, at Wyoming, Hawai'i, at UNLV, at San José St, Nevada, at North
  Dakota St, UTEP.
