# Arkansas State — magazine findings (Athlon + Phil Steele, 2026 editions)

RULES: extract unit FACTS only. G5 = TWO-SOURCE (Athlon + Phil Steele); no Pick Six for the Sun Belt.

## THE SHAPE (2-source consensus)
Butch Jones (no HC change; new OC Garrett Altman ex-Vanderbilt). A 7-6 team (Xbox Bowl win) with a
strong returning skill/OL core + elite special teams, but a QB reset (Raynor -> Iowa State; 4-man
competition led by Texas transfer Trey Owens) and a near-total DL rebuild (lost best D player Sigler
to Auburn). A middle-tier Sun Belt West profile.
