## Pick Six 2026 (B12 pp. 100-101)
- 2025 arc (historical): 12-2, 8-1; first outright conference title in 70 years (34-7 over BYU
  in the title game); 11 regular-season wins all by 20+ (only 2018 Bama and 2019 Ohio State had
  done that in the Playoff era); Playoff debut = 23-0 shutout loss to Oregon off the bye.
  Beat #14 Utah 34-10 in the opener (Whittingham: "never would have believed in a million years
  that we would lose the line of scrimmage"). All seven front-seven starters earned All-B12.
- SORSBY RESOLVED: "the staff signed the #2 rated transfer quarterback this cycle, five-star
  Brendan Sorsby (Cincinnati). With an ongoing legal battle, IN JUNE HIS COLLEGE CAREER ENDED
  and he will prepare for the pros." -> Sorsby NEVER PLAYS for TT.
- QB: Will Hammond (Top200) = the guy: career 1,151 yds/9 TD; led four scoring drives to beat
  Utah in relief, 2 rush TD vs Kansas, 64-yd run in the opener; dual-threat. "Still recovering
  from an ACL injury but with Sorsby gone, the timeline has been moved up to a full return in
  mid-August, just in time for kickoff." + Kirk Francis (Tulsa's 2024 starter) + 4* FR Lloyd
  Jones (WVU game reps).
- RB: "one-two punch returns" - 1,000-yarder Cameron Dickey (263 vs KSU) + explosive J'Koby
  Williams; both All-B12; combined 60 catches/600+ yards; Joyner (USC 4*, '25 transfer) back
  healthy - "somehow they retained all three."
- WR: Douglas + Virgil gone (league top-16 yardage); Eakin returns; adds = two-year starters
  Kenny Johnson (Pitt) + Top200 Malcolm Simmons (Auburn); 5* Micah Hudson "breakout potential";
  Top100 FR Chase Campbell + three more 4*s. "Plenty of firepower here yet again."
- TE: All-B12 Terrance Carter returns (2nd in league receiving among TEs); Jett Carpenter
  (Nevada) All-Mountain West, led his team in catches/yards.
- OL: '25 "strong enough to win the conference, but on the Playoff tier a limiting factor"
  (#60 Run Push / #51 Pass Pro; vs Oregon 1 yd/carry outside one run, 4 sacks). 2026: three
  All-B12 starters back (LT Sampson, C Wilson, RT Ponton) + 4* transfers Church (Louisville) +
  Zambrano at guard, 5* Ojo + Top100 Gilmore at tackle. "Upgraded for 2026."
- DEF 2025: DC Shiel Wood (hired from Houston) -> P6's #1 opponent-adjusted defense; nine
  All-B12 starters, five first-team; Bailey + Rodriguez unanimous All-Americans. Rodriguez:
  117 tkl, 11 TFL, 7 FF, 4 INT, 2 FR, 2 rush TD - Butkus winner, 5th in Heisman.
- DL: Rodriguez + FOUR drafted DL gone (Bailey, Height, Hunter, Gill-Howard - "legendary").
  McGuire: no exact replacements but "four future NFL draft picks" added: Ibirogba (Wake,
  top-rated 4* transfer) + Butler (Washington) at NT; 1st-team all-league G5 edges Trick
  (Miami OH) + Trey White (San Diego State). A.J. Holmes returns (All-B12 accolades);
  Cofield back in the two-deep; 5* FR edge Guyton.
- LB: Roberts returns (B12 title game MVP, 2 INTs in it); "Tech signed the #2 linebacker
  transfer, two-time All-Big 12 Austin Romaine (Kansas State)." Platt (Houston) "high upside
  but has missed two seasons with injury" [P6 wording; PS/PFF show 12 gm, 40 tkl, 357 snaps
  at Houston in '25 - P6 line refers to earlier years; tape controls].
- DB: FOUR starters return - 1st-team All-B12 CB Brice Pollock + Amier Boyd corner pair,
  LB/S hybrid John Curry, S Brenden Jordan; Top200 Peyton Morgan "spring hero and ballhawk"
  earns the open safety spot.
- Schedule note (fact): avoids BYU and Utah; four toughest B12 opponents visit Lubbock.
