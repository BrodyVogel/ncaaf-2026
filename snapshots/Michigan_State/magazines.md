# Michigan State 2026 — magazine notes (blinding v2: team-level projections/rankings EXCLUDED)

Sources: Athlon 2026 (p.8 — header-verified); Pick Six 2026 (book pp. 92-93 = pdf
33-34, header-located); Phil Steele 2026 (p.19, text image). Captured 2026-07-16.
2025 (fact): 4-8, 1-8 B10 — 3-0 start (42-40 2OT over BC), then 8 straight league
losses (first five by double digits; OT loss at Minnesota 20-23; 17-20 Iowa), closed
by beating Maryland 38-28. "The 38-13 loss to a 1-4 UCLA team was the point of no
return for the Smith era" (P6).

## Coaching — FULL CHANGE
- Jonathan Smith FIRED after year two. NEW HC: PAT FITZGERALD (Northwestern 2006-22,
  110-101; two division titles; fired in the hazing scandal aftermath, "eventually
  vindicated with a wrongful termination lawsuit and settlement" — P6; two years
  studying NIL/portal/analytics). "Toughness was born here in Michigan State
  football."
- NEW OC: Nick Sheridan (Alabama '25; Indiana under Cignetti before). DC JOE ROSSI
  RETAINED (continuity mitigant) + Max Bullough added (co-DC — Athlon). NEW STC:
  LeVar Woods — the Iowa special-teams architect.
- $400M donor commitment ("For Sparta") — future-cycles context (P6).
- Transfer strategy: "college production over potential" — first MSU class with more
  non-P4 than P4 transfers; 27 transfers, ZERO four-stars; class ranked outside the
  national Top 50, 14th of 18 in the B10 (P6). PS: 43 players left via the portal.
- Athlon scout: "Their entire staff of position coaches made me go 'Oh, s—.'...
  I don't think they added a lot of players that change the game... but the culture
  wasn't great. Maybe... completely hitting the reset button is actually a good
  thing. I'm pretty bullish on them." Names OL coach Tabacca + safeties coach James
  Adams ("the best I've ever been around").

## QB
- Chiles (benched, then injured) -> Northwestern. MILIVOJEVIC is the guy: took the
  final four starts — 1,267 yds total (64.2%, 10-3 per PS; Athlon: 986 across the 4
  starts), "his QB Rating would have placed ninth in the Big Ten" (P6); led three
  late drives vs NEB/IND/MICH before starting (PS).
- The flag (Athlon): 25 SACKS in his four starts — "he'll need a quicker trigger and
  help from his offensive line."
- Depth: Fancher (28 career starts across Marshall/FAU/UCF — PS; "will push
  Milivojevic"), true FR Coffman (PS#25).

## RB
- GONE: Frazier (520 @ 4.5, team lead) -> Ole Miss; Tau-Tolliver (428 @ 5.9) expired.
- RETURN: Tullis (301 @ 4.4, short-yardage); Clarizio back from the injured year
  (PS#39 FR, ex-Bama commit).
- IN: Edwards (UConn — PS: rushed 1,240 @ 5.9; Athlon: 1,240 scrimmage = 13th FBS,

  Parrish (WKU; 576 @ 5.4; CUSA All-FR), K.Williams (Nebraska; KR). P6: "a deep
  room"; PS: "should easily top the last 2 years' production."

## WRTE

  WR), Kelly (1st-tm CUSA import who led '25), TE Velling (1st-tm P12 import) — plus
  TE Masunas -> Texas.

- IN: KK Smith (Notre Dame) + F.Moore (Michigan) — "high-potential players stuck in

  "tight end-quarterback-receiver-magician."

## OL
- '25: injury-shuffled ("only 3 OL started more than 5 games"), 123 rush (3.8), 37
  sacks allowed (PS). This year they lose SEVEN OL (38 career starts) incl. Gulbin
  (the best tape) and Ramil -> Auburn.
- RETURN: Conner Moore — the only returning offensive starter on the team (T).
- IN (5 transfers, 72 career starts — PS): Fraley (NDSU — FCS All-American and FCS
  RIMINGTON winner, C), Murawski (UConn, "highly-touted"), Sharpe (South Carolina),
  Wright (Georgia Southern). Fitzgerald: the group that "improved most in the
  spring... all 5 OL have all-conference potential" (PS, coach-attributed).
