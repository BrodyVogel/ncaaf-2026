## Pick Six 2026 (B12 pp. 11-12 = book 108-109; verbatim)

  6-1 in them (3 Sanchez game-winners; "gutsy" Weigman). P6's own retrospective: #7 B12 Game
  Grader, #12 opp-adjusted yardage margin - "not as strong as their record... 6-1 in one-score
  games is unlikely to be repeated" (context fact + opinion, recorded for variance).
- Program model (attributed): funding disadvantage -> "moneyball" ("we have to be right" - Fritz);
  2025 haul produced 13 starters, 8 All-B12. 2026: only TWO starters lost to portal; doubled down
  on non-AQ all-conference adds; still national top-10 in career snaps.
- RECRUITING BOMB: signed 5-star Keisean Henderson (Spring, TX) - #1 OVERALL prospect in the
  nation. "Future of the program"; Fritz didn't rule out 2026 game reps; Weigman: "the talent is
  off the charts."
- QB: Weigman back - 2,711 (P6) / 2,705 (Athlon/PS), 25 TD, #5 B12 QB Rating + 700 rush/11 TD on
  ~13 carries/gm.
- RB: Connors (All-B12 HM, just shy of 1,000) graduated. Butler (300), Sanford (2024 leading
  rusher, back from ACL), + MAKHI HUGHES (Oregon): 1,378 + 1,401 at Tulane FOR FRITZ; 1,000+ of
  it after contact; "if he can get back to 2024 form, Houston has a star."
- WR/TE: targets funneled to two players (Thomas + Koziol = 141 rec; all others 45). Thomas
  (966/12, 1st-tm All-B12) returns in slot; Koziol (1st-tm; FBS TE-high 74 catches) pro. Young
  (8 starts) returns outside; adds Walker (OSU: 1,714 L2Y), Turner (TT), Tounkara (Florida), 4*
  Bushnell. OVERMYER (UTSA) takes the Koziol role - "one of the most productive TEs in the AAC."
- OL: #106 P6 Run Push; lose Merryman, Brooks, Hunter (-> Colorado). Return Ebosele + Agnello.
  Adds: three all-league non-AQ linemen - Boswell (Toledo, All-MAC), Terrill (Miami OH, All-MAC),
  Hurst (Tulane, 1ST-TEAM All-AAC at guard) - "all earned three-star transfer grades."
- OC Nagle yr 2: offense bottom-5 ('24) -> #35/68 P4 ('25); "second-year bonus at coordinator
  and quarterback."
- DEF: after Shiel Wood left for TT (post-'24), slid #24 -> #52 of 68. Six All-B12 defenders -
  five gone. DL: lose run-stopper Allen + sack leader Walls; return Laufau + edge hybrids Mack +
  L.McCutchin; add Porter (Oregon 4*), Egodogbare (Yale All-Ivy), De'Marion Thomas.
- LB: Garner grad + Platt -> TT. Add Yates (Ole Miss, 9 starts; 115 tkl at Marshall '24 -
  "100-Yard Landlord"); Fotu back at MLB; Bryant/Glass up.
- DB: THREE returning starters (James all-league CB, Webb + Allen at safety) but lose All-B12
  duo L.McCutchin + W.Brown. Adds: White (Tulane, All-AAC - nickel), Mayo (SFA standout, played
  well vs UH in the opener), 4* Melvin may play early (two-deep thinned by graduations).

## Phil Steele 2026 (B12 PDF p6 = book ~198; VISUAL page-read)
- QB: Weigman 2705 (65%, 25-9) after A&M (15 gm, 2694, 19-7 career there); "runs 21 mpg";
  adds Henderson (PS#1) + Carney (Syracuse). "One of the Big 12's top QBs... talented room with
  a high collective football IQ."
- RB: 2025: Connors 977 (4.9) + #3 receiver; Butler #2 (301, 4.4); Sneed injured wk 6. Hughes:
  just 70 yds at Oregon LY. "3-headed monster in the backfield that can all block well."
- WR: 2024 baseline was dire (top 4 = 1,194 combined); Thomas arrived from UAB (670 in '24) ->
  966 (14.4); Koziol 74 rec/727 (#5 DC JAX); Stephon Johnson OFY after 6 games (275 at 21.2) -
  gone (yr-4). Walker: 823 (12.1) at OSU LY. "Just as solid... more depth and playmaking options
  than in recent seasons."
- OL: 2024: 135 (3.8) w/ 33 sacks ("weak protection" behind the 9-17 ratio); 2025 added 5
  transfers (92 career starts, avg 311 lb) - "most improved unit" (Fritz) -> 178 (4.2), 19 sacks.
  2026: return 2 starters + add Toledo (1st-tm MAC) / Tulane (1st-tm AAC) / Miami OH starters -
  "should continue to improve... much more depth and veteran leadership."
- DL: 2021 "Sack Avenue" had 44 sacks - "not been the same since." 2025: 129 (3.7), 28 sacks.
  2026: lose Allen (13 st, #1 tklr) + Walls (13 st, 7 sk) - "their top two DL... need that crop
  of big DL to emerge."
- LB: 2025 slipped to 15 ypg below avg (#60, from #12 in '24); lose #2/#8/#9/#14 tacklers (20
  starts). Adds Yates + NB White "who started for playoff teams last year and are actually more
  experienced and stronger. The first 3 are rock solid but the team is still looking for a
  fourth ILB to emerge."
- DB: PDR arc #115('23) -> #39('24) -> #42('25). 2026: lose CB McCutchin (13 st, 9 pbu) "but
  will move up in the PDR with 3 starters back. Fritz really likes his starters and feels good
  about depth on the back end."
- ST: Dougherty (Aussie lefty) 42.0 (39.2 net) in '24; STR #84 -> #88. 2026: lose K Sanchez
  (21-26, L52) + RM Mews - "may take a step back in the STR."
- Coaches: Fritz resume (Sam Houston B2B FCS titles, GaSouthern SBC title yr 1, Tulane 12-2 '22
  Cotton Bowl champs = biggest single-season turnaround in FBS history, 11-1 '23); OC Nagle yr 2
  (Tulane asst '16-'23); DC Armstrong yr 2 (age 31; Florida DC '23-'24); STC Couch yr 2 (Florida).
