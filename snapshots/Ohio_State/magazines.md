# Ohio State 2026 — magazine notes (blinding v2: team-level projections/rankings EXCLUDED)

Sources: Athlon 2026 (p.12 — header-verified); Pick Six 2026 (book pp. 64-65 = pdf
5-6, header-located); Phil Steele 2026 (p.3, text image). Captured 2026-07-16.
2025 (fact): 12-2, 9-0 B10 — beat preseason-#1 Texas 14-7 and Michigan 27-9 (snapped
the 4-game streak); #1 scoring defense in the CFP era (9.3 ppg, 219 ypg); LOST the
B10 title game to Indiana 10-13 and the CFP quarterfinal to Miami 14-24 (combined 10
sacks allowed, 2.1 ypc in those two).

## Coaching
- Day yr 8 (82-12; extension). DC Matt Patricia YR 2 (retained — built the CFP-era's
  best defense in year one). NEW OC: ARTHUR SMITH (ex-Falcons HC / Steelers OC) with
  Keenan Bailey co-OC — the two-NFL-coordinator model an opposing coach calls "so
  innovative... schematic dudes that know what the f— they're doing" (Athlon scout).
- Draft context: 11 more picks in April (after 14 the year before — 4 first-rounders
  twice); "first school with 7+ drafted in the first two rounds in consecutive
  drafts... first since 1967 with four of the top 11 picks" (P6). A program-record
  51 newcomers on the roster (Athlon).

## QB
- SAYIN RETURNS (yr 2): Heisman finalist; NCAA regular-season RECORD 78.4%
  completion rate; 3,610, 32-8 (P6/PS); Shaun Alexander FR of the Year; beat AP-#1
  Texas in his first start (first QB to do that since 1984).
- The named flaw (P6): off-script creation — "unable to create when the pocket broke
  down" vs Indiana/Miami (critical first-half INTs); "needs to create plays with his
  legs in crunch time... the Craig Krenzel type plays."
- PS: "held under wraps in some games as a rFr, but will probably top 4,000 yards."
- Depth: Kienholz -> Louisville; IN J.Martin (Maryland; PS#20); St. Clair (PS#3 '25
  recruit) develops.

## RB
- Bo Jackson RETURNS off a "surprise 1,000-yard freshman season" (P6): 1,090 @ 6.1,
  six 100+ games, "the most explosive back" (PS). West (310 @ 5.3) "borderline
  sensational" (Athlon, w/ Jackson).
- GONE: Donaldson (330 @ 3.7 short-yardage, yr4), Peoples (344 @ 5.6) -> Penn State.
- IN: Ja'Kobi Jackson (Florida; 509 @ 5.4 in '24); rFr Rogers (PS#6) returns; Top100
  ATH Legend Bey — Day compares him to Tyreek Hill/Curtis Samuel; slot/H-back
  do-it-all (P6). New OC Arthur Smith's "power-running game" trademark (Athlon).
- PS: "more experienced and loaded."

## WRTE
- JEREMIAH SMITH RETURNS for a final season: unanimous All-American; 1,243 yds, 12
  TD; "many believe the best overall player in college football" (P6); "on pace to
  break all school receiving records."
- GONE: Tate (SELECTED FOURTH OVERALL); 5*s Graham + Porter -> Notre Dame ("likely
  due to the breakout of five-star Chris Henry, who surged all the way to the #2
  role in spring" — P6); Rodgers -> USF.
- RETURN: Inniss (5*) in the starting trio; Henry the #2. IN: McCuin (UTSA — All-AAC,
  65/726), Parker (LSU); FR Boyd "big spring reviews."
- TE: BOTH All-B10 TEs gone (Klare, Kacmarek). IN: all-leaguer Welcing
  (Northwestern), All-MAC M.Williams (Ohio); Top200 Roberts "the spring hero" (P6).

## OL
- FOUR starters back, ALL FOUR All-B10, 89-91 combined starts — "one of the most
  veteran lines in America" (P6); "a clear Joe Moore Award contender" (PS): LT/T
  Siereveld (did NOT allow a sack all year — "their best shot at an All-America
  season"), C Hinzman, G Montgomery, T Daniels.
- GONE: Tshabola -> Kentucky ("after losing his starting job" — P6).
- The 5th spot: Padilla vs VanSickle vs "spring hero" Ian Moore — all former Top200s.
- The caveat (P6): "strong in the regular season... then struggled against the elite
  lines of Indiana and Miami."

## DL
- GONE: 1st-tm AA DT McDonald (65 tkl, 9 TFL; rd-2 HOU), DE Curry (11 sk, 16.5 TFL;
  rd-5 IND... P6 says "led with 11 sacks"), DT Malone, + Atkinson expired; 8 backup
  DL hit the portal (PS); Hicks -> USF, Carter -> Miami.
- RETURN: Jackson Jr. — "the only returning starter on the line" (P6); 5* Houston
  "ready for a breakout season at tackle"; W.Smith Jr., Grady.
- IN: BOTH Alabama linemen — DT J.Smith + DE Russaw (former 5*s, 4* transfer grades)
  — plus Top100 UCF starter Walker. Larry Johnson still develops the room (Athlon).
- PS: "loaded with 4th and 5th year players and, of course, talent."

## LB
- GONE: ALL THREE starters — two All-American FIRST-ROUNDERS Reese (#1 tkl, 6.5 sk)
  + S.Styles (#1 tkl) — plus NB L.Styles (rd-5). "Even the greatest reloading
  programs would struggle here" (P6).
- Pierce steps into the top role (43 tkl; PS#14); IN Alliegro (Wisconsin starter —

  Johnson (#1 LB of 2026).
- PS: "they remain solid, but are less experienced and a concern."

## DB
- GONE: Downs (a top-7 pick; "one of their best safeties of all-time" — P6) +
  1st-tm B10 CB Igbinosun (rd-2 BUF) + NB L.Styles; portal: Delane -> LSU, Scott ->
  Oregon, West -> Wisconsin, Hartford -> UCLA, Haddad -> Iowa.
- RETURN: McClain ("star potential" — P6), Mathews ("welcoming his new role as the
  top corner" — Athlon), 5* SOPH Sanchez ("poised to be yet another Buckeye great").
- IN: a pair of All-ACC safeties — Little (FSU; 2nd-tm ACC '24, 70 tkl) + T.Moore
  (Duke; 2nd-tm ACC '24) — plus Calhoun (Bama), Kelly (UGA); 5* FR Timmons + Top100
  Bradford.
- PS: "may drop some... CB Devin Sanchez could be that next #1 DC." PDR #15 in '25.

## Specialists
- K Fielding GONE (16-20; misses vs Indiana and Miami — the named postseason cost).
  IN: Hawkins (Baylor — "top notch," PS). P McGuire RETURNS (42.1/41.2 net on just
  29 punts; Aussie). 61 TB on 86 KO last year (Fielding's KOs also leave).
- Returns: Inniss + FR Bey. Athlon: "ill-timed missed kicks, lackluster punt returns
  and inconsistent kick coverage have haunted OSU"; PS STR #36.
