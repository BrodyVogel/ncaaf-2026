## Athlon (SBC p9) — depth chart + prose
- Header: Ricky Rahne, 30-33 (5 yrs). NF 94, Sun Belt East 2. 10-3, 6-2 SBC (incidentally visible - NOT used).
- OFF: "The Monarchs matched their best season in program history in 2025. But the core of that roster - on
  offense - scattered when the portal opened. QB Colton Joseph, the Sun Belt Offensive POY, was the most
  high-profile departure." The Cure Bowl (a win over USF) "provided a glimpse of 2026" - QB Quinn Henicle
  (his 2nd career start) + RB Devin Roche each ran for 100+. Added Jaxon Potter (Washington State transfer)
  "but it looks like Henicle's job to lose." Henicle "takes the reins of an offense breaking in FIVE new
  starters along the line and a revamped receiver room" - transfers JJ Harrell (Holmes CC) + Zion Agnew
  (D2 Lenoir-Rhyne).
- DEF: "ODU's defense was lights-out last season - held 8 opponents to 10 or fewer, allowed only 57 across a
  6-game closing win streak. With STARTERS BACK AT EVERY LEVEL, that unit should again be among the SBC's
  best. Safety + top tackler Mario Easterly returns along with run-stuffing DL Chris Forbes, both All-Sun
  Belt. LB saw several departures, so they brought in transfer Montrell Chapman (Mississippi State) to man
  the middle. Few teams created havoc at the rate ODU did, but some of the most productive players from a
  unit that finished top 10 nationally in sacks/turnovers are no longer there."
- ST: "Nathanial Eichner took over kicking in November (7-of-10 FG, 19-of-19 XP the rest of the way); ODU
  brought in FAU transfer Andrew Nelms to compete. Ian Brandt is back at punter (~41 avg, low end). Roche
  led the conference in punt-return average, expanding to KR."
- Scout (opposing SBC assistant): "I hope they've got a regression coming... that QB they lost to Wisconsin
  [Joseph] was the real deal, him being gone is the biggest thing. Their corner Brandon Crutchfield + safety
  Lucas Struck really flashed on tape, and they're back... they signed a LB, Montrell Chapman, from Miss St."
