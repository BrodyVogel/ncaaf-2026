# Indiana 2026 — magazine notes (blinding v2: team-level projections/rankings EXCLUDED)

Sources: Athlon 2026 (Indiana section, p.4 of B10 extract — header-verified); Pick Six
Previews 2026 (book pp. 66-67); Phil Steele 2026 (p. 4 of B10 pdf, text + lineup, read
as images). Captured 2026-07-16.
2025 result (historical fact): 16-0, 9-0 B10 — won the national championship (beat OSU
13-10 for the B10 title, then Alabama 38-3, Oregon 56-22, Miami 27-21). First outright
B10 title since 1945. Outscored opponents 666-187.

## Coaching
- Cignetti 27-2 in two years here (146-37 career; never a losing season as HC; JMU
  52-9 before this). NO staff turnover flagged: OCs Mike Shanahan + Tino Sunseri and
  DC Bryant Haines (2025 BROYLES AWARD winner) all RETAINED.
- Transfer philosophy is the program identity: "production over potential" (Athlon).
  P6: Cignetti's first two transfer QBs here (Rourke, Mendoza) both hit at all-conf+
  level; Cignetti says Hoover is "at a similar point post-spring" as those two were.
- Athlon scouting box (opposing B10 assistant): "The biggest glaring thing is the
  production they lost in the receiver room... they paid a s— ton for Nick Marsh...
  very young there. Defensively, they still return most of their production... hard to
  replicate what Mendoza did. Hoover is a good player... some turnover issues. But
  other than that, they reloaded... they're gonna lose that rush of James Madison guys
  ... could struggle in some areas of continuity... I'm definitely not gonna
  underestimate Indiana again."

## QB
- GONE: Fernando Mendoza — Heisman winner, national-high 41 pass TD, #1 QB rating,
  #1 overall NFL pick (3,535, 72%, 41-6 + 276 rush). Backup Alberto Mendoza (24-33,
  390, 7-1) portaled to Georgia Tech.
- IN: Josh Hoover (TCU) — "the most productive returning QB in FBS": 9,629 yds, 71 TD
  across 36 games/31 starts at TCU (65.2%); nearly 4,000 yds in 2024. PS ranks him
  top-40 QB nationally (#34, unit-level).
- The flag, in both books: turnovers — "42 turnovers in 31 starts" (P6); "a turnover
  issue that needs fixing" and "very generously listed at 6-2" (Athlon). Athlon:
  "heady player who will thrive in Cignetti's scheme."
- Depth: Athlon QB2 = FR Tyler Cherry; PS lists Grant Wilson (class conflict w/ feed —
  see news.md). Thin behind the rental.

## RB
- GONE: the 1,000-yard duo — Kaelon Black (1,039 @ 5.6; drafted rd-3 SF) and Roman
  Hemby (1,120 @ 4.9) both pro.
- RETURN: Khobie Martin — 505-506 yds @ 6.5 as the #3; "waited his turn and looks
  primed to have a breakout season" (Athlon). Lee Beebe Jr. back from injury (OFY
  after 3 gm / 209 @ 7.7 in '25; 1,276 @ 5.8 F3Y at UAB).
- IN: Turbo Richard (Boston College starter; 749 @ 5.2 in '25, 1,927 L2Y; four-star
  transfer rank per P6) — P6 notes two goal-line fumbles that cost BC games.
- PS: "a strong deep room just like '25." rFr Cuono is PS#70 RB.

## WRTE
- GONE: both All-B10 receivers pro — Sarratt (830 yds; NATION-HIGH 15 rec TD; #4 DC
  BAL) and Cooper (937 @ 13.6; #2 in P4 w/ 13 TD; #1 DC NYJ); also EJ Williams (443)
  and All-B10 TE Nowakowski (16 st; #5 DC PIT).
- RETURN: Charlie Becker — "closed out 2025 looking like one of the country's top
  playmakers" (Athlon); title-game hero (back-shoulder 4th-down catch); 679 @ 20.0.
- IN: Nick Marsh (Michigan State's former top receiver; ~1,300 yds over two seasons;
  P6 top-rated transfer); Shazz Preston (Tulane's #1 WR; 16.8/catch = top-5 AAC);
  Tyler Morris back from '25 injury (Michigan transfer; "the spring hero in the slot"
  — P6; yr4 medical-return override, see news.md).
- TE = "the biggest question heading into fall camp" (P6): Barker (FR blocker) +
  4* Brock Schott (Miami transfer, receiving threat). Shanahan may lean 4-WR sets.

## OL
- FOUR starters back from the Joe Moore semifinalist (P6; PS: finalist) title line;
  PS: "once again a Joe Moore Award favorite."
- LT Carter Smith: All-American + reigning B10 Offensive Lineman of the Year. C Drew
  Evans, RG Bray Lynch return (P6 sketches Lynch at C; PS/Athlon print Evans at C,
  Lynch at RG); RT Ajani "surged up the depth chart in the playoffs" (P6; 4 st/15 gm).
- GONE: All-B10 C Pat Coogan (#6 DC TEN) — the lone starter departure — and '23 RT
  Benson.
- IN: Joe Brunner (Wisconsin starter; #3 interior-lineman transfer this cycle — P6;
  24 career starts per PS text).

## DL
- 2025 context: 46 sacks (#2 FBS), 129 TFL (#1 FBS), 77 rush ypg allowed (3.0/carry).
- RETURN: the DT pair — "could be one of the nation's best pairs" (P6): Tucker ("could
  push for All-America") + Landino ("a wrecking-ball throughout the season"). Ndukwe
  "played well after the starters were injured" (P6).
- GONE: 5 DL (37 combined starts, 12 sk, 143 tkl — PS) incl. Kamara (10.5 sk in '24;
  turned down NFL once already), Daley, Wheeler (to Baylor), Wyatt, Ratcliff.
- IN: BOTH of Kansas State's starting ends — All-B12 Chiddi Obiazor + "elite pass
  rusher" Tobi Osunsanmi (P6); Josh Burnham (Notre Dame; 65 tkl, 6 sk career) and Joe
  Hjelle (Tulsa; 46 tkl, 2.5 sk '25) as three-star adds.
- PS: "figure to be down a notch after allowing just 77 ypg rush with 46 sacks."

## LB
- GONE: 1st-team AA MLB Aiden Fisher (#7 DC HOU) — "the quarterback-of-the-defense"
  (P6) — and NB Boykin (#5 tkl).
- RETURN: BOTH other backers off All-B10 seasons — Rolijah Hardy (#1 tkl '25; 124 tkl,
  9 sk, 11.5 TFL L2Y per PS lineup) + Isaiah Jones (#4 tkl; 122 tkl, 7.5 sk career).
- P6: "with a proven pair, they may show more 4-2-5 looks rather than their
  three-backer 4-3." PS: "remain solid, but... down a notch with the loss of their
  leader. They are not as deep and need a 3rd LB to emerge."
- Depth: Turner (25 gm career), Utzinger; 4* ATH Ohlinger is the top '26 signee.

## DB
- 2025: PS PDR #12 two years running (#17 -> #12). Nine All-B10 defenders team-wide in
  '25; six return for '26 (P6).
- GONE: 1st-team AA CB D'Angelo Ponds (#2 DC NYJ; opened the CFP semi with a pick-six)
  + 1st-team S Louis Moore (16 st) + NB Boykin.
- RETURN: CB Jamari Sharpe — "AA and NFL potential" (PS); made "the national
  title-sealing interception" (P6); 28 st/40 gm, 14 pb career. S Amare Ferrell — 28
  st, 8 INT L3Y (PS lineup). Gandy (arrived '25 from Pitt) in the CB2 battle.
- IN: AJ Harris (former five-star, Penn State starter; PS#3 CB — battling Gandy at
  CB2 per P6); FS Preston Zachman (Wisconsin; 7th year; 20 st/130 tkl/7 INT career;
  was Wisconsin's "Green Dot" defensive signal-caller before Wk-3 OFY); Quan Sanks
  (Cincinnati; 13 st, 92 tkl L2Y); Carson Williams (Montana St; 47 tkl, 9 pb).
- Rover: SO Byron Baldwin (9 gm as true FR) per Athlon; PS prints Bonds as NB backup
  (feed conflict — news.md). PS: "they are my #9 rated secondary" (unit rank, allowed).

## Specialists
- 2025: "Indiana special teams units were the best in the country" (Athlon); PS STR
  #19; 9 blocked kicks in two years; two PR TDs.
- K Nico Radicic RETURNS: 28-of-30 on FGs since 2024 (PS#5 kicker; "sneaky weapon").
- GONE: Franke (KO man; 58-yd school-record FG; 46 TB on 51 KO) + P (38.6 avg).
- IN: P Billy Gowers (Hawai'i — "top notch"; 49 punts, 46.2 avg, 25 I20 per PS lineup);
  K/KO Paddy McAteer (Troy; hit a 56-yd FG in the spring game); LS Drew Clausen (Iowa
  State — "a big pickup" per PS). Athlon prints Quinn Warren at P (open battle w/
  Gowers — news.md).
- Return game: Morris (PR per Athlon), Beebe (KR), Preston — "plenty of talented
  athletes" (Athlon).
