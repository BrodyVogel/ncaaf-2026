## THE SHAPE

opponent-adjusted offense powered by a Heisman-runner-up dual-threat QB. 2026 is a post-Pavia rebuild.

zero experience), the Mackey TE (Stowers) was drafted, and four OL starters departed off a line that
was already poor in pass pro (PBLK p4). What remains: a strong returning RB duo, a loaded/experienced
secondary, and elite special teams. The market projects a clear regression (a "rebuilding season").
