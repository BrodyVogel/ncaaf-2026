## THE SHAPE (3-source consensus)
Clark Lea Year 5 (no HC change; band x1.00); OC Tim Beck returns. Coming off a 10-3 season - the
first 10-win year in school history - built on the "Pavia Effect" (the #1 opponent-adjusted offense,
Heisman runner-up). 2026 is a post-Pavia rebuild: the do-it-all QB is gone (replaced by the #1
OVERALL recruit, true frosh Jared Curtis), the Mackey Award TE (Stowers) was drafted, and four OL
starters are gone. Strengths remain at RB, a loaded/experienced secondary, and elite special teams.
Market projects a clear regression (Athlon NF 45, "rebuilding season" per P6).

## Pick Six (p162-163, Brett Ciancia) — facts only (rankings/Game-Grader NOT recorded)
- Lea's "10-year plan" hit a 10-win season in half the time. The NMSU core (Pavia + Beck's staff)
  built the #1 opponent-adjusted offense. Pavia: 3,593 pass/9.4 per att/29 TD + 862 rush/8 TD;
  Heisman runner-up; first-ever school 10-win season (beat Tennessee 45-24 in the finale).
- QB: signed the #1 OVERALL recruit Jared Curtis (5-star flip from Georgia; Vandy's first-ever 5-star);
  enrolled early, clear #1 in spring, "will be a true freshman starter." "The absence of Pavia... and
  the loss of four starting linemen are major uncertainties."
- OL: "Cade McConnell is the only returning starting lineman"; 3 impact transfers all projected
  starters: Beau Johnson (NDSU, FCS All-American T), Lyndon Cooper (Pitt, multi-year C), Micah DeBose
  (Alabama, Top200). Irving + Azubuike battle at the other tackle.
- TE: "Stowers... selected in the 2nd round in April." Cole Spence returns as a starter (12-personnel);
  + 4-star transfer Jayvontay Conner (ECU).
- WR: "a pair of 800-yard receivers - Tre Richardson + Junior Sherrill - but Richardson transferred to
  Louisville." Sherrill returns + transfers Ja'Cory Thomas (ODU, 719 yds, 17.5 ypc) + Cole Adams (Alabama)
  + Tristen Brown (slot).
- RB: "Sedrick Alexander and Makhilyn Young both return... combined for 981 yards and 17 scores, Young
  a high 9.0 per carry"; a bigger workload without Pavia's carries. + 4-star Evan Hampton (KY Gatorade POY).
- DL: "returns three DL starters + gets back NFL-prospect DT Yilanan Ouattara (1 game before injury).
  Khordae Sydnor will be missed but Miles Capers returns at the other edge." 5 transfers: Edwin Kolenge
  (Boston College, P4 starter), Brian Allen (Iowa, 4-star grade), CJ Jackson (LSU, 4-star '24).
- LB: "Langston Patterson's eligibility appeal was declined"; Bryan Longwell returns (3rd year starting
  ILB); Bryce Cowan + Nick Rinaldi move up.
- DB: "starting corner pair returns... Jordan Matthews the #1 cover corner, a possible star. Martel
  Hight became Vandy's first two-way player (4 INT + 7 catches)." Safety room "absolutely loaded":
  CJ Heard (led team in tackles) + school's first all-conf transfer Ricardo Jones (Clemson, ACC-best 6
  INT) + Dontae Carter + 4-star frosh.
- OUTLOOK: "the league's lowest talent profile, Post-Pavia... set for a rebuilding season."
