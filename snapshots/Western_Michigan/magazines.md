- 2025: 10-4 (7-1 MAC), MAC CHAMPIONS. Results verified: L at Michigan St
  6-23, L North Texas 30-33 OT, L at Illinois 0-38, W Toledo 14-13, W Rhode
  Island 47-14, W at UMass 21-3, W Ball State 42-0, L at Miami (OH) 17-26,

  at Eastern Michigan 31-21, W MAC title vs Miami (OH) 23-13 (Detroit), W
  Kennesaw State 41-6 (Myrtle Beach Bowl).
- OFFENSE ran 67% last season and "that doesn't figure to change": MAC
  Offensive POY Broc Lowry back at QB (963 rush, 14 TD; 63% comp, 9 TD/3
  INT, 6.9 ypa "could stand to improve") + third-team All-MAC RB Jalen
  Buckley (1,003 yds, 9 TD). MAC Coach of the Year Lance Taylor wants
  Lowry to push the passing game in year 2 as starter.
- OL "will again be strong, with three returning starters"; C Jeremy
  Schleicher back after missing most of 2025 hurt.
- WR: two of the top three back (Baylin Brooks, Aveion Chenault); Egypt
  Nelson "turned heads in the spring."
- DEFENSE: allowed 17.4 ppg LY - 9th nationally; "tough time matching
  that" but "should be stout again." Top three corners back incl. 3rd-Tm
  All-MAC Joshua Franklin; John Peters impressed as true-FR nickel; S
  Micah Davis (former Michigan signee) productive. Dakari Frazier ready
  for an increased DT role; adds Ahmed Tounkara (Ohio State) and Austin
  Alexander (North Carolina). LBs Domanick Moon + Dillon Moore (42
  combined reserve tackles) return; Tennessee transfer Brenden Anes +
  juco Justyce Betts bolster the unit. Lost Nadame Tucker (14.5 sacks,
  21 TFL).
- SPECIALISTS: Domschke 10-of-13 from 40+; P Millmore 42.4 avg with 11 of
  50+; juco Kordell Gouldsby had 5 KR TDs in two years at Hutchinson CC.
- FINAL ANALYSIS: "lost just one projected returning starter to the
  portal"; brought in 10 Power 4 transfers; closes with Miami (OH) at home
  ("MAC title game implications").
- Scouting (opposing MAC assistant): "I think they're gonna repeat in the
  MAC. They're returning a quarterback, returning some key pieces and not
  losing a lot of guys in the portal... The only way they don't repeat is
  because in the MAC, any given Tuesday... they slip up. I don't think
  Lance Taylor is gonna be there for another year after this one...
  Everything is going right at that school right now."
