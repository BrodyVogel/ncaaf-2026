## Athlon (p.9)
- 2025: 3-9 (2-6 MWC) — snapped a three-year bowl run. Results: L CMU
  14-16, L at Texas 7-38, W Idaho 31-28, L at Stanford 29-30, W New
  Mexico 35-28, L at Wyoming 28-35, L at Utah St 25-30, W Hawai'i 45-38,
  L Air Force 16-26, L at Nevada 10-55, L at SDSU 3-25, L Fresno 14-41.
- 2nd in MWC passing but "undone by 17 interceptions." QB battle: Robert
  McDaniel (last year's third-stringer; spring leader), Hawai'i transfer
  Luke Weaver, FR Daniel Rolovich.
- WR room produced the NATION'S receiving-yardage leader in BOTH
  Niumatalolo seasons (Scudero '25, Nash '24 — triple crown) and is now
  "starting from scratch." Malachi Riley (slated to start '25, lost Wk 1,
  lower-body) should be the No. 1; Penn State transfer Anthony Ivey "one
  to watch."
- RB: Jabari Bates returns for the bulk — team-best 7.1 ypc, ~50 ypg as a
  reserve.
- OL: league-low 13 sacks allowed but only TWO starters return; C Joseph
  Harbert an all-league candidate.
- DEFENSE (0) bold returning starters; "rebuilding." OLB Ethan Powell
  back (Athlon prose treats him as returning); OLB Zane Carter (slated
  '25 starter, missed year, injury); MLB Danielson + FCS Weber St
  transfer Aizik Mahuka (big early spring impression). DE Justin Stearns
  (6 career starts) leads the line; Nate Lewis "athletic... signs of
  development."
- DB: allowed the MWC's 2nd-highest passer rating; league-low 7 INTs. CB
  Greco Carrillo (would have started, missed '25 hurt); Kejuan Bullard
  Jr. atop the depth chart; FCS Cal Poly S Brian Dukes Jr.; converted WR
  Tuli Tagovailoa-Amosa; NEW pass-game coordinator/CB coach Brian
  Norwood.
- ST: P Trent Carrizosa all-league candidate (45.6). K battle: Mathias
  Brown (4-7 FG LY) vs FCS Idaho St transfer Trajan Sinatra.
- FINAL ANALYSIS: "starting over... wide variance between SJSU's floor
  and ceiling. Recent track record of an elite pass offense, but the
  defense is full of questions."
- Scouting (opposing MWC assistant): "that offense is a system, and that
  offense produces... Niumatalolo is a winner... I'd be willing to bet
  they're gonna have a pretty solid bounce-back year and be a contender
  ... They're just great at finding ways to get their playmakers the
  ball."
- Depth chart (bold=returning starter, *=2026 transfer): OFF (2) — WR
  Riley/Clark, WR Ivey*/McKenzie*, WR Amisone/Hoch, WR McClure*/
  Anderson*, LT Tapu*/Taukeiaho*, LG Moleni/Perez, C HARBERT/Tuliau, RG
  CHEN/Manukainiu, RT Afalava/Tupou*, QB McDaniel/Weaver*, RB Bates/Teu.
  DEF (0) — DE Stearns/Tuiileila*, DT Langi/McGlothen*, DT Siaosi/
  Shewell*, DE Lewis/Finau, OLB Powell/Lee, MLB Danielson/Mahuka*, OLB
  Carter/Hoeft, CB Bullard/Crawford, CB Carrillo/Buxton*, S Dukes*/
  Barton*, S Tagovailoa-Amosa/Walker*. ST — K Brown, P Carrizosa, KR
  Bates, PR Tagovailoa-Amosa.
- Coach box: Niumatalolo 10-15 (2 yrs); career 119-98 (18). OC Craig
  Stutzmann; DC Bojay Filimoeatu (new). CEFCU Stadium (18,203).
- 2026 schedule: at USC, at EMU, Cal Poly, Fresno St, at Hawai'i,
  Wyoming, at UTEP, at Nevada, New Mexico, NIU, at Air Force, UNLV,
  North Dakota St (13 games).
