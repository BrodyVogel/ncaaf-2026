## Program frame
- 2025: 5-7 (4-4 MAC) — after 1-23 the previous two seasons (0-12 in '24 with a
  21-game losing streak, −30 ppg, −283 ypg). HC Kenni Burns placed on leave in
  March, fired April 11; OC Mark Carney named interim days before spring ball,
  went 5-7 (−50 ypg MAC play) and was awarded the job full-time after the
  comeback win over BGSU. Opposing coach: "one of the best turnarounds I've
  ever seen at the FBS level."
- 2026 = Carney yr 2 (Cleveland native; ex-Charlotte OC '19-22; All-American QB
  at Fordham under Dave Clawson). OC Clay Patterson yr 2; DC Cherokee Valeria
  (promoted from DB coach July '25 — ex-Sacramento St pass-game coord '19-24);
  new ST analyst = KSU great Josh Cribbs. HC continuity → no multiplier.
- Roster build: 24 true FR signed (11 early enrollees); "building the team from
  the ground up" (PS).
- Athlon final analysis: "He was able to hang on to his QB, expectations have
  risen, although a bowl might be just out of reach this year."
- Opposing MAC assistant (Athlon): "Those guys played hard for Carney... you
  could see the belief... [DeShields] doesn't turn the football over, ever...
  There's not one thing that flashes with him. He just does everything really
  well."

## Offense (Athlon: 6 returning starters — DeShields, W.Harris, Meadows, Morell, Dunn, Hopperton)
- QB: Dru DeShields RETURNS — kept out of the portal ("fortunate to hold on").
  2,030 @ 56.7%, 18-3 TD/INT in his first season as starter (9 st); "very fast
  but they limited his running (36 yd) to keep him healthy" — off 3 separate
  ACLs, now another year removed. PS: "should be one of the MAC's top QBs."
  Kargman → Maryland and Montes portaled after losing the job. Depth: rFrs
  Good/Ruffin + NAIA Defiance transfer Thanthanavong (1,466 @ 66%, 10-6 —
  "may have the strongest arm").
- RB: top 3 depart (997 combined @ 3.8; Garcia 647 led). NO RB1 emerged from
  spring (Athlon: "most uncertainty on the team"): Cincinnati transfer Caffey
  (2 gm there), FCS Illinois St transfer King (883 @ 6.6 in '23 — old tape),
  White, Delaney, NAIA Armentrout, true FRs.
- WR/TE: loses top-2 pass catchers (Wolford 509 @ 26.8 → Cincinnati; Martin
  507 → Virginia) but returns a real group: slot Wayne Harris (383 @ 10.8, led
  in receptions as rFr; big spring step), TE Terik Mulder (19-198-4 — Athlon:
  "All-MAC candidate... will figure heavily"), Banks (2 st, 205 @ 17.1;
  ex-Kentucky RS), and KR star Da'Realyst Clark "expected to carry more of a
  load at WR" (3 st, 108 @ 36.0 receiving). Adds D2 Tiffin's Willis.
- OL: "holes to fill, but incoming transfers make this unit deeper." Returning
  starters: Meadows LT (7 st, 9 gm as a TRUE FR), Morell RG (28 career starts:
  8+9+11), Dunn (8 st as rFr — INJURED IN SPRING, "? for '26" per PS —
  monitor), Wilson (1 st '23, OFY '25 injury return). Adds: C Midea (St
  Francis, 9 st), RT Adewole (Robert Morris, 12 st), Imbt (SFU 3rd-Tm NEC '24,
  DNP '25), Chasko (JC; OFY at KSU LY).

## Defense (Athlon: 3 returning starters — Aden, J.Cordoba-DT, Jackson III)
- DL: loses 3 starters + 3 backups (~40 starts, 13.5 sk) incl. its best player
  (Mathis → Texas Tech) — but the '25 unit improved rush D from an FBS-worst
  265 ypg/5.9 ypc in '24 to 191 (4.6). Returns "Grandfather" DT Thomas Aden
  (ex-Pitt; 9 st, 24 tkl) + 3rd-year-program edges Roy and Bottiggi (Athlon:
  "worked their way to the top of the depth chart") + true-FR riser
  Blachewicz (21 tkl, 3.5 tfl). Adds Semaj Bridgeman (ex-Michigan/Michigan St
  — feed-gap, in MSU's frozen departure list) + RMU's Wilborn (5 st, 51 tkl).
- LB: loses the top tacklers (Woods #1, walk-on-safety-turned-LB Young #2,
  C.Williams #6). Rocco Nicholl BOOMERANGS back (started at KSU in '24 — 8 st,
  61 tkl — spent '25 at USF, 4 gm; "will be one of the leaders"). Abuhamdeh
  (38 tkl) the top returnee; Kentucky transfer Dawahare; unit finished 125th
  in FBS total defense.
- DB: BOTH young safeties back — Derrick Jackson III (7 st, 49 tkl as a TRUE
  FR; feed lists yr4 in error — override documented) and Joel Cordoba (8 st,
  68 tkl as true FR) — "the frosh S's are now sophs." Nickel Barnes played 70%
  of snaps (ex-West Florida D2). CORNERS ALL NEW (Rawlinson → West Virginia;
  Miller expired): Muhammad (5 st LY; ex-UTEP) vs Pitt transfer Pritchard vs
  FRs — Athlon: "there could be a learning curve there."
