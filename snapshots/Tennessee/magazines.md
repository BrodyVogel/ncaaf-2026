## THE SHAPE (3-source consensus)
Heupel Year 6 (no HC change; band x1.00). The 2025 team was an elite offense
(as-played OFF p92, PASS p99) and a middling-to-poor defense (DEF p54, fell to
"#50 of 68 P4" per P6). 2026 is defined by two swings: (1) QB Joey Aguilar
(3,565 yds) is out of eligibility after losing an appeal -> an unproven QB
battle; (2) Heupel fired DC Tim Banks and hired Jim Knowles (Ohio State, natl
title) -> arguably the top coordinator hire of the cycle, plus a wave of P4
transfers who know his scheme. Big returning strengths: the OL (5 back), RB
Bishop, LB corps (stacked). Big losses: the QB, the entire DL starting group,
and two NFL-drafted corners.
