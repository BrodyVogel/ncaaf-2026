# Oregon 2026 — magazine notes (blinding v2: team-level projections/rankings EXCLUDED)

Sources: Athlon 2026 (p.13 — header-verified); Pick Six 2026 (book pp. 62-63 = pdf
3-4, header-located); Phil Steele 2026 (p.5, text image). Captured 2026-07-16.
2025 (fact): 13-2, 8-1 B10 — beat OkSt 69-3 (their worst loss in a century), won the
last Civil War 41-7, took the PSU White Out in 2OT (30-24); both losses to eventual
champ Indiana (20-30 home — snapped an 18-game home streak — and 22-56 in the CFP
semi); Peach QF 23-0 shutout of #4-seed Texas Tech; 51-34 over JMU in the first home
playoff game in school history.

## Coaching
- Lanning yr 5 (48-8; $11M/yr extension; 39 years old). NO HC change. BOTH
  coordinators left for HC jobs pre-playoffs; NEW OC Drew Mehringer (internal — co-OC
  /TE coach the last 4 years) + NEW DC Chris Hampton (ex-Tulane DC). ST coach Lorig
  RETAINED (#11/#23 STR finishes).
- Recruiting context: only program with three straight Top-5 classes AND two Top-5
  transfer hauls (P6); "recruiting and competing like one of the top five or 10
  programs" (Athlon).
- Athlon scout: "That might be the best roster in college football... [Dante Moore]
  was the best quarterback we faced last year... at no point did I think what they
  did on defense was super complicated, but it was just so sound... they look like a
  team with a lot of 5-stars."

## QB
- DANTE MOORE RETURNS — "surprised by returning" after 3,565-3,665 yds (by book),
  30-10, #5 P4 QB rating, 72% comp (top five); "a polished passer... Heisman
  favorite" (P6). 90% completion game vs Minnesota (27-30 — program record). The
  named blemish: "a rough game" in the semi vs Indiana (PS).
- DYLAN RAIOLA (Nebraska; 2,000 @ 72%, 19-5 in '25) transferred in BEFORE Moore's
  announcement and STAYED after it — "a developmental year before gunning for the
  2027 job" (P6). PS: Moore "could be the top QB in the 2027 draft."
- Depth: rFr Akili Smith Jr. (PS#9 '25), Thomas.

## RB
- The freshman duo aced the audition: Davison (PS#7; 236-lb bruiser; 667 @ 5.9, 15
  TD) + Hill (PS#9; 656 @ 8.7-8.8 — nation-best ypc). "These two are the lead pair"
  (P6); "could be the first Duck duo to each top 1,000 since 2008" (PS). Athlon:
  "thunder and lightning."
- GONE: Whittington (829 @ 6.4, grad); the phenoms "sent three other backs to the
  portal" (P6) — Limar -> Washington, Harris -> Kansas State, Hughes -> Houston.
- IN: Top100 FR Tradarian Ball (PS#4) as RB3; Price (Colorado). PS: "my #3 RB corps
  in the FBS."

## WRTE
- The 5* pair returns healthy: Dakorien Moore (the #1 WR recruit of '25; missed
  games) + Evan Stewart (knee, missed all of '25; yr4 medical — "returned fully
  healthy" P6; "an elite one-two punch"). McClellan (Top100 FR who "stepped up")
  starts too; staff going "position-less" to play all three (P6).
- IN: Hooks (UAB — ~900 yds; "the X-factor" Athlon) + two more 5*s (Lott, Bair).
- GONE: Benson (719 @ 16.7 team lead; rd-6 LV), Bryant (yr4).
- TE: Sadiq — 1st-tm All-B10, FIRST TE DRAFTED in April — gone; "minimal drop-off to
  Jamari Johnson" (Athlon), who "surged over the back half and was matching Sadiq's
  performance" (P6); Top50s Olesh (Penn State) + FR Harrison battle for #2.

## OL
- GONE: three All-B10 starters — tackles World + Harkey, guard Pregnon. RETURN:
  1st-team AA C Laloulu ("spurned the NFL draft" — Athlon) + 3rd-yr guard Iuli.
- "The staff declined the transfer portal this cycle and doubled down on blue-chip
  recruiting" (P6): LT = 4* Crader vs Top100 Addison; veterans Rogers + Bennett
  (Yale) challenged by four Top100 linemen. PS: 52 career starts; "remain strong."
- '25: Joe Moore finalist (one of the top 3); 199 rush (5.4), 16 sacks; all five
  linemen earned All-B10 (12 offensive players all-conference).

## DL
- THE ENTIRE STARTING FRONT RETURNS — all four "opting to skip the 2026 draft and
  await 2027" (Athlon): Uiagalelei (led the B10 in sacks in '24; 3rd-yr starter) +
  Tuioti ("high motor"; 5.5 sk) off the edges; Alexander + Washington ("a pair of
  NFL caliber DTs" — PS) inside. "This could be the best four-man front in the
  country" (Athlon); "my #2 rated DLine" (PS).
- The tell: all four BACKUPS transferred out to P4 schools with 4* grades (Porter ->
  Houston, Green -> Alabama, Gray -> Notre Dame, Purchase -> Ole Miss).
- Restock: Robinson (UNC starter), 5*s Rushing + A.Jones, Top200s Wyatt + Breland,
  Dixon (Clemson EDGE), Simmons (ULM).

## LB
- GONE: Boettcher — 136 tackles, the most by a Duck since 1979; Burlsworth winner;
  rd-4 IND. (NB Canady — 2nd-tm SEC import, rd-4 KC — counted at DB.)
- RETURN: Mixon ("a breakout 2025" — Athlon; #5 tkl) + Jackson (Top100 w/ "sprinter
  speed... poised to finally break out" — P6; 47 tkl as backup in '24, starter-level
  snaps in '25). "The staff ignored the portal here" — three 4* FR reserves (P6).
  PS: "down a few notches."

## DB
- P6's rave: "the secondary is simply unbelievable... I don't think I've seen this
  amount of raw firepower in any defensive backfield in Pick Six Previews' history —
  four five-stars, four more Top50s, four more Top100s."
- GONE: All-American S Thieneman (drafted rd-1-adjacent; PS "#1 DC CHI") + NB Canady
  (rd-4). Portal: Austin -> Arizona, Laulea -> Missouri, Lopa -> Cal, Fields ->
  Oklahoma, Florence -> Missouri.
- RETURN: Finney — FRESHMAN ALL-AMERICA at corner ("one of the best young corners in
  the country, if not one of the best corners, period" — Athlon) + Obidegwu (FR-year
  starter) + S Flowers; 5* Offord ("potential breakout" — Athlon); Woodyard.
- IN: Koi Perich (Minnesota — the #1 transfer safety; two-time All-B10; "a more than
  capable replacement for Thieneman" — Athlon; Athlon also lists him at KR), Scott
  (Ohio State 5*), C.Williams (Baylor — nickel), 5*s McNutt + J.Washington in the
  two-deep. PS: "my #2 secondary."

## Specialists
- GONE: both legs — K (19-24) and P (38.4 net). IN: K Emmett (Nevada) vs returners
  Hurych/Graziano; Athlon lists P Ettridge* and LS RJ Todd* as portal adds (feed
  gaps — news.md). ST coach Lorig retained (#11 and #23 STR the last two years).
  PS: "should remain strong." Returns: Perich (KR), D.Moore (PR).
