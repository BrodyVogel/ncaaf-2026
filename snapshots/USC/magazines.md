# USC 2026 — magazine notes (blinding v2: team-level projections/rankings EXCLUDED)

Sources: Athlon 2026 (p.18 — header-verified); Pick Six 2026 (book pp. 68-69 = pdf
9-10, header-located); Phil Steele 2026 (pdf p.6 spread, header-verified).
Captured 2026-07-16.
2025 (fact): 9-4, 7-2 B10 — a 73-13 opener; 7-0 at home; the Iowa comeback (down
21-7, scored on five straight possessions, 29-10... won 26-21 per P6's account of
the comeback [Athlon table prints 26-21]); King Miller's 158 yards flattened #15
Michigan 31-13 with two OL and three RBs down; won the Nebraska "Blackout." Losses:
the Illinois shootout (32-34), Notre Dame (24-34 — a kickoff-return TD the swing),
Oregon (27-42 in the quasi play-in), and the Alamo Bowl vs TCU (27-30 OT).
P6: "an important nine wins" — Riley survived to land "this program-changing
recruiting haul" (the #1 class, first since the Carroll era). Riley: "we'll have
better teams here in the future... but I don't know if we'll have had a more
important team."

## Coaching — NO HC change (Riley yr 5; 90-28 career)
- NEW DC: D'Anton Lynn LEFT for Penn State after two years ("no breakthrough" —
  P6). GARY PATTERSON takes the defense — 20 years of elite TCU defenses,
  match-zone 4-2-5; "I've never had this big of a defensive line" at the opening
  presser. OC Luke Huard (uncle of QB2 Sam); Riley calls the plays.
- NEW STC: Mike Ekeler from Nebraska (Kansas/N. Texas/Tennessee before).
- Athlon scout: "The defensive questions are very much there, but they're also
  recruiting to the point where it wouldn't surprise me to see those questions
  answered... damn near 50-50 [on the CFP]... They're hoarding a lot of the big
  bodies. A player like Jahkeem Stewart is one of the more unbelievable guys
  you'll scout."
- The build: only 9 portal ins vs the nation's #1 recruiting class — "less
  plug-and-play and more homegrown" (Athlon).

## QB
- MAIAVA RETURNS: 3,711 (5th nationally) @ 65.8-66%, 24-10; 9.2 Y/A (2nd B10);
  QBR 89.9 (2nd nationally); 3rd-team All-B10; 157 rush. "Will need to cut down
  on the 10 picks, but this should remain a Top 10 offense with him at the helm"
  (P6). "Potentially elite" (Athlon). PS: "my #7 QB unit."
- GONE: 5* Longstreet -> LSU ("projected as the 2027 starter of the future").
- Depth: Huard (yr4 — prints as QB2 in BOTH charts; 2,247 @ 61% at Cal Poly '23;
  a fake-punt TD pass LY) + 5*-adjacent true FR Jonas Williams (PS#7) + JC
  Doherty.

## RB
- RETURN the top duo: JORDAN (576 @ 6.5; 6 starts then OFY — two 100-yd games in
  five while healthy; JC 1,614 @ 7.4 before) + MILLER (the walk-on: 972 @ 6.2,
  four 100+ games, the Michigan 158). Riley: "the best RB room he has ever had"
  (PS, coach-speak); PS: "my #9 RB corps."
- GONE: Sanders (258 @ 5.9; OFY after 6 — 1,063 @ 7.2 at NMSU prior; yr4).
- IN: Top100 true FR pair — Redeaux ("fastest RB") + Alston ("physical") —
  from the #1 class; Wormley depth.

## WRTE
- GONE: the top three — LEMON (1,156 @ 14.4; the BILETNIKOFF winner; led P4 at
  96 rec ypg; 11 TD; FIRST-ROUND pick PHI — yr3 DECLARE) + Lane (743 @ 15.2;
  rd-3 BAL — yr3 DECLARE) + TE McRee (450 @ 15.0; yr4). TE Lyons -> BYU.
- RETURN: HINES — "impressed as a freshman"; emerged late with 561 @ 16.5
  (100+ games vs Oregon and TCU). Z. Williams (Utah's '24 #1 receiver; injury-

- IN: Anderson (NC State's lead receiver; 629 @ 16.1; 4* transfer; 5th in ACC
  yds/catch) + the recruit wave: near-5*s Feaster + Dixon-Wyatt, Top200 Mosley

  Ashcraft (Wisconsin — "instant impact").
- Riley's TE usage doubled '24->'25; "he may shift back to more 11 or 10
  personnel if those elite receivers are as-advertised" (P6).

## OL
- ALL FIVE original starters return (P6): PAIGE LT ("the anchor"; 20 st L2Y),
  TAUANUU RT (12 st LY), NOA (22 st L2Y), RAYMOND (13 st at three spots —
  "positional flexibility"), O'CONNOR C. Riley: "if we can keep these guys
  healthy, this group will have the opportunity to be the most dominant that
  we've had."
- The '25 context: "crushed with injuries... seven different line combinations"
  yet "consecutive Top 25 finishes in both OL Run Push and Pass Protection"
  (P6).
- GONE: Reed (the '25 C volume; yr4), Banuelos + Saina -> Purdue, Payne -> UVA.
- IN: 5* Pepe (the nation's #1 tackle) + 4* Dyakonov + Top100-class interior
  FRs. Athlon scout: "They may have a freshman or two start on the offensive
  line."

## DL
- Patterson inherits the stockpile: RETURN Abasiri (DT), Crawford + Shelby
  (the top two sack guys — 4.5 sk Shelby), STEWART (5*; Freshman All-America;
  played 11 games with a foot issue as a true FR — "could be a force"), Boucard,
  Jarrett (360 lb; Georgia pedigree — a '25-cycle add, NOT a 2026 transfer).
- GONE: three linemen — Lucas (the #2 snap tape), Thompkins -> Alabama, Silver
  (yr4).
- IN: VanSumeren (Michigan State starter; 12 st) + Fisher (Penn State starter;
  4* transfer) + the class: 5* Winfield, Top100 Topui, 5* Wafle (#1 DE in the
  class). "USC hasn't posted a Top 30 run defense since 2014 but this is their
  best shot" (P6). Riley: "his best DL" (PS).

## LB
- GONE: Gentry ("do-it-all... whose skillset allowed for creative packages").
- RETURN: STEPHENS (led with 89 tackles) + Walker + Newby.
- IN: Bryant (Washington — 62 tkl, 10 st; "brings Big Ten starting experience")
  + Rufo (Georgetown; 103 tkl — FCS) + Top100 true FR Ili ("could push into
  the rotation").

## DB
- The '25 injury carnage: original starters C. Johnson + P. Brown lost by
  mid-September; BOTH star safeties injured in November. Now: Johnson + Brown
  RETURN from injury; the safeties are GONE — Fitzgerald (1st-team
  All-America; 5 int; yr4) + Ramsey (rd-5 HOU — yr3 DECLARE). Also gone:
  Nicholson (12 st; yr4), Conley -> Georgia, Harvey (yr4).
- RETURN: M. Williams, Graham, Pierce ("all started games last year" — P6),
  Urlacher, Gallegos.
- IN: JONTEZ WILLIAMS (Iowa State) — "the #1 transfer corner," All-B12, "an
  instant starter here" (P6) + Top100 super-recruit Hill + C. Pierce (Okla
  State). "Patterson has plenty to sort out in the secondary" (P6); Riley
  "likes the depth at cornerback" (Athlon).

## Specialists

  89 KO. Backup Chittenden (26-33 at UNLV '24; injured LY).
- GONE: the P (35.6 net) + the top KR/PR (Lemon). IN: P Carrigan (Memphis).

- PS STR: #15 ('23) -> #43 ('24) -> #86 ('25); the '24 Ray Guy winner
