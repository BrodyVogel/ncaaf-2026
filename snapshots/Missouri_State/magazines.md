## THE SHAPE (Athlon + beat)

Missouri State's 2nd FBS season (moved up to CUSA in 2025; a 7-6 FBS debut). A near-total roster turnover under
a staff that "got there late when the portal opened" (scout): an open transfer QB battle (Belin/Locklear) after
losing the '25 starter; the entire starting OL gone; heavy DL/secondary/LB turnover. But real strengths remain:
a deep, p79 receiving corps (Robinson + returning depth), an efficient returning RB (Green), an LB centerpiece
(Lloyd), + returning quality specialists (HM All-CUSA K Obeid + P). Athlon rates them CUSA #7 (NF 128).
