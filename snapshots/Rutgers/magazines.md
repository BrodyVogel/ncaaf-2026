# Rutgers 2026 — magazine notes (blinding v2: team-level projections/rankings EXCLUDED)

Sources: Athlon 2026 (p.16 — header-verified); Pick Six 2026 (book pp. 94-95 = pdf
35-36, header-located); Phil Steele 2026 (pdf p.18 spread, header-verified).
Captured 2026-07-16.
2025 (fact): 5-7, 2-7 B10. The split season: the best Rutgers offense of the B10 era
(first 3,000-yd passer / 1,000-yd rusher / 1,000-yd receiver trio since 2007; school
record for 30-point games; P6 #29 adjusted offense, 10th passing) attached to the
worst defense P6 has EVER graded ("dead last in FBS at 7.8 yards/play; the 7.6 in
league play is the B10's worst since 2000; the lowest-rated statistical defense I've
graded since I began tracking in 2010"). Blew second-half leads in four losses,
4Q leads in three (late INT vs Iowa; botched-snap chain in the 3-pt Minnesota loss;
scooped fumble decided PSU 36-40).

## Coaching — NO HC change (Schiano yr 7 of stint 2)
- Schiano 99-108 overall (school wins leader; '01-'11 first stint). Athlon scout:
  "Schiano is an excellent coach... but they were just not talented on defense last
  year. You turn on the tape, and they didn't have anybody that could do anything.
  That's a program problem." Also: "the school thinks they're actually going to
  make this investment... the rubber meets the road in the next year or so."
- OC Ciarrocca yr 4 RETAINED (the offense architect; ex-Minn/PSU OC).
- NEW DC after a 64-DAY search: co-DCs Robb Smith + Zach Sparber FIRED; TRAVIS
  JOHANSEN in — South Dakota HC in '25 (10-5, FCS quarterfinals), their DC
  '19-'24 (Top-10 FCS defense '24). "An unconventional hire" (P6); he brought
  "a number of familiar faces from his lower-level stops" (Athlon).
- P6's offseason frame: three requirements — retain top starters (kept Raymond/
  Duff/Asamoah), add portal talent ("signed the lowest-rated transfer class in the
  Big Ten"), fix the defense (FCS-heavy DC + imports). "Five proven starters
  transferred out to Power 4 programs."

## QB
- GONE: Kaliakmanis expired — 3,124 @ 62%, 20-7 (first 3,000-yd QB since Teel '08;
  #7 in B10 QB Rating; rd-7 WAS).
- The battle (into August — P6): LONERGAN (BC transfer; ex-Alabama, Saban/O'Brien
  pedigree; 9 starts, 2,025 @ 67%, 12-5 — "a few 300-yard games in September but

  true FR Stearn.

  4th-straight 1,000-yard rusher" (PS).

## WRTE
- DUFF RETAINED — "one of the best players in the Big Ten this season" (Athlon);
  1,084 @ 18.1 (B10-record 241 yds vs Purdue; first 700+ Rutgers receiver
  since '15 was the '24 top guy); 6'6, 17-yd average target depth, "excels in jump
  balls"; the one-handed PSU catch is a "catch of the year" candidate (P6).
- GONE: Strong (676-762 @ ~15, #2) -> Cal with a 4* transfer ranking; Sheffield
  (577-822 @ ~12.5-13) expired; TE Fletcher (#4) expired.
- Behind Duff: Black moves up ("veteran backup"), M. Thomas + 4* true FR Carter
  in the slot (P6); rFr Houston, Braithwaite, Allen, Toure. TE: Blake + Anthony
  (Ball St; 10 st, 117 yds). "Competition... should be strong among a number of
  returnees and talented incoming freshmen" (Athlon). PS: "remain strong."
