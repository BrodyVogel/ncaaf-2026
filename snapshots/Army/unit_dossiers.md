Compiled 2026-07-15. Sources: PFF 2025 (snapshot pff/), CFBD pulls, PS 2026 (OCR-verified), Athlon 2026,
On3/GBK spring-game report, official releases. AAC offsets: QB +0.4, RB +2.2, WRTE +0.5, OL +1.2,

SCHEME CONTEXT (touches every unit): triple-option; WR/TE usage is tiny by design (unit volumes below
normal floors); OL is run-block-dominant (team PBLK 56.6 vs RBLK 86.4 - pass pro is the known weakness);
defense plays fewer snaps behind clock-eating offense. Grades are still national percentiles on the
frozen ruler - scheme explains usage, not quality. HC continuity (Monken yr 13); DC change only
(Dixon/Sloan for Woody) = no coach-change variance multiplier. Academy: zero portal in; churn = graduation.

## QB — 2025 as-played 61st pctile (77.4 adj on 126 db - option volume)
- Cale Hellums (SR) RETURNS: 10 st, 304 carries (2nd nationally), 1,223 yds, 18 rush TD (T-3rd);
  694 pass (54%, 4-3); PFF 77.0. Rose from 3rd string (Athlon). Spring: clear frontrunner, improved
  "footwork, fundamentals, reads" (GBK 4/20).
- In THIS offense the QB unit is the engine: returning a proven 304-carry option QB with system
  continuity is the strongest possible configuration for the position. Passing ceiling is the cap
  (54%, 694 yds - defenses need not respect it).
- Depth: Luter/Washington (no starts) battle for QB2; Coleman (78.5/42) departed.
- ≥3 sources: PFF, PS, Athlon, GBK spring. Confidence: M (proven starter, low passing ceiling).
