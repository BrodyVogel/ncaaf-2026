# Louisiana — magazine findings (Athlon + Phil Steele, 2026 editions)

RULES: extract unit FACTS only. G5 = TWO-SOURCE (Athlon + Phil Steele); no Pick Six for the Sun Belt.

## THE SHAPE (2-source consensus)
Michael Desormeaux Year 5 (no HC change). A 6-7 team (started 2-6, won 4 straight to a bowl behind QB
Lunch Winfield) with a LOW-churn, mostly-returning roster - but the defense graduated 9 of its top 10.
Keeps a dual-threat QB + a 5-star WR (Sampson) + a veteran OL + a star CB (Gordon); lost both proven RBs
to the P4, graduated its top DL, and resets LB. Middle-tier Sun Belt West.
