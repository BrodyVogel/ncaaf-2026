## Pick Six 2026 (B12 pp. 25-26 = book 122-123; verbatim)
- 2025 arc: 5-7 off a top-10 returning-production year - "the pass game was the only thing that was
  as-advertised." 4-2 start (Robertson 440 vs SMU; 3 straight 400-yd games - first B12 QB since
  Mahomes; KSU comeback w/ Redding pick-six + Lanz blocked FG) then lost 5 of final 6. Defense
  allowed ~200 rush ypg (bottom ten); 13 sacks = fewest by a B12 defense in a decade. Aranda 36-37;
  November AD change "perhaps saved his job"; he SHOCKINGLY REJECTED a bowl bid (bonus practices
  declined). "2026 is certainly a make-or-break season."
- QB: Robertson graduated (3,681/31, both top-5 national). IN: DJ LAGWAY - #1 QB of the 2024 cycle,
  5-star, 16 Florida starts; 2nd in SEC QB Rating 2024, second-worst 2025; 6-5 240, run dynamic,
  "bowling ball" short-yardage; father was a Baylor RB; Spavital's scheme "reminds me of my high
  school offense" (5,500+ yds / 75 TD as a senior in it at Willis TX).
- OC Spavital returns: "we're a brand-new team" - 10 starters gone from a #20 opp-adjusted offense.
  Tempo = "our biggest weapon"; choice routes.
- WR/TE: ALL FOUR receivers gone (1st-tm All-B12 Cameron, Hawkins, Prentice, Wilson) + TE Trigg
  ("pass-game mismatch and human highlight reel"). IN: Dre'Lon Miller (Colorado - "most dynamic
  addition," WR starts as Fr then RB in 2025) + Freeman (OkSt). Returners Porter + Brown combined
  15 catches. Livingston (Florida 4* TE) followed Lagway; Klopfenstein back as the blocker.
- OL: "another complete rebuild in the trenches" - 4 of 5 gone (All-B12 Aigbedion grad; Price ->
  Kentucky; Thompkins -> LSU). RT K.Sieracki = only full returning starter (brother Koltin started
  a few at RG). Projected transfer starters: Walker (Memphis) C, Kibble (Texas) LG, Moore (UAB) LT.
  "The starting five were all three-star recruits or transfers."
- RB: Pendergrass (pre-season injury KO'd his 2025) "returned healthy as the lead back"; Washington
  (fumbles; ypc Top-40 -> below avg) -> Auburn; Knighten back off 104/469 as true FR.
- DEF: NEW DC Joe Klanderman (K-State): "track record of effective defenses... focus on creating
  havoc directly addresses the pass rush problem... Top 25 of turnovers in three of the past four
  seasons." Aranda gives up play-calling -> "CEO-type role." Aranda: "this is the most money we've
  spent on defense since I've been here." Powledge era: bottom-ten average rank last three years.
- DL: Tezino back at DT + Wheeler (Indiana natl champ, 4*) who "is APPEALING for a bonus eligibility
  season" (PENDING). Whyce (Marshall) + Mack (Coastal) interior depth; Hardy (S.Carolina) = "the
  overall #1 JUCO prospect of 2025"; Kyler Jordan returns (started final two).
- LB: Keaton Thomas ("the leader of the defense") -> Ole Miss. BACK: Barnes (original starter,
  former C-USA POY - injury), Reed (starter), rising 4* Burns.
- DB: two returning starters w/ All-B12 honors: S Redding + CB Thornton. Devin Turner (former
  All-B10 at Northwestern) "back healthy to start at safety" - Klanderman: "difference-maker."
  CB2 battle: Bush, Jordan (OU), JUCO All-Am McKelvey. Klanderman brought two K-Staters: Rowe +
  McCalister.
- Context (facts only): "four starters return, but they also get back two more original starters
  from injury, two starters off other Power 4 teams, and two JUCO All-Americans." Team-level 2026
  projections EXCLUDED.
