# Northwestern 2026 — magazine notes (blinding v2: team-level projections/rankings EXCLUDED)

Sources: Athlon 2026 (p.11 — header-verified); Pick Six 2026 (book pp. 88-89 = pdf
29-30, header-located); Phil Steele 2026 (p.15, text image). Captured 2026-07-16.
2025 (fact): 7-6, 4-5 B10 — the 22-21 upset at Penn State (biggest since 1995; the
final blow for Franklin), 19-0 over Purdue, 38-35 comeback over Minnesota; losses to
Tulane (3-23 opener, 4 INTs), Nebraska/USC/Michigan/Illinois; won the bowl 34-7.

## Coaching
- Braun yr 4 (19-19; B10 COY '23; first NU coach to win his first two bowls). NEW OC:
  CHIP KELLY (Oregon HC -> UCLA HC -> OSU's 2024 title OC) — "a program-defining
  change" (Braun). QB coach Jerry Neuheisel added. DC McGarigle yr 3 (LB coach here
  since 2018) — defensive continuity. New dedicated STC (Creighton).
- New Ryan Field opens THIS October ($862-865M — the most expensive stadium in CFB
  history); lakefront facilities. NO HC change — x1.13 does not apply.
- Athlon scout: "I was super interested in the Chip Kelly move... Everything they've
  built is this ground-and-pound, man-zone running scheme... Kelly is run-heavy, and
  there's this misconception that he throws it all over the yard. He's not, he's just
  a tempo guy... I don't think it was a shocking move, but it raised some eyebrows."
- Braun on Kelly's system: "You don't feel like there are any wasted plays... the
  ability to change tempo... is seamless" (Athlon).

## QB
- GONE: Stone (2,400 @ 60%, 17-12; 4-INT opener; 305-yd November rally vs Minnesota)
  — yr4. "A transfer has opened the season as starter every year here since 2019"
  (PS).
- The battle: Chiles (Michigan State — near-5* in '23; 20 career starts; 3,807 @ 61%,
  24-14 + 452 rush career; benched late '25 but "improved his decision-making with a
  10-3 ratio... still graded as a Top 15 transfer quarterback" — P6; KELLY RECRUITED
  HIM at UCLA — Athlon) vs Marchiol (West Virginia — 7 career starts L3Y; PS#18).
  Both dual-threat, both fit the scheme (P6). Boe (FR) the developmental #3.
- PS: Braun "feels they have 3 guys they can win with and the room is much deeper."

## RB
- Komolafe RETURNS: 941 @ 5.0, 11 TD, All-B10 honors after Porter's season-ending
  injury; four 100-yard games. Himon II returns (486 @ 4.9); Preckel the
  short-yardage ace (Athlon).
- IN: Sawchuk (Florida State via Oklahoma — 1,600 career yds, 7 st, PS#2-at-FSU
  label), M.Porter (Bowling Green). C.Porter's career ends (yr4, injured).
- PS: "my #26 RB corps." P6: Kelly "may share carries" (Henderson/Judkins pattern).

## WRTE
- Wilde RETURNS: 71 catches, 880 yds, 8 TD — 3rd-team All-B10; "the school's best
  season in a decade" (P6); ex-South Dakota State import who hit (1,147 @ 16.4
  there — PS).
- Eligon II RETURNS: 521 @ 14.1 — more than half his production in the final three
  games (7-127 vs MINN, 8-99 vs ILL — PS); "a vertical threat to complement Wilde"
  (P6).
- Depth: Covey + Ahumaraeze veterans, Wagner; Top150 four-star FR Jaden McDuffie on
  the two-deep (P6). NO transfer WRs taken (Athlon); Braun: "the mid-tier of that
  wide receiver room is going to have to elevate."
- TE: Welcing (all-league honors) -> Ohio State. IN: Honig (UConn; 6'6-7, 270-275
  in-line blocker) + D2 All-American Dehnicke (Minnesota-Duluth). Athlon: "bullish"
  on both.

## OL
- 2025 was "one of their best lines in recent memory" (P6): OL Push #133 -> #71,
  pass pro #60 -> #27; 160 rush (4.6), 17 sacks (PS); "worthy of Joe Moore Award
  mention" (PS).
- GONE: FOUR starters incl. BOTH draft picks — Beerntsen (#7 DC BAL) + Tiernan
  (#3 DC MIN) — plus Carsello and Lewis. Career starts drop to 43 (PS).
- RETURN: Oratokhai — "the top guy" (PS); can play guard or center (P6).
- IN: Seagren (Oklahoma State — "a proven starter"; "the biggest addition" —
  Athlon), Anugwom (Alabama — "unknown"), Anchondo (D2 Saginaw Valley). New OL coach
  Drevno (Kelly disciple). P6: "a full rebuild... though Kelly has never failed to
  produce an effective, productive run game." "They signed zero four-star linemen."

## DL
- GONE: the entire starting front — Hubbard (5th all-time NU sacks, 20.5) graduated;
  Saka -> Texas A&M ("their future first-rounder was poached" — P6); both DTs
  Bastone + Story.
- RETURN: Kilbane — "Braun likes the DEs" (PS); 7.5 TFL, 3.5 sk (Athlon); Flakes the
  veteran DT ("questions at defensive tackle around" him — Athlon).
- IN: J.Johnson (UCF), Kirks (Ohio State), Stephen-Wangboje (Sacred Heart FCS).
- PS: "they need some guys to develop on the interior to improve the rush D."

## LB
- GONE: Uihlein — 2nd-team All-B10; 100 tackles, 4 INT; 13 st. RETURN: Brus (81 tkl
  in his first starting year; "one of the most talented LBs" — PS).
- IN: McKinzie (Oklahoma — 4* transfer grade, 8 OU starts; "should start in
  Uihlein's spot" — P6). Glover (Ohio State '25 arrival; PS#56) — "high ceiling,
  but needs to emerge as depth is a question" (PS).

## DB
- "Northwestern should have one of the Big Ten's best secondaries" (Athlon):
  all-league S Robert Fitzgerald (#1 tackler), CB Fussell (16 career starts), nickel
  Turner (Star; #4 tackler). "Three stars back... above average on returning
  production alone" (P6). PS: Braun says "the best the back half has looked in
  years."
- Walters's health at field safety "is key" (Athlon). GONE: two starters (#5/#7
  tacklers — Wallace, F.Davis); Smith -> Duke, Tatum -> Miami (OH).
- IN: Deasfernandes (Central Michigan), Pate (Weber State).

## Specialists
