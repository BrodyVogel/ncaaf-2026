Faulkner - both GONE. An opposing ACC assistant (Athlon): "Those two hid a lot

two people." 2026 keeps TWO real strengths - RB (Justice Haynes Michigan + Hosley
697yds) and ST (K Aidan Birr, elite) - but replaces the QB (Mendoza, Indiana,
unproven), a gutted WR/TE corps (top-3 WRs all gone), a 3-new-starter OL, a
rebuilt DL off a weak base, and a secondary that lost 4 starters, all under a NEW
OC and a 4th-DC-in-4-years defense. A step back from 9-4 is the story.
