- OL: "Three new starters along with returnees Ethan Mackenny at LT and Malachi
  Carney, who moves to guard after starting at RT in 2025."
- DL: "New DC Jason Semore will emphasize aggressiveness/takeaways after the unit
  struggled to force turnovers (9) and get to the QB (13 sacks) in 2025. The line

- LB: "LBs Kyle Efford and E.J. Lightsey return as the two top tacklers from 2025,

  Duclona [USF] should step in at corner; returning safeties Tae Harris and Fenix
  Felton look to take their games to a new level."
- ST: "The Jackets return one of the best placekickers in the country in Aidan
  Birr, who had multiple clutch field goals in 2025. The punting situation is a
