# Georgia State — magazine findings (Athlon + Phil Steele, 2026 editions)

RULES: extract unit FACTS only. G5 = TWO-SOURCE (Athlon + Phil Steele); no Pick Six for the Sun Belt.

## THE SHAPE (2-source consensus)
Dell McGee Year 3 (no HC change; new co-DCs Cam Clark/Duke + John Haneline/Youngstown State). A 1-11
team - one of the worst in FBS - undergoing a near-total teardown. The one real asset is returning
dual-threat QB Cameran Brown (a good grade). The defense (448.8 ypg allowed) is rebuilt largely via
transfer; the secondary was raided (6+ DBs to the P4). A near-bottom Sun Belt East profile.

## Athlon (SBC p6) — depth chart + prose
- Header: NF 132, Sun Belt East 7. 1-11. New co-DCs Cam Clark (Duke) + John Haneline (Youngstown State FCS).
- OFF: QB Cameran Brown returns (1,296 pass/271 rush; the most effective '25 option when healthy - 241
  yds/4 TD vs South Alabama) + FCS-transfer competition Ayden Pereira (Merrimack, 1,818/853). Lost
  record-setting WR Ted Hurst (NFL); returns WR DJ Riles + ascendant TE Grant Hollier (21 catches/4 TD).
  Clemson transfer Keith Adams at a thin RB. OL allowed an SBC-low 18 sacks; Deandre Duffus + Tyhler
  Williams the top returners + Michigan import Connor Jones ("high marks").
- DEF (448.8 ypg, 58 TDs allowed): edge Jabriel Rhodes-Nelson (41 tkl, the most among returners) + S
  Deuce Walker (33 tkl, injury return). Michigan LB transfer Jaydon Hood ("much is expected"); FCS DBs
  Deuce Lee (Monmouth, top tackler) + Tre Mittman (75 tkl). Only 3 fumbles recovered + 3 INT + 18 sacks in '25.
- ST: SPEC p30; a transfer punter (Christian Teeter).
- Scout: "personnel-wise they were really good... man to man as good as anybody... Cameran Brown played well."
