## Program frame — FBS NEWCOMER
- 2025: 7-5 (5-3 Big Sky, FCS) in their last FCS year — lost the finale to UC
  Davis in a de-facto playoff-spot game. Program history: Troy Taylor era 23-1
  in Big Sky play ('19-22), four straight playoff trips through '23, then 3-9
  in '24; Brennan Marion's one year (2025) rebuilt via portal and went 7-5
  with his "Go-Go" offense (415 ypg, 34 ppg).
- THE TIMING DISASTER (both prints): the MAC/FBS invitation arrived AFTER
  signing day and AFTER the portal closed; the roster "was built on the idea
  it would be competing as an FCS Independent" (Athlon). New HC Alonzo Carter
  (FIRST season as an HC anywhere — ex-Arizona/San Jose St RB coach under
  Brent Brennan; JC head-coaching background at Contra Costa) added 13+
  post-spring JC players to face an FBS schedule. Marion left after one year
  — SECOND HC change in two years → coach_change=TRUE ×1.13.
- Staff: co-OCs Eric Kiesau (ex-Boise St OC) + Eric Scott; co-DCs Adam Clark
  (new 3-3-5 install; ex-NAU DC) + Kenwick Thompson; new STC Milo Austin.
  Everything is new: HC, both coordinators rooms, schemes on both sides.
- PS 2026 prose: "Sacramento St is coming off a 7-5 year but with all the
  change and the unexpected jump to the FBS, I will call this a rebuilding
  year." Athlon: "will likely struggle in its first year... questions along
  both lines, which is never good when a team is stepping up a level."
- Opposing MAC assistant (Athlon): "I think it's gonna be better that
  [Marion] isn't there anymore... traveling to the Midwest to play a November
  Tuesday game is gonna be different. It's gonna be hard."
