# Central Michigan — magazine notes (blinding v2: team-level ratings/projections/market numbers excluded)

Sources: Athlon MAC preview p.7 (header-verified "CENTRAL MICHIGAN CHIPPEWAS");
Phil Steele MAC pdf p.7 (300dpi spread, banner visually verified). No Pick Six.

## Program frame
- 2025: 7-6 (5-3 MAC; bowl loss to Northwestern 7-34 in the GameAbove Sports
  Bowl). Tied for first in the MAC at 5-2 with 6- and 3-point losses before
  falling at Toledo 3-21 in the finale; led eventual MAC champ WMU 21-14 in the
  4Q. All 5 MAC wins by double digits, +11 ypg MAC play.
- 2026 = Matt Drinkall yr 2 (7-6; career 49-23 — Kansas Wesleyan NAIA 42-17 his
  last 4, then Army OL coach '23-'24 incl. the 2024 Joe Moore Award line). First
  CMU coach to win his road debut since 1951. Inherited a McElwain program with
  "culture and talent."
- Staff continuity: OC Jim Chapin (ex-Eastern Washington), DC Sean Cronin (ex-
  Army DL coach) — both yr 2. STC Keith Murphy yr 7. No coach-change multiplier.
- SCHEME CHANGE (chosen, not forced): switching from Pro style to a POWER SPREAD
  built on QB run; a rare FB-using "21 personnel" team (PS). "They had hoped to
  install this offense last year but did not have the players; this year they do."
- PS defense frame: "loses 8 starters and 9 of their top 10 tacklers including
  their top 6... LB and secondary go from veteran strength to question mark.
  They have talent and hungry players and won't drop off too much."
- Athlon final analysis: "defense has a lot of questions at all three levels, but
  the offense has potential if Glasser can translate his spring performance."
- Opposing MAC assistant (Athlon): "Drinkall is doing a great job... built a
  culture coming from Army... very hard school to recruit to... you're gonna
  find a spark every once in a while."
- PS 2026 prose: "Drinkall's big year will be year 3 but CM could pull some
  surprises once again."

## Offense (Athlon: 4 returning starters — Lewis, Russell, Nehf, Koivisto)
- QB: TWO-QB PLAN BY DESIGN (both prints agree): Flores = the runner (team-high
  107 att, 527 yds, 8 TD; threw just 28 passes, 19 comp; missed 4 games hurt),
  Glasser = the pocket passer (6 gm over 2 yrs; started the final 3 of '24 for
  439 yds; OFY vs Pitt in '25; "featured plenty after a strong spring"). Labas
  (1,854 @ 71%, the actual 2025 passer) is gone. rFr Gomez (PS #184), Beamon
  (ran for 88 in '24). PS: "figure to take a step back in their passing
  numbers" — the offense is being rebuilt around QB run.
- RB: top two depart (Biggins 540 @ 5.1; Tulane transfer Cornist 470 @ 4.5).
  Vaughn Blue arrives from Liberty ("three up-and-down years"; 441 @ 5.4 + 162
  rec L2Y) as RB1; Townsend returns (418 @ 5.1, ~6 carries/gm, "will be a
  factor"); Holy Cross transfer Clerveaux (1,704 @ 4.7 over 2Y, 762 LY); true
  FR Trav Moore (PS #89, Athlon #1 recruit) "impressed in the spring." FB Rusty
  VanWetzinga (ex-Iowa, 5 st) is central to the 21-personnel identity.
- WR/TE: Lewis (3rd-Tm All-MAC, 596 @ 13.9, first Chip with B2B 100-yd games in
  10 yrs) is the ONLY returning starting wideout. Loses Davis (270 @ 13.5) and
  McIntosh (422 @ 13.6, 10 st — eligibility appeal pending, both prints treat
  as gone). Nasir Williams "strong spring"; Ruffin, Payne, Kruisenga depth.
  Adds: Fordham TE Jaden Allen (11 st, 327 @ 17.2, PS TE #153), ex-MichSt WR
  Foster (1 gm there), JC WR Robinson (1,713 JC '23-24; RS'd at Sacramento St
  LY), D2 TE Spillers. Athlon TE1 is FB/TE VanWetzinga — PS TE1 is Allen
  (divergence noted; 12-personnel usage in '25).
- OL: PS claim: "may have the BEST OLINE IN THE MAC" — continuity case, not
  2025-tape case. Returning starters: Russell (13 st at LT, moves to LG,
  "all-league candidate" — Athlon), Koivisto (13 st RT), Nehf (9 st RG then
  OFY), Iannuzzi (1st-Tm Ivy at Columbia '24; 1 st then OFY LY), Ploucha (5 st
  LY, 12 st '24), Booth (23 gm backup → LT1). Adds Georgetown pair Maka (12 st,
  2nd-Tm Patriot Lg) + Swan (23 st L2Y), E. Illinois OT DiSanto (30 st L3Y),
  JC John Williams. Drinkall/Joe-Moore OL pedigree is the argument.

## Defense (3 returning starters — Demma, Bird, Jackson)
- DL: loses its BEST player — DE Michael Heldman (#5 tackler, 10.5 sacks) — plus
  starting NT Decker and Hayden (→ E. Kentucky). Returns edge Korver Demma (13
  st, 27 tkl, 3.5 sk; JC '24) and DT Quavion Bird (took over the position
  mid-year). Staff "excited about" So. JJ Douglas — true-FR TE→DE convert, "looks
  like he will break out in Year 2" (23 tkl, 2 sk as a rookie). Adds Cornell
  pair: Reinbold (18 st, 74 tkl, 5 sk L2Y) + Van Fleet (10 st, 32 tkl LY), plus
  transfers Zirkle (50 tkl LY) and DT Jaxon Cherry.
- LB: "has to be completely rebuilt" (Athlon) / "looks weaker and is my biggest
  question" (PS). Loses Kwiatkowski (#1 tklr, 2nd-Tm MAC, 12.5 tfl, 3 int, 3x
  defensive POW) + Cochran (13 st, #2 tklr) + more — the unit's top returnees
  combined for 42 tackles. 2026: White (rotation; son of an NFL player,
  great-nephew of '79 Heisman winner Charles White), Earl (limited bench), TRUE
  FR Drew Esper projected to start (Athlon), "very talented true frosh" class.
- DB: CB Jaion Jackson = 3rd-year starter, "unquestioned leader of the back
  line." Everything else rebuilt: loses Carroll (→ TCU), Deasfernandes (→
  Northwestern), Spann, Gordon, Rikard, Blackwell (five 350+ snap DBs). CB2
  battle: true FR JaJuan Jones vs transfer Ty Williams. "Safeties will be
  inexperienced": Wyoming transfer Justin Taylor (1 st, 19 tkl there; also 2 gm
  Wisc '24) + journeyman Darrell Bryant Jr. (North Carolina '24: 8 st, 52 tkl →
  N. Colorado '25: 11 st, 75 tkl) + Campbell's Philord (33 tkl, 2 int as rFr).
  2025 pass D was PS PDR #48 — figures "to take a drop."
- 2025 ST/DEF ranks (retrospective): PS STR #122 in '25 (fell from #42).
