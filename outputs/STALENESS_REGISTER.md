## How to use this (the re-check loop)

1. When a bet list exists (Step 4), re-verify those teams FIRST regardless of L-flags.
2. In August, as camps name starters and injury news breaks, resolve the open battles
   below and any L-QB in the CSV. Source rules unchanged (R16): P4 = 3-source, G5 =
   2-source.
3. Any grade that actually changes goes through the repeat loop (HANDOFF §3): edit
   `snapshots/<T>/grades.json` → `grades_check` → `final_pass` → commit, and log the
   reason in `_meta.planned_vs_final_deviations`. Then regenerate this register.
4. Owner has final say on every grade — findings here are recommendations, not auto-edits.
