−1 away / 0 neutral. Verified on the actual schedules: UMass's home Ball State game is 42.1%

## The filter fix (your real catch)

My rule was "favored in ≥ ⌈line⌉ games." That wrongly failed **UMass over 2.5**: UMass is
favored in exactly 2 — Sacred Heart and Stonehill, the two FCS layups — and the over needs one
more win from *ten* tries (Ball State 48%, Akron 39%…). Our model puts that at **85%**. That's
not a partial-win gamble; the line (2.5) sits far below expected wins (~4.3) with a 2-game
locked floor. The correct metric is **"wins/losses needed from the wrong side of the favorite
line,"** and the threshold is ≤1: UMass needs 1 (fine), Colorado over 4.5 needed 3 (the bet you
correctly hated). Fixing it also restored Colorado State, Texas under, and Vanderbilt (all
"need 1").
