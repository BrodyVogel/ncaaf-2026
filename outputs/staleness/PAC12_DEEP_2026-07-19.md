## The two changes

**Utah State QB 32 L → 34 M.** The freeze grade had Hillstead as the likely starter in an
unresolved room ("L: unproven at USU, limited recent sample"). Deep dig: he was **named
the starter on 2026-04-20** (USU official) after a real spring competition; he has genuine
prior FBS starting production the "little recent tape" line undersold (**4 starts, 1,062
yds, 11 TD as a USU true freshman in 2023**, before a BYU detour); and Mendenhall calls QB
the team's **"strongest position."** The battle that drove the L is genuinely resolved and
he has starting tape → 34 (the proven-G5-starter tier the grade placed him just below) and
L→M. The 2-year recent-rep gap is why it's not higher. _This is the guardrail's allowed
case: the L lifts because the uncertainty actually resolved, not because I looked._

**Oregon State WRTE 26 L → 30 L.** The freeze grade called it "a bevy of young unproven
weapons… no returning FBS production" — but it **missed Aaron Butler**, a former 4-star
**Texas** WR transfer who is a **projected 2026 starter** (confirmed across the OSU roster,
247, On3, and portal reporting), and under-weighted returning 4-star Malachi Durant. With
Butler + Hampton (Alabama) + Durant, the room carries real Power-4 pedigree — a higher
ceiling than graded. Still **L**: no established FBS WR1, new HC/QB/scheme, transfer
production unproven at this level. Level up (26→30), confidence stays L.

## Deep vs triage — did it bear fruit?

Yes, in two distinct ways the triage could not:

1. **Coverage.** The high-leverage triage only looked at QB/DL/DB/RB, so it never examined
   Boise WRTE, Oregon State WRTE/OL, or Colorado State WRTE/OL. The deep pass examined all
   of them — and that's exactly where the Oregon State WRTE miss (Butler) was caught. The
   triage would never have found it.
2. **Depth on the overlap.** For units both passes saw, the deep dig went further: on Utah
   State QB the triage said "starter named, stays L"; the deep dig surfaced the prior
   1,062-yд/11-TD production + the coach's "strongest position" quote that justify L→M and
   the bump. It also let me **catch a stale source** (a "Week 1 depth chart" that was
   actually the 2025 chart, listing a graduated QB as the starter) — the kind of error an
   unverified triage can ingest.

Net for the Pac-12: **2 of 12 grades moved**, both small on the board, plus materially
higher confidence in the 10 that held (I now know *why* each is L, from current sources).
