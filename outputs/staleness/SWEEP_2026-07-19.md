# High-leverage L-unit sweep — 2026-07-19 (pre-camp)

Owner-directed pass over the **high-leverage** low-confidence units — QB, DL, DB, RB (the
four with the largest grade→points conversion weights; LB/WRTE/OL move the rating far
less). **96 units across 69 teams**, triaged by 11 parallel conference researchers against
each unit's graded assumption, then adjudicated centrally. Raw findings:
`sweep_2026-07-19_raw.json`.

**Guardrail (owner, verbatim intent):** research is NOT allowed to force higher
confidence. An L stays L unless the underlying uncertainty genuinely resolves. Digging
that turns up nothing — or that merely re-confirms what was already modeled — leaves the
grade and the L exactly as they were. That is the expected majority outcome pre-camp, not
a failure.

## Outcome: 1 grade changed of 96 units

**The one change — Duke DL 44 L → 42 L (factual error).** The grade counted Wesley
Williams (73.1) as a returner, but he was **drafted by Jacksonville in Round 4 of the 2026
NFL Draft** (Apr 25, 2026 — three months before the freeze). Removing a drafted
starter-quality edge from an already reserve-heavy front drops the level one tier;
O'Connor (75.9) remains the lone proven returner. Confidence stays L. Board effect: Duke
5.46 → 5.35 (−0.11), rank 50 held. This was the ONLY factual roster-accounting error found
across all 96 units — the other draftees the sweep surfaced (Duke's Rivers, San Diego
State's Chris Johnson, Wake Forest's Claiborne) were all already booked correctly as
losses.

## Everything else: no change (and why that's the right call)

**A. Portal reloads the grader already priced (~10 units).** Several researchers flagged
units the grade described by their losses ("gutted," "returns most of itself") and pointed
to a portal reload as a contradiction. On adjudication, the grade rationales **already
named those exact transfers** — often judging them more skeptically than the optimistic
previews did. No change, stay L:

- Hawai'i DL 16 — grader explicitly judged the restock (Elliott/FCS, Pollard/NAIA,
  Tomczyk/WVU) "sub-FBS"; a preview's "losses not enough to matter" is optimism, not new fact.
- San José State QB 20 — Weaver (ex-Hawai'i) is explicitly in the graded room (his 2-start
  sample is why it's 20, not lower).
- NC State DL 40 — Dyson (Tulane, 8 AAC sacks) is named in the rationale as "the one
  bright spot but AAC-level."
- Texas State DL 30, California DL 42, Colorado State QB 24, Bowling Green QB 34, Miami
  (OH) QB 30 — all name the flagged transfers (Bradford, Jericho Johnson, Hejny/Farrar,
  Novosad, Gotkowski's actual 2025 tape) already.
- Florida Atlantic DB 15 — the rationale explicitly models the "considerable overhaul"
  (JK Johnson, Foster, Reynolds) and Reid's unclear role; 15 is a deliberate
  "above-the-anchor-on-adds" grade. Reid's 2026 role stays a genuine open gap → stays L.

**B. Battle settled since freeze, but the starter is an unproven transfer (~13 units).**

## Takeaways

1. **The original grading was thorough.** Across 96 high-uncertainty units, the process had
   already incorporated the winter/spring portal moves; the sweep found exactly one stale
   fact (a draft departure). That's a strong validation of the freeze-time grades.
2. **The guardrail did real work.** A naive "research → upgrade" pass would have raised
   Hawai'i DL, SJSU QB, NC State DL, etc., toward optimistic previews. The evidence-first
   rule (and the grader's own skepticism) correctly held them.
3. **Net board impact: one team (Duke, −0.11).** Pre-camp, that is the honest result.
4. **August is where the yield is.** The ~35 still-open battles and the ~13 named-but-thin
   starters resolve in fall camp; that pass will move more than this one did.

## Depth & verification limits (read before trusting this for a wager)

This was a **triage** pass, not an exhaustive dig. Receipts: 11 subagents, all 69
teams / 96 units, **166 web searches + 174 page fetches (~3.5 web operations per unit)**;
transcripts on disk under the session's `subagents/`. That depth answers "has a starter
been named / did a transfer arrive / did a counted player leave" — it is NOT the
every-lead depth applied to the Gethers check (official roster + dated article +
cross-reference).

Verification asymmetry, on purpose: only the **Duke** finding was independently
re-verified (my own draft search) before its grade moved. The other 95 units rest on the
subagents' triage plus a read of the actual committed grade rationales. So an agent miss
would surface as a *missed change*, not a *wrong change* — grades only moved where
personally checked. Before betting a specific team, re-dig that team to Gethers depth.
