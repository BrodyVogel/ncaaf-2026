## What "uncorrelated" does and does not mean here

**Pairwise: genuinely uncorrelated.** No two bets share a team; conferences are spread across 11
leagues; the MC average pairwise correlation is 0.007. Individual games won't drag the book
together.

**Systematically: they share ONE thesis.** 14 of 15 are "compression" bets — a low-total dog's
OVER or a high-total favorite's UNDER — which are the *same* market-wide play (the market
over-disperses win totals). The portfolio's net loading on that factor is **−0.17**: in a
"chalk dominates / favorites blow out their totals" season, most of these lean the wrong way
*together*. This is irreducible with team-diversification because it **is** where the edge comes
from — I can't hedge it with other +EV bets, only with weaker counter-thesis ones. If you want
to damp it, swap the three lowest-EV legs (Southern Miss, Kent State, Missouri State) for
counter-loading edges like **East Carolina over 7.5**, **West Virginia under 5.5**, **FAU under
5.5** — that roughly halves the systematic tilt for ~4% less EV.

## Read this before betting

- **The EVs are model-implied and overstated.** A "+0.40 EV" is what the model believes if its
  probabilities are exactly right; they aren't. Trust the **rank order and the sign** far more
  than the magnitude. Realized edge after model error and closing-line movement is a fraction of
  the printed number.
- **Sizing: small and flat, or heavily-fractional Kelly (≈1/8).** Full-Kelly on these printed
  edges would tell you to bet the house — do not. The model uncertainty is larger than the
  within-model variance.
- **Liquidity is the catch.** The biggest edges are on G5 totals (UMass, Kent State, Missouri
  State, New Mexico, Southern Miss, Nevada) precisely *because* those markets are soft and
  low-limit — you can't get much down. The liquid legs (Miami, Texas, Indiana, Colorado, UConn)
  carry smaller but more trustworthy edges.
- **Flagged model-risk legs (⚠):** Navy and New Mexico (service-academy / heavy-hype G5 where our
  grade most disagrees with the market) and UMass (juice-capped). Stake these smallest.
- **Conference totals were excluded** from this book — thin, stale-prone, and strongly correlated
  within a league. A few show huge nominal edges; treat them as speculative, not core.
- **The H2H props didn't make the cut**; the best (Tennessee −0.5) is +0.03 calibrated EV, far
  below the ~+0.32 floor of this list.
