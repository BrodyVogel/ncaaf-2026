stretching our ratings to the market's *own* dispersion (the "market-matched" set). Edge that
survives that is, by construction, a team-specific disagreement — not the market-over-disperses
tilt. I required a real market-matched edge (≥8%) and near-zero dispersion-factor loading. The
book's net loading is **+0.008 (was −0.17 on the original list)** — it is no longer that edge at

**Filter 2 — no partial wins; the schedule has to carry it.** For every over, the team must be
**favored in at least as many games as the line requires** (chalk clears it); for every under,
favored in **few enough** that it's projected to actually lose the games, not drop coin-flips.
This throws out "over 4.5 while favored in 2" outright.
