## 2. Conference-wide news
- [QUOTE][2026-07-15] Commissioner Gloria Nevárez said the MW is pausing membership expansion: "We're going to pause right now as far as additional membership," and referenced 10-to-12 members as historical precedent but the league is "not actively looking right now." (Source: ESPN, https://www.espn.com/college-football/story/_/id/45758544/mountain-west-conference-pauses-expansion-amid-media-rights-negotiation; InForum, https://www.inforum.com/sports/bison-media-zone/mens-sports/mcfeely-mountain-west-commish-again-mentions-possibility-of-12-members, 2026-07-15)
- [QUOTE][2026-07-15] Nevárez on membership standards: "Membership standards are 10 to 12 categories across the league of areas that directly impact competitive performance and creating a baseline so that our schools are constantly moving forward." (Source: InForum, 2026-07-15)
- [QUOTE][2026-07-15] Nevárez unveiled a new MW football title trophy; league theme is "Built Bold." Reported record revenue distribution verbatim per Mountain West Connection: "Record revenue distribution of $118 million to member schools in 2025-26, with $35 million from postseason football and the CFP." Streaming partner named as KISWE, with MW+ returning "60 percent of every subscription dollar" to the enrolling school; six-year media package with CBS, Fox and The CW begins this season. (Source: Mountain West Connection, "Built Bold," https://www.mwcconnection.com/mountain-west-news/89114/built-bold-nevarez-delivers-a-mountain-west-state-of-the-union-in-las-vegas, 2026-07-15)
- [QUOTE][2026-07-15] Nevárez reaffirmed UNLV's grant-of-rights obligations amid speculation UNLV wants out: "There have been questions about whether the grant of rights is invalid or doesn't hold, and what I want to reiterate is, we will be meeting those obligations." (Source: InForum, https://www.inforum.com/sports/bison-media-zone/mens-sports/mcfeely-mountain-west-commissioner-takes-a-victory-lap, 2026-07-15)
- [REPORTED][2026] NDSU joined as football-only member effective July 1, 2026. Per Sportico, the entry deal is a "$12.5 million entry fee in six installments, with an initial payment of $7 million due upon admission, followed by five annual payments of $1.1 million," plus a $5M NCAA reclassification fee; NDSU is dues-exempt (~$220K/yr) through 2031-32 and ineligible for bowls/CFP until 2028-29. NDSU will play a 12-game 2026 schedule (8 MW games, 3 non-conference FBS, 1 FCS). Other sports remain in the Summit League. (Source: ESPN, https://www.espn.com/college-football/story/_/id/47870245/sources-north-dakota-state-joins-mountain-west-football; Sportico, https://www.sportico.com/leagues/college-sports/2026/north-dakota-state-mountain-west-agreement-spending-1234884353/; NDSU, https://www.ndsu.edu/mountain-west)
- [REPORTED][2026] Northern Illinois joined as football-only member July 1, 2026 (other sports remain in the MAC); UTEP joined as a full member from Conference USA after 21 years in C-USA. (Source: CBS Sports, https://www.cbssports.com/college-football/news/north-dakota-state-mountain-west-fcs/; KFOX)
- [REPORTED][2026] MW plays an 8-game conference schedule in 2026. (Source: CBS Sports; NDSU)
- [REPORTED][2026-07-15] MW headquarters relocating from Colorado Springs (its home since 1999) to Las Vegas in July 2026. (Source: KOAA, https://www.koaa.com/sports/mountain-west-to-leave-longtime-conference-home-in-colorado-springs-for-las-vegas)
