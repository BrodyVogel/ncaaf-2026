## 2. Conference-wide news
- [TEAM][REPORTED][2026-07-15] Commissioner Jim Phillips unveiled a new football championship-game tiebreaker. Head-to-head remains the No. 1 tiebreaker; a new third tier uses a SportSource Analytics "Team Success Ranking" (also used by the CFP) to identify the strongest "body of work." Guiding principles: head-to-head first; no team overly rewarded/penalized for the number of conference games it plays; body of work decides otherwise. Effective 2026. (Source: ESPN, "ACC implements new tiebreaker policy for football title game," 2026-07-15, https://www.espn.com/college-football/story/_/id/49366844/acc-implements-new-tiebreaker-policy-football-title-game)
- [TEAM][QUOTE][2026-07-15] Phillips: "We talked a lot about it, used a lot of consultants, did 10,000 algorithms of different scenarios." (Source: ESPN, 2026-07-15, same URL)
- [TEAM][REPORTED][2026-07-15] Change was prompted by 2025, when a five-loss Duke reached the title game over No. 10 Miami (Miami still made the expanded CFP and reached the national title game). (Source: AP via ABC News, "ACC revamps tiebreakers for conference title game after 5-loss Duke team got in over No. 10 Miami," 2026-07-15, https://www-cdn.abcnews.com/Sports/wireStory/acc-revamps-tiebreakers-conference-title-game-after-5-134784928)
- [TEAM][REPORTED][2026-07-15] 2026 is a "transition year": 12 of 17 teams play nine ACC games; five play eight — Boston College, Clemson, Florida State, Georgia Tech, North Carolina — because of existing Power Four nonconference contracts. Each program must play a minimum of 10 Power Four opponents. Beginning 2027, 16 teams play nine ACC games and one plays eight annually (the eight-game team scheduling two nonconference Power Four opponents). (Source: ESPN, "ACC shares 9-game conference schedule in 'transition year,'" https://africa.espn.com/college-football/story/_/id/47324406/acc-shares-9-game-conference-schedule-transition-year; theACC.com, 2025-12-16, https://theacc.com/news/2025/12/16/football-acc-announces-2026-league-opponents-as-move-to-nine-game-conference-schedule-begins.aspx)
- [TEAM][REPORTED][2026-01-26] 2026 ACC Championship Game moves to noon ET on Saturday, December 5 at Bank of America Stadium (first noon title game since 2008). (Source: theACC.com, "ACC Unveils 2026 Football Schedule," 2026-01-26, https://theacc.com/news/2026/1/26/acc-unveils-2026-football-schedule.aspx)
