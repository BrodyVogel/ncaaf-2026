## 0. Top signals

- FIU — Willie Simmons publicly anchored the team to QB JJ Kohl (“led by JJ Kohl, our quarterback”); QB1 confirmed (QB).

- LIBERTY — Chadwell kept the three-way QB race (Purdie/Henderson/Vasko) open; “It will be a big battle there.” Vasko confirmed returning from shoulder surgery (QB).
- LIBERTY — Chadwell says he is healthiest since 2023 after resolving a 2024–25 undiagnosed condition (TEAM).
- MISSOURI STATE — Casey Woods still framed Locklear vs. Belin as an open competition entering camp (QB).
- DELAWARE — Ryan Carty treats Minicucci as the established QB1 but wants growth (“Can I get better at processing?”); Blue Hens fully bowl/championship eligible in 2026 (QB/TEAM).
- KENNESAW STATE — Jerry Mack declined to name an RB1, framing the backfield as a committee replacing Cam Cook (RB).
- WKU — Tisdale vs. FSU transfer Brock Glenn QB battle not publicly resolved; Bodie Reeder is the new pass-first OC (QB).
- CONFERENCE — Bowl Confidence Index again replaces the preseason poll; Jax State, Liberty, WKU unanimous (100%); Kennesaw St 85%, Delaware 75% (TEAM).
- CONFERENCE — Preseason POYs: Minicucci (Off.), Hopson (Def.), Rippa (ST); MacLeod says league is happy at 10, expansion “not a targeted” priority (TEAM).

## 2. Conference-wide news

- [TEAM][QUOTE][2026-07-21] Commissioner Judy MacLeod on membership: “People ask are you going to stay at 10. We are always going to explore, but we are going to do it in a very strategic way. We are very happy with the 10.”  On Texas expansion specifically: “It’s definitely something we will look at, but not a targeted expansion.”  (Source: A Sea of Red, <https://www.aseaofred.com/three-big-takeaways-from-judy-macleods-speech-at-cusa-media-day-2026/>, 2026-07-21)
- [TEAM][QUOTE][2026-07-21] MacLeod led her address on the Protect College Sports Act (Senate bill covering NIL, one-time transfer, five-year eligibility, limited antitrust exemption): “Conference USA, early, supported the Protect College Sports Act. We realize it is not perfect… It’s desperately needed. We’ve got to have something. It’s not working (currently) and it’s not sustainable.”  (Source: A Sea of Red, 2026-07-21)
- [TEAM][REPORTED][2026-07-21] MacLeod noted six new head coaches in the league (including the two coaches new to CUSA via Delaware and Missouri State). Casey Woods (Missouri State) is the only brand-new head coach in 2026.  (Source: A Sea of Red, 2026-07-21; Diehard Sports Network, <https://www.diehardsportsnetwork.com/g6/conference-usa-football-media-days-2026>)
- [TEAM][REPORTED] Membership for 2026 is 10: Delaware, FIU, Jacksonville State, Kennesaw State, Liberty, Middle Tennessee, Missouri State, New Mexico State, Sam Houston, Western Kentucky. UTEP left for the Mountain West and Louisiana Tech left for the Sun Belt. Louisiana Tech agreed to pay a CUSA-record exit fee “of over $8 million” per Front Office Sports’ David Rumsey, with the move formally announced/effective July 1, 2026 (Louisiana Radio Network). UTEP’s buyout was reported at ~$8 million by The Athletic (per HERO Sports), though CBS Sports separately reported a figure near $2 million at announcement — sources conflict on the exact UTEP amount. (Sources: Diehard Sports Network; Front Office Sports; Louisiana Radio Network; The Athletic via HERO Sports; CBS Sports)
- [TEAM][REPORTED] Delaware and Missouri State transition eligibility: Both have completed their two-year FCS-to-FBS transition and are eligible for the CUSA Championship Game and bowl season in 2026. (Delaware began its transition in 2024, was ineligible for the FCS playoffs, and is now fully eligible.) (Source: Athlon Sports, <https://athlonsports.com/college-football/delaware-fbs-debut-why-blue-hens-success-might-be-sustainable>, 2026-02-25)
- [TEAM][REPORTED] Media rights: CUSA is in year three of its ESPN/CBS Sports package; about half of games on linear, half on ESPN+. (Source: Diehard Sports Network; A Sea of Red)
- [TEAM][REPORTED] Season/championship dates conflict across sources: Bowling Green Daily News (2026-07-17) reports CUSA opens Week 0 on Aug. 29 (Jax State at North Dakota State; NM State vs. Florida State) with the 22nd CUSA Championship Game on Dec. 4. Diehard Sports Network reports a Week 0 opener of WKU hosting Sam Houston on Aug. 23 and a title game Dec. 5. Both presented; not reconciled.
