# AAC 2026 media days — source digest

Compiled: 2026-07-27 · Event: American Conference Football Kickoff & Media Days, July 23–24, 2026, Hilton St. Petersburg Bayfront (333 1st St. SE), St. Petersburg, FL — first time the event has been held in St. Petersburg (coaches’ press conferences Friday, July 24; ESPN+ live coverage) · Baseline: 2026-07-12 snapshot

> Note on branding: The conference rebranded from “American Athletic Conference” to “American Conference” (slogan “Built To Rise,” mascot “Soar the Eagle”) effective July 21, 2025; commissioner Tim Pernetti: “A brand is more than a name… We’re not slick, we’re strong.” Used as “American Conference” on first reference, “the American” thereafter, to reduce confusion with the ACC. (Source: FOX Sports/AP, 2025-07-21)

## 1. Media poll & preseason honors

- **No official conference poll.** The American discontinued its preseason media poll beginning in 2025; Pernetti called it an “industry-wide trend” and noted “the American marks just the second of 12 conferences to step away from an annual preseason poll of this sort.” He added the poll was dropped because “it’s more difficult to actually put your finger on how this should look, given that rosters look dramatically different every year.” (Source: The Roost, attheroost.com, 2025-07-25) [QUOTE]
- **Unofficial media poll (TheRoost.com):** Navy picked as the favorite to win the American in 2026 — “Navy is the Favorite in 2026 American Conference Preseason Media Poll.” (Source: The Roost, attheroost.com/2026/07/23/navy-2026-american-conference-media-poll, 2026-07-23; corroborated WCTI-12, 2026-07-24) [AGGREGATED] Full order and vote totals were not retrievable from available (partly paywalled) snapshots — see coverage gaps.
- **Preseason AP Top 25 (national):** No American team ranked; four received votes — Tulane (23), Memphis (9), Navy (5), Army (2).  (Source: Underdog Dynasty, underdogdynasty.com) [AGGREGATED]
- **Magazine picks:** Sporting News, Athlon, Lindy’s, and Phil Steele all picked Navy first. Sporting News: Navy, Memphis, UTSA, Tulane, ECU. Athlon: Navy, Memphis, UTSA, Army, ECU. Lindy’s: Navy, Tulane, USF, Army, Memphis. Phil Steele: Navy, UTSA, Tulane, ECU, Army. (Source: Navy Athletics, navysports.com, 2026-07-21)  [AGGREGATED]
- **All-conference (media/magazine, not league-official):** Athlon Sports’ Steven Lassan published a 2026 American All-Conference Team “led by 13 selections from Navy, with UTSA (12), Memphis (12) and Temple (12).” First-team notables: QB Cale Hellums (Army), OL Malcolm Johnson (Navy), K Nico Gramatica (USF), P Caile Hogan (UTSA), KR Jaylin Lucas (Tulane), PR Easton Messer (FAU). (Source: Athlon Sports, athlonsports.com) [AGGREGATED] The league itself did not issue an official preseason all-conference team.
